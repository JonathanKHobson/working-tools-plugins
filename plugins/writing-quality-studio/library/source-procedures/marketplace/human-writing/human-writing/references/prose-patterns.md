# Prose patterns

New first-party support guide, authored for Writing Quality Studio on 2026-10-10. This implements the recovered Human Writing method's prose-audit requirements. It is a current repair, not a recovered historical reference. Copyright 2026 Jonathan Kyle Hobson. CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Set the editing contract

Read the piece's job, reader, format and requested change before marking patterns. Record protected facts, names, numbers, quotations, citations, uncertainty, promises and the author's position. For a supplied sample, note its usual cadence, vocabulary, formality, punctuation and paragraph shape. A sample governs style; the fact inventory governs meaning.

Use this guide to find reader friction. A familiar phrase, passive sentence, three-item list, fragment or formal register can be appropriate. Neither a cluster nor a single pattern identifies authorship. Do not promise detector outcomes or insert mistakes, slang, random variation or imagined personal experience.

## Inspect patterns in context

For each candidate, ask what the sentence contributes, what it costs the reader, and whether a smaller change preserves its job. Work through the following checks only where they apply.

| Pattern to inspect | Repair when it impedes the piece | Keep when it serves the piece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abstract praise or importance without a referent | Replace with the supported action or function; remove the unsupported benefit | A general claim accurately states the source's limited scope |
| Stock introductions and recap endings | Begin with the actual point; close with the necessary action or consequence | Orientation or a recap helps the intended reader or required format |
| Repeated rhetorical frames | Rebuild around the paragraph's particular job | A refrain, comparison or required recurring structure carries meaning |
| Uniform sentence or paragraph shape | Combine or split around the ideas and their emphasis | Comparable fields or controlled instructions benefit from consistency |
| Synonym cycling or inflated diction | Use the precise established term and a direct verb | Domain vocabulary, deliberate formality or the author's sample requires it |
| Hidden actor, action or condition | Restore only the agency and relationships established by the source | Passive voice accurately preserves unknown agency, privacy or focus |
| Decorative specificity | Remove invented detail or identify an explicitly permitted hypothetical | A supplied detail demonstrates the point or expresses the author's experience |
| Over-explained interpretation | Let evidence or a deliberate image do its work; keep needed qualification | The audience needs the explanation to follow a technical or unfamiliar point |
| Chatbot residue and ornamental formatting | Remove process commentary, empty enthusiasm and unnecessary emphasis | Headings, lists or emphasis support scanning and the requested format |
| Forced casualness | Restore the audience-appropriate register | Authentic contractions, dialect, fragments or humor fit the author's voice |

Specificity is constrained by evidence. An unsupported statement about productivity cannot become a time saving, personal anecdote or numerical result merely to sound concrete. Preserve the unknown, state a verified function, remove the unsupported claim, or flag the missing information.

Courtesy carries relationships. Changing a request to a command, a possibility to a promise, or a personal preference to a general ranking changes meaning. Preserve those distinctions while editing rhythm.

## Revise in working order

1. Read once for the piece's purpose and content inventory. Mark the strongest useful details and deliberate voice choices to protect.
2. Identify the few patterns that most obstruct the reader. For review mode, record their exact spans and reader cost without rewriting the whole piece.
3. Give each paragraph a clear job. Reorder, merge or split paragraphs when their sequence hides the point; preserve causal, chronological and conditional relationships.
4. Rebuild sentences around that meaning. Choose diction and sentence length for emphasis, not to achieve a numerical variety target.
5. Read adjacent paragraphs aloud or quietly for cadence, continuity and register. Retain purposeful roughness and repetition.
6. Compare the revised text with the inventory. Check changed nouns, numbers, dates, status verbs, modals, attribution, ranking, sequence, scope and certainty.
7. Inspect the exact final text and destination conventions after the last change. Preserve code, data, frontmatter, link targets and protected quotations in a prose-only file edit.

Stop when the text works for its reader and preserves meaning. A successful review can retain an already effective passage.

## Synthetic example and fidelity check

**Supplied fictional facts:** A team has a saved draft. Two reviewers have not replied. The writer asks for comments by Friday and will decide the release date after review. No outcome or speed improvement has been measured.

**Draft:** “We are thrilled to announce this transformative milestone. Our draft is ready, unlocking a faster review process. The team has truly come together. Please send your feedback by Friday. We will release on Monday.”

**Revision:** “The draft is saved, and we're waiting on two reviewers. Please send your comments by Friday. I'll decide the release date after the review.”

The revision keeps the draft's saved state, the outstanding replies, Friday's request and the decision after review. It removes the unsupported speed claim and Monday promise. It does not assert that the reviewers approved, that the draft was published, or that review will end on Friday.

**Retain:** “Could you send your comments by Friday?” is a legitimate request. Do not turn it into “Send comments Friday” solely to remove courtesy. A repeated heading in a required weekly update can also remain.

## Return and limits

Return finished prose first for a rewrite. For review, give a small number of precise findings with the smallest useful repairs. If a material fact is missing, make the gap visible. This guide is an editorial method, not evidence of measured readability, engagement, authenticity or authorship.
