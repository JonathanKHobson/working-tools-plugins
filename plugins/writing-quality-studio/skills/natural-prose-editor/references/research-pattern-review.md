# Research-informed pattern review

Use for a substantive anti-slop/Humanizer review when sentence grammar hides meaning, requested variants differ only cosmetically, or a supplied series repeats one shape. It is a conditional extension of the existing editor, not an extra pass on every message. A pattern is a reason to inspect reader cost; it is not evidence of AI authorship. No classifier, randomness target or new linter is added.

## Inspect grammar beyond fashionable words

Read the actor, action, affected object, condition and degree of certainty. Chains of abstract nouns, nominalizations, agentless passives or repeated subordinate clauses can hide who did what and who must act. Revise when that omission impedes the reader's task. Restore an actor only when the source establishes it; otherwise retain the unknown or ask when it matters. Inanimate subjects can be accurate: a sensor measures, a server retries, a policy requires.

**Friction:** “Implementation of the reassignment resulted in the initiation of a review.” If the source says a coordinator reassigned the file and requested review, write “The coordinator reassigned the file and requested a review.” If those actors/actions are unknown, do not manufacture them to obtain active voice; flag the missing responsibility only if needed.

**Keep:** “Samples were stored at −80 °C” when storage is the point and the actor is irrelevant; “Tokenization precedes indexing” when these are terms of art. Passive voice can preserve accurate unknown agency, privacy, topical continuity, tact or disciplinary convention. Nominalization is not automatically verbosity. Never turn “was reviewed” into “was approved,” or a condition into a demonstrated cause, just to sharpen the sentence.

[Reinhart et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422455122) examined 66 grammatical/rhetorical features in specific GPT-4o/Llama 3 variants and English registers. It motivates looking beyond word lists; it does not prove these constructions are wrong, establish a preferred frequency, or validate this editing rule. The operational test above is an editorial adaptation.

## Compare pieces when the set is in scope

If reviewing a series, campaign, batch or requested variants, inspect opening moves, argument order, evidence placement, cadence, formatting and endings across the supplied pieces. Several individually clear drafts can still reproduce one template. Name the shared frame, then vary a suitable piece's structure where it better serves that piece's job. For a single draft, do not retrieve an unrelated corpus to run this check.

**Friction:** Four posts each ask a teaser question, give three abstract bullets, then repeat the teaser as a takeaway. A factual update might instead lead with the change; an explanation might follow a concrete example; a decision request can lead with the ask. Synonym swaps or random punctuation do not create meaningful variety.

**Keep:** A required weekly status format, a useful recurring newsletter section, consistent safety steps or comparable pricing fields. Reader orientation and brand continuity can justify repetition. Use the existing [template selection method](../../voice-and-style/references/writing-templates.md) only when choosing new mechanics is useful. Evaluate each draft's usefulness and the set's range separately; don't sacrifice one for the other.

[Doshi and Hauser (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290) found increased similarity across AI-assisted short stories despite benefits on some individual ratings in their experiment. This supports a separate collection-level question; it does not establish the result for marketing, this model, or Kyle's writing.

## Preserve what a blunt rule would erase

Before shortening a hedge or request, check its relationship and uncertainty. “Could we move the deadline to Friday?” can preserve an actual request; “Move the deadline” claims a different authority. Keep meaningful courtesy, quiet grief and formal restraint. [Care-sensitive communication](../../communication-crafter/references/spoken-and-care-messages.md) already owns apologies and condolences; [voice evidence](writing-style.md) owns personal matching. Do not add a joke or enthusiastic close merely to sound human.

Before deleting a final image, repeated phrase or fragment, trace whether the author established it earlier and whether its return advances the piece. Preserve a useful refrain, anaphora or callback; repetition alone is neither a reason to delete nor an automatic reason to retain it. An unrelated motivational slogan can go. A speech's repeated “We remember” or a story's returning porch-light image may be its intended structure. The current brief and authorial intent govern the inherited No AI Slop kicker/fragment rules.

Concrete repairs still need evidence. If “boosts productivity” lacks support, do not turn it into “cuts review time from 30 to 8 minutes” or even assume that reviews became faster. State the verified function if known, remove the claim, retain honest generality when informative, or mark the missing evidence. A hypothetical may explain a mechanism only when the task permits it and is clearly introduced as imagined; it never proves a result or supplies testimony. The existing [mutation audit](controlled-contrast-revision.md) governs meaning and relationship checks.

## Output and success check

For detect-only requests, quote the actual span or identify the repeated cross-piece frame, explain its reader cost and suggest the smallest fix without rewriting or guessing authorship. For edits, return the requested prose, preserving strong passages; no change may be the correct result. Check that any revision preserves facts, exact spans, agency/unknowns, certainty, audience relationship and purposeful repetition. Do not make every piece sound equally conversational or polished.
