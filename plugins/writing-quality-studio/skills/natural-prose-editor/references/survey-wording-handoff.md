# Survey wording handoff

When the requested text is a survey question, response option, scale label, screener, consent/introduction, or invitation tied to an instrument, resolve the separate `survey-studio:survey-strategist` owner. It defines the decision, construct, target population, time window, question type, response coding, branch logic, burden, accessibility, privacy, and analysis use. Research Methodology can frame method choice and interpretation before that handoff.

Polish respondent-facing wording only after identifying those constraints and the canonical instrument/version. Improve clarity, neutral phrasing, reading effort, and consistent terms while preserving the measured construct, answer coverage, scale anchors, skip logic, validated-measure wording, and IDs/codes. Flag a method problem rather than silently fixing it as prose. Return proposed edits as a diff or item-ID map to the instrument owner for QA and export from its canonical source. A polished sentence alone is not a field-ready survey or a validated measure.

For invitation or reminder copy, use `communication-crafter` for audience and action, then keep sampling, consent, distribution, and fielding status with Survey Studio. Drafting does not imply an invitation was sent or a survey published.
