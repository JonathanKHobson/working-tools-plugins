# Voice And Style

Personal voice is opt-in for the current task. A routine email, professional edit, assistant conversation, or plugin mention alone does not activate historical voice matching or private-profile retrieval. Preserve supplied wording and the current communication goal without looking up a personal corpus. For an explicit personal-voice request or an authorized profile update, read [professional voice handoff](references/professional-voice-handoff.md) first. Its activation boundary overrides the broad substantive-writing trigger in the preserved procedure below.

For an explicitly selected generic template or requested blend, read [the template handoff](references/writing-templates.md) and use `writing-templates:writing-templates` if installed. Preserve the recipient author’s audience-appropriate correspondence voice and article voice while applying selected mechanics. Ordinary personal or brand voice work does not load the generic library.

For personal/brand matching or a tone change, identify the requested mode and use the complete [sample, brand and tone method](references/sample-brand-and-tone.md). Follow the activation and source-custody handoff above; use only the relevant support and verify the final text against its facts and voice brief.

- For an explicit request to match the user's personal writing, use the complete [Write Like Me provider method](../../library/source-procedures/provider-methods.md#write-like-me) if installed after the handoff above. Prefer designated samples or the authorized private profile; otherwise search only relevant connected sources. Ordinary retrieval keeps up to three title-and-link references internal and saves no samples. For user-supplied local archives and explicitly requested private retention, use the handoff's bounded exceptions to connector-only retrieval, source-link format and saving. These exceptions grant no wider retrieval, sharing or execution authority. Current facts, audience, format and delivery remain authoritative.
- For an authentic sample match, use [sample matching](references/sample-brand-and-tone.md#sample-matching) and compare observed syntax and stance rather than an adjective-only profile.
- For organizational voice, use [brand voice](references/sample-brand-and-tone.md#brand-voice), the existing approved guide and the task's factual constraints.
- For a register change, use [tone revision](references/sample-brand-and-tone.md#tone-revision) while preserving claims, conditions, commitments and the actual relationship.

Use only the sources relevant to the task. Current user instructions, factual evidence and host tools govern any historical source approval language, affiliate promotion, unsupported detector claims or account assumptions. Keep personal samples and saved profiles outside the plugin; a style preference does not authorize fabrication. Where two bodies overlap, use one method and retain the other's unique detail. For ordinary sentence polishing use `natural-prose-editor`; broader audience/relationship communication uses `communication-crafter`; formal research and job applications route to Research and Career. An occasional UX research follow-up remains part of communication, not a new specialist head.

For multiple distinct directions on the same facts, or a reusable task voice, use [same-fact voice selection](references/same-fact-voice-selection.md) and its complete relevant cards/examples. Keep the user’s selected voice and requested variant count; do not retrieve personal samples for a fictional example or treat task voices as a personal profile.

For an article or long post explicitly edited from the author's transcript by preserving their literal wording, read and apply the complete [extractive transcript method](references/extractive-transcript-writing.md). Its source-span and editorial-addition checks govern that mode; ordinary voice matching stays in the methods above.

For a persistent professional/social voice brief, read [professional voice handoff](references/professional-voice-handoff.md). Reuse authentic samples and the existing private profile; Social Content owns LinkedIn hook forms, Career owns evidence/positioning, and natural-prose-editor owns local text diagnostics.

For an explicitly requested interactive template choice, use the installed `browse_writing_voice_library` tool or the [Writing Library bridge](../writing-voice-library/SKILL.md). If unavailable, use the [public library](https://activity-atlas.jkylehobson.chatgpt.site/writing-library/) for examples and portable downloads. Personal-source matching remains a separate, private route.
