The registration call to action is a literal placeholder; it is not a link. Each version changes stance, sequence, syntax, rhythm and relationship with the reader. None claims a participant outcome or promises a seat. Publication is held until registration and access details are confirmed.

## Mutation audit

Codex checked each passage against F1–F11. The six versions all retain both dates, exact time and zone, total capacity versus unknown availability, access uncertainty, recording uncertainty, and the absence of outcomes. V4's opening is a reflective proposition, not a tested result; its second paragraph restores the full logistics. V5's “Plan for” is preparatory wording, not a promise of enrollment. No participant quotes, invented organizer, endorsement, platform, fee, location, outcomes or registration URL were added. The passages remain prototypes; this is a writer's fact audit, not a human readability test.

Lead review removed the unsupported phrase “new offering” from V1/V5: absence of an evaluation does not establish that the offering is new. V6 was added to test a more pronounced register and rhythm shift. Root checked all six against the fixed brief; human interpretation remains untested.

Integration correction, 2026-09-30: V6’s imperative “Register by” was changed to the factual deadline statement because the CTA is not live. This preserves the supplied deadline without implying an actionable registration route.
