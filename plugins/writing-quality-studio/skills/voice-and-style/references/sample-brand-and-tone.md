# Sample, brand and tone work

This is Writing Quality Studio's own procedure for a requested voice task. It uses the Studio's source-custody, meaning-preservation and continuity methods. It is not a reproduction or execution of a marketplace procedure.

## Sample matching

1. Establish the artifact, reader, purpose and scope of matching. Prefer the exact samples supplied for this task. Use the [professional voice handoff](professional-voice-handoff.md) before any personal-source retrieval or retention. An ordinary edit does not call for a private corpus search.
2. Check who wrote the sample and whether it fits the genre. Keep coauthored, dictated, AI-assisted or unattributed text labeled; do not treat generated examples as the author's original writing. A short sample supports a provisional local match, not a universal profile.
3. Make observations with actual excerpts or locations: how the author addresses the reader, uses first-person claims, arranges explanations, qualifies certainty, varies sentence length, introduces examples and uses courtesy or humor. Do not infer personality, nationality or private history from phrasing.
4. Turn only useful observations into a brief for this artifact. Include a positive example, a counterexample and the situations where a trait should change. A candid disagreement letter and a welcoming invitation may share voice while needing different intensity.
5. Draft from the verified factual material. Keep sample events and biography out of the new text unless those facts belong to the current task. Apply recognizable mechanics proportionally; do not copy an authentic sample's unrelated sentence or reproduce accidental errors.
6. Compare the draft with both the brief and the source facts. Check claims, negation, conditions, numbers, quotations and commitments before judging cadence. Keep deliberate roughness where it serves the author. Use [voice continuity](voice-continuity.md) for long work and [same-fact selection](same-fact-voice-selection.md) only when distinct variants are requested.
7. Return the requested artifact. Explain a consequential uncertainty or departure only when it changes confidence in the match. Saving a profile requires authorization and the existing private project; never put samples in the distributable plugin.

## Brand voice

Start with the organization's approved guide, authentic examples and this artifact's reader action. If those are unavailable, work from the explicit brief and label new choices as a proposed task voice. Do not retrieve a private personal profile merely because the brand belongs to the author.

Translate abstract traits into observable choices. For example, a request for clear and considerate language can become direct action labels, stated conditions, and a recovery sentence that explains the next useful step. Keep promised outcomes, required disclosures, terminology and accessible wording stable. A playful marketing line is not automatically appropriate for a failed payment.

Draft against one useful model paragraph or sentence, then check the difficult state as well as the welcome state. Compare tone across success, uncertainty and error without turning every context into the same emotional register. Keep the incumbent guide and organization identity authoritative; propose a guide change separately if it is actually requested.

## Tone revision

Read the original for meaning before choosing a register. Record the relationship, requested change and boundaries: warmer, more direct, more formal, more conversational or more concise. Keep obligation, certainty, deadlines, scope and consent unchanged. Remove or add courtesy for a reader reason, never merely to meet a pattern count.

Revise diction, sentence structure and degree of explanation. Compare the final text to the original proposition by proposition. If the change makes a request sound like a command, a tentative plan sound agreed, or a disagreement sound like an endorsement, restore the distinction. Return one usable version unless alternatives were requested.

## Worked check

Facts: the review is unfinished; the author expects to share a draft Friday, subject to receiving source data. A warmer register can thank the reader and explain the dependency. It cannot say the final report is confirmed for Friday. If an authentic sample contains a story about a former client, use its way of qualifying the expectation without importing that client's name or event.

## Delivery checks

- The requested reader action and factual conditions survive the rewrite.
- Observed sample traits have source locators; a proposed task voice is labeled as such.
- No personal or organizational facts came from style examples alone.
- The final authored outgoing text received the ordinary [editorial gate](../../natural-prose-editor/references/editorial-gate.md).
- Any profile retention uses the existing authorized private record, never this plugin.
