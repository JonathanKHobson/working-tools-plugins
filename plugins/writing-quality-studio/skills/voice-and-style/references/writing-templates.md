# Select and blend writing templates

Writing Templates owns the verified generic collection contract. The inspected current edition uses the 53-resource V01–V24/P25–P53 namespace; the actual recipient-authorized catalog supplies current entries and classifications. Profiles, candidates, modules, aliases and merges remain distinct, and unavailable or excluded resources must not be invented. Writing Quality Studio uses this reference to preserve personal voice and compatible blends when a template is explicitly selected. Profiles supply reusable structures and prose mechanics, not substitute identities. Ordinary drafting, editing, a typo fix, a protected quotation or an effective no-change edit does not trigger a generic catalog browse.

## Choose without making the user manage the process

An explicitly named generic template, supplied style, requested blend, or requested number of template options comes first. Use `writing-templates:writing-templates` with its actual authorized external collection when available. Resolve the current entrypoint and collection contract; installation alone supplies the runtime, not the prose collection. Modules, aliases and merges resolve to their verified base/module routes rather than becoming independent voices. If asked to choose from templates, offer a short purpose-based shortlist with names, differences and a recommendation; show the current verified resources and their classifications when requested, reporting unavailable entries distinctly. If asked to draft, deliver it without a mandatory menu or approval stop. Multiple templates in one piece do not imply multiple output drafts.

When no template is specified, choose structure from the reader's goal, audience, platform, genre, emotional context, accessibility, available evidence and current draft. Vary compatible mechanics within a substantial piece when they help the argument or attention; do not load the generic catalog merely for novelty. This is editorial judgment, not uniform random selection or a requirement to maintain a history database. Do not vary facts, persona, commitments or safety-critical wording for novelty.

For an explicitly selected generic profile, use `prepare_writing_voice_handoff` to verify its exact registered ID, version/source identity, selected and base guides, compatible modules, examples, source-edition readings, four-axis references and actual QA closure. Read those returned authorities before applying the template; use the collection's current offline engine/profile and separately authorized editorial companion where applicable. A public card or title alone is not the complete procedure. Freeze supplied facts, exact spans, claim strength, uncertainty and required terms first. Choose one organizing structure, then borrow compatible moves where a section needs a different job: a concrete opening, skeptical evidence check, conversational turn, explanatory passage or reflective close. Keep transitions intelligible and the point of view coherent. Do not combine contradictory reader contracts, such as delaying a decision in a decision-first memo.

## The author’s voice is the base

For writing on the author's behalf, start with current supplied prose or approved audience-relevant voice evidence. Use the existing [writing-style method](../../natural-prose-editor/references/writing-style.md) for narrowly relevant authentic examples only when needed. Preserve directness, register, rhythm, vocabulary, formatting, humor and sign-off insofar as evidence supports them. Formality follows the relationship and purpose; professional does not automatically mean stiff, and personal does not automatically mean intimate.

**Personal and professional correspondence:** maintain the author's personalized formal or informal voice throughout. A template may quietly organize the message; it must not rotate his personality, greeting, relationship, or sign-off. Routine replies can be drafted directly from available context without a library browse. V02 Warm colleague, V18 Diplomatic dissent and V20 Personal letter are structural options, not definitions of the author. In one-to-one outreach, this correspondence rule takes precedence over campaign variety.

**Articles, essays and newsletters:** keep recognizable aspects of the author's voice through most of the piece, including the body and transitions, rather than only an opening/sign-off. Mix compatible styles across a substantial article and vary those combinations between articles. Choose changes that follow the argument or reader's attention: a scene or question can open, investigation can test the claim, plain explanation can carry a difficult section, and reflection can close. V03, V04, V05, V07, V14, V22 and V23 supply different moves. Short pieces may need only a subtle shift; a formal or explicitly uniform brief may warrant a single register. Do not impose numerical voice percentages, a fixed section recipe, or paragraph-by-paragraph persona changes.

**Marketing and public promotion:** select and vary styles for ethical persuasion and useful attention. Fit audience awareness, the offer, proof, channel and requested action. Use honest relevance, concrete specificity, curiosity with a real payoff, a fair contrast or a supported objection. Rotate the opening and information order across a campaign where appropriate; hold the brand's identity, offer and evidence stable. No fabricated scarcity, urgency, social proof, outcomes, guarantees, outrage, shame, or clickbait that the body cannot fulfill. Apply the existing Marketing claim check to material assertions, including comparisons and sweeping claims; retain clear, prominent sponsorship disclosure where applicable. Keep this check internal unless review details were requested or an unresolved claim needs attention. Variety is an editorial choice, not evidence of conversion lift. For a separate brand, follow its approved voice; for the author's personal brand, retain his voice foundation.

**Fiction and creative work:** the creative owner keeps canon, POV, narrative distance, genre and character voice. Templates can shape scene movement or cadence; they do not authorize changed plot, new real-world biography, or flattening deliberate character differences. the author's correspondence/article default does not make every fictional narrator sound like the author.

For personal voice work, use only recipient-designated authentic samples or a profile supplied in the recipient’s private project. If none is available, continue from the current brief without claiming a personal match.

Prefer matched-genre finished samples for cadence; chat prompts primarily supply current preferences and interaction cues. Treat apparent durable traits as working hypotheses, especially with old or sparse samples. Do not infer authorship or assistance from rhetorical polish.

If authentic voice evidence is unavailable, use the supplied text and explicit preferences conservatively. Do not call the result a verified personal voice match or promote synthetic calibration into an approved profile. The release's examples are AI-authored demonstrations. Its private provisional comparison is excluded from this plugin. Keep personal samples and profiles outside generic plugin assets.

## Check the resulting prose

Compare the draft directly with the source facts and exact spans. Check missing evidence and access state explicitly: no results supplied does not mean no test occurred; a registration link that is not live does not mean registration is closed through every channel. Preserve unknown availability. Do not invent a promise to publish a link, send updates or take another action to complete a call to action. For these status/relationship changes, apply the existing [source-first mutation check](../../natural-prose-editor/references/controlled-contrast-revision.md). Check any binding length limit before returning. Check that chosen mechanics appear in the writing, not just its label; that blended passages connect; and that correspondence or article voice remains continuous. Remove a device that calls attention to itself without helping the reader. An effective original may remain unchanged. Humanizer and line-editing passes preserve purposeful rhythm, fragments, punctuation, repetition and chosen structure; they must not normalize the selected templates into one smooth cadence. Do not add errors or optimize for detector evasion.

Return the requested writing first. Explain the chosen template/blend briefly only when it aids selection, review or a reusable brief. Keep the choice in an existing project brief if ongoing consistency needs it; do not create a separate tracker. Drafting is not sending or publishing.

Use current owner-returned references instead of a historical archive path or hardcoded count. Its router distinguishes primary voices, candidates, modules, aliases and merged extensions. Editorial 1–5 communication targets and model-estimated ratings are different evidence types. Separation is uncalibrated and repeatability is not established. Test protected facts first, mechanism second; preserve failed trials and label editorial repairs separately. Same-brief comparisons are useful reader checks, not a universal voice test.

For a small/local model, pass only the selected complete guide, relevant examples and exact brief into a task-specific system context. Exclude personal names, signatures and unrelated host defaults from generic transfer tests; record the real model, context, recipe, sample hashes and any repairs. A failed trial remains failed.
