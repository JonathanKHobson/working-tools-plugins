# Adopted writing decision method

Integrated 2026-09-30. Read the full card for its actual trigger; routine correspondence does not require a rhetoric exercise. Historical coverage statements describe the pre-integration audit. ## P1 — Select a rhetorical architecture before a tone label

**Decision:** Should the message primarily inform, invite, recommend, teach, or elicit neutral evidence, and in what order should the reader encounter the point and its limits?

**Current coverage inspected:** writing-quality-studio/skills/communication-crafter/SKILL.md already asks for reader, relationship, decision, medium and evidence and offers answer-first, problem–evidence–action, case narrative, service offer and repair shapes. Its references/audience-and-story.md names reader decisions and useful structures. The legacy source's communication-stack.md covers intent, frame, structure, evidence, style and medium. These are substantial current capabilities.

**Observed gap:** When asked for multiple voices on one brief, no owner method requires a shared fact ledger plus materially different argument/reading sequences before tone changes. The user-reported goal in prompt 04 is to avoid one confident promotional register. Historical U03 [0021]–[0026] proposes transfer of kairos and rhetorical canons to UX/AI; those claims are reflective proposals, not proven effects.

**Source:** Aristotle's Book III (E01) distinguishes means, language and arrangement and treats clarity/appropriateness as situational choices; this is historical theory. Morkes and Nielsen (E05) compared five same-information variants on a 1997 travel website, a useful precedent for controlled comparison in that setting, not proof that any voice here performs better.

**Mechanism:** (1) Lock known facts, unknowns and prohibited inferences. (2) Name speaker, audience, relationship, channel, stakes, decision and real timing. (3) Select a structure: notice = facts first; conversation = reader's question first; decision note = bounded claim and grounds; editorial = concrete image then logistics; teaching orientation = reader choice and next step. (4) Choose only voice dimensions that serve that route: stance, distance, register, sentence length/rhythm, imagery and humor/intensity. (5) Use the canons as checks, not a formula: invention asks what support exists; arrangement asks where it belongs; style asks how to express it; delivery asks how the channel affects reading or speaking. Timing/kairos names a real decision window (October 17), never invented scarcity.

**Conditions / counterexample:** Use for substantive multi-variant copy or a consequential message. Skip for a routine one-sentence correction. Five drafts headed “Exciting news,” “Great news,” and “Wonderful news” with identical body order fail structural variety. An identity group is not a voice preset.

**Example:** V1 starts with logistics; V3 starts with the reader's decision and explicit unknowns; V4 opens with a modest reflection then lands on logistics. All retain F1–F11. **Owner:** communication-crafter for audience/architecture, voice-and-style for prose execution. **Overlap:** existing format-and-voice.md owns document shapes; this card adds controlled same-fact selection, not another catalog. **Effort:** S. **Test:** reviewers can point to at least two changes in hierarchy, stance, rhythm or relationship while a mutation audit finds zero material fact/qualification changes.
