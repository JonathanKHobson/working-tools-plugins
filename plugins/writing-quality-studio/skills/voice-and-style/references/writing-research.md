# Evidence behind voice adaptation and attention

Read when calibrating a personal voice from mixed sources, diagnosing a disjointed blend, or reviewing a persuasive hook. These sources inform editorial decisions; they do not validate the 24-profile taxonomy or measure any recipient author’s voice. Checked 2026-10-03. No source corpus is copied here.

- **Audience and register:** [Purdue OWL on tone, mood and audience](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/writing_style/diction/tone_mood_audience.html) supports adjusting diction and attitude to the reader and situation. Practical application: preserve the relationship and factual stance while adjusting formality, terminology and pace. This is professional guidance, not a measured effect of these templates.
- **Coherent variation:** [Purdue OWL on cohesion](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/the_writing_process/proofreading/revising_for_cohesion.html) emphasizes related topics and progression from familiar information. Check what connects adjacent passages after blending styles. The selection method's scene/investigation/explanation example is an editorial possibility, not a research-prescribed sequence.
- **Personal evidence:** [Sundararajan and Woodard, COLING 2018](https://aclanthology.org/C18-1238/) show topic interference and different contributions of syntax and vocabulary across settings. [Wang et al., TACL 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.tacl-1.80/) investigate whether authorship representations capture style; their results find sensitivity to style, while authorship prediction alone does not establish that all signals are stylistic. Neither study validates a personal prose generator. Our inference: compare matched genres, keep prompt-history cues separate from polished prose, and treat stable personal traits as hypotheses until corroborated. Do not use a similarity score as personal approval or infer authorship from style.
- **Attention:** [When curiosity gaps backfire](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11704130/) analyzes 8,977 headline experiments and finds context-dependent concreteness effects. It does not support a universal curiosity-gap winner or predict results for a recipient author. State the actual payoff, avoid misleading omissions, and evaluate reader fit before claiming an attention benefit.
- **Persuasion:** The FTC's [advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business) and [native-advertising guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/native-advertising-guide-businesses) support substantiated claims and recognizable commercial intent. Apply the existing Marketing claim review to material assertions, including comparisons and universal claims; keep relevant sponsorship disclosures clear and prominent. A confident template never supplies evidence. This reference is an editorial boundary, not a legal compliance certification.

Success checks: a formal and informal version preserve the same facts while fitting different relationships; a blended article has a traceable line of thought at each transition; a marketing hook accurately forecasts its body and every material claim has support or is qualified/removed. Use actual prose, not template labels, as evidence. Human preference and real engagement remain unmeasured.
