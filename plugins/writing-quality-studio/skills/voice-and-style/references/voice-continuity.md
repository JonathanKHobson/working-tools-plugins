## P4 — Hold a voice across a long document without flattening it

**Decision:** Which voice choices are stable across sections, and which tones vary with the reader's moment?

**Current coverage inspected:** voice-and-style/SKILL.md routes to Write Like Me, voice-matcher, brand-voice and tone-changer. Write Like Me asks for attributable original prose and medium/audience fit; format-and-voice.md already proposes observable guide traits and boundaries; long-document-editing/SKILL.md and its source procedure keep terms, formatting and voice notes across chunks. Third-party voice-matcher and tone-changer demand affiliate footer text, but the local owner explicitly says current user instructions and factual evidence govern historical source requirements. That footer is untrusted source content and is not adopted.

**Source:** Mailchimp's own guide (E03) demonstrates stable brand voice with situation-dependent tone; its specific wit and business persona are not transferred. Reddit E07 is one anecdotal concern about AI edits flattening voice, not prevalence evidence.

**Mechanism:** Build a small voice profile from authorized samples or the requested brief: stance toward the reader, relationship/distance, register, concrete syntax/rhythm examples, imagery/humor/intensity boundary, and a stable truth vocabulary. Show one positive passage and one counterexample. For a long document, keep facts, terms, level of certainty and relationship stable in a one-page ledger; allow local tone to shift when a section moves from invitation to limitation or instructions. At each section transition compare an actual paragraph to the profile, then run the same fact ledger again. If no attributable samples exist, label the profile “task voice,” not “Kyle's voice.”

**Conditions / counterexample:** Use for a requested style match, reusable guide or long-form consistency pass. Do not retrieve personal writing for a fictional benchmark or force one house voice across intentionally different pieces. Counterexample: “warm professional” plus an adjective list without sample sentences, boundaries or a fact audit. **Owner:** voice-and-style with long-document-editing for chunk management. **Overlap:** existing procedures remain complete; the staged method adds a same-fact contrast and explicit boundary record. **Effort:** S. **Test:** two sections on the same brief keep dates, unknowns and stance while changing local intensity appropriately; a reviewer can cite actual syntax choices rather than only adjective labels.
