## P2 — Map a claim through its warrant and strongest objection

**Decision:** Is the evidence adequate for the proposed conclusion and ask?

**Current coverage inspected:** communication-crafter/SKILL.md already requires proof near the point and preservation of uncertainty; ethical-communication.md rejects inflated authority and fake urgency; the legacy rhetorical-appeals.md names ethos/logos/pathos/kairos and warns against misuse. It does not provide a concise claim-to-warrant audit for incomplete evidence.

**Source:** Purdue OWL (E02) teaches claim, grounds, warrant, backing, qualifier and rebuttal as an explanation of Toulmin's method. Toulmin's original book was not inspected. U05 shows the author's historical analysis of appeal cues in ads; its campaign and medical claims are not adopted as proof.

**Mechanism:** Write the proposed claim in one sentence. List each ground with its actual provenance and status. State the warrant that connects those grounds to the claim; if the warrant is an assumption, expose it. Add backing only when it truly supports that bridge, and label normative backing as normative. Bound the claim with a qualifier tied to missing facts; a vague “maybe” does not replace a specific limit. Formulate the strongest plausible objection from the reader's position. Respond to its real cost or reason without changing the evidence standard. Finally, size the ask to what the claim can support.

**Conditions / counterexample:** Use for recommendations, invitations with material unknowns and sensitive persuasion. If the communication is a neutral survey question, do not run it through a persuasive rebuttal shape (P3). Counterexample: “Twenty-four seats, so only a few remain.” Total capacity does not establish availability. No amount of credibility language repairs the missing ground.

**Example:** examples/argument-and-neutrality.md maps the recommendation to stage copy but hold a registration CTA until the destination is live. It answers the real concern that delay costs lead time. **Owner:** communication-crafter/references/ethical-communication.md or an adjacent staged argument reference. **Overlap:** Research Methodology owns scholarly argument and citation; Career/Marketing own their applications. **Effort:** S. **Test:** ask a reviewer to identify claim, grounds, warrant, qualifier and strongest objection from the finished prose; if any link has to be invented by the reviewer, revise.

For an objection about time, cost or trust, distinguish a plausible consequence from an observed one. State conditional concerns as conditional; no supplied current date means no claim that a deadline is imminent. A nonlive or unavailable route does not prove no alternative exists. Recommend verifying a route rather than inventing its absence or an authority to fix it.
