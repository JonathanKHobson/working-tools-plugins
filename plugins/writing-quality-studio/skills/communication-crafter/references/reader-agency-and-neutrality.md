## P3 — Preserve reader agency and route neutral research away from persuasion

**Decision:** Is the ethical goal a fair choice, a neutral answer, or a persuasive ask?

**Current coverage inspected:** communication-crafter/references/ethical-communication.md already rejects manufactured urgency, inflated authority and guilt; audience-and-story.md limits an ask to evidence and relationship. Survey-wording-handoff.md routes instrument wording to Survey Studio. The owner is already strong here; this card makes the branch explicit during voice variation.

**Source:** NCA's 2024 Credo (E04) is a normative statement favoring accuracy, acknowledged dissent and responsible communication. It is not a result study. U03's suggestion to turn appeals into UX metrics remains the author's proposal, not a validated metric. U13's reflection on leading questions is a user source lead, not permission to optimize research for persuasion.

**Mechanism:** Before drafting, write “reader may freely decline/redirect” or “respondent must answer without being led.” Place a caveat beside the claim it changes. A deadline is a date, not evidence of scarcity. Keep access uncertainty, availability and outcome status visible in every voice. For research instruments, drop the CTA/benefit frame and ask one observable, non-presupposing question. Keep the survey owner responsible for sampling and analysis.

**Conditions / counterexample:** Use whenever publication, outreach, research or access claims could change a real decision. Counterexample: “How much did our helpful lab improve your dashboard skills?” presupposes an improvement not measured. A neutral alternative is in examples/argument-and-neutrality.md. **Owner:** communication-crafter, with Survey Studio for instruments. **Overlap:** current ethical reference remains the rule; this is a branch test, not an ethics replacement. **Effort:** XS. **Test:** mark each phrase that implies an outcome, seat, access feature or authority; every mark must map to a verified fact, explicit uncertainty or be removed. Neutral question contains no favorable premise.
