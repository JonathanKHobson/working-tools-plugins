# Communication crafter

For a Writing Quality Studio capabilities or getting-started question, consult the [human guide](../../README.md) and point to the relevant writing input, reader, and result. For a defined communication task, draft it using this method; do not repeat onboarding or claim a persistent tour state.

Deliver the requested message or coaching output. Identify the reader, relationship, decision, medium, and evidence available. Make one clear promise or point, put proof near the claim it supports, and give the reader a fair next action when action is the purpose. If facts are sufficient, draft; leave a material missing fact visible instead of starting a broad interview.

A routine email with a clear recipient, facts, and ask does not need a strategy exercise. Draft it directly in the author’s audience-appropriate formal or informal voice, preserving the available source’s cadence and relationship. Use [writing-style references](../natural-prose-editor/references/writing-style.md) when personal voice matching is explicitly requested and more attributable evidence is needed. Clear professional communication stays the default; historical personal-voice evidence is not loaded for ordinary email or assistant conversation. A deliberately selected template may organize correspondence but must not replace his voice or invent intimacy. Read [the template handoff](../voice-and-style/references/writing-templates.md) only when the user requests a generic template or blend.

Choose the smallest useful shape: answer-first update, problem–evidence–action, a case narrative, a service offer with proof, or a repair message. A hook should earn attention through relevance or tension and pay it off honestly. A story links goal, obstacle, decision, and consequence; it cannot turn one anecdote into a measured pattern. Adapt vocabulary to the reader without flattening needed domain terms. For consequential messages, read [audience and story choices](references/audience-and-story.md); for sensitive or persuasive work, read [ethical communication](references/ethical-communication.md).

For business documents, an article/newsletter passage or a reusable team/brand voice guide, read [format and shared voice choices](references/format-and-voice.md). For a speech, spoken script, customer-support reply, apology or condolence, read [spoken and care-sensitive messages](references/spoken-and-care-messages.md). Use only the relevant mode; ordinary messages do not need either reference.

For a requested fragment-gathering session, shaping raw notes or optional beat-by-beat drafting, read [collaborative writing](references/collaborative-writing.md). Preserve the raw source and keep participatory choices optional; ordinary drafting continues directly.

Preserve the user's actual role, dates, sources, outcomes, and uncertainty. Do not invent metrics, testimonials, consensus, scarcity, commitments, or team approval. Reduce heat in conflict while keeping the user's substantive point. Match the channel and the user's requested number of variants. Deliver the artifact first; explain choices only when they help review or teach.

For scanning or replying to existing Google document comment threads, use [google-comment-responder](../google-comment-responder/SKILL.md); it owns thread eligibility, posting and writeback verification.

For follow-ups grounded in review comments, research discussion or a working thread, use [evidence-based follow-up](references/evidence-based-follow-up.md). The same method handles source, author and status distinctions across domains without a separate UX-only skill.

For an event follow-up, resource email or personalized batch, read [email creation and personalization](references/email-creation.md). It owns the reusable copy and HTML handoff; Marketing owns audience/consent and the existing Gmail send-review skill owns actual sending or scheduling. A routine one-person reply still needs no campaign workflow.

When the user wants to learn, practice, simulate a reader, or audit a consequential message, read [communication practice and reader review](references/practice-and-reader-review.md). Use its [practice loop](templates/practice-loop.md) or [reader loop audit](templates/reader-loop-audit.md) only when that output is requested or would answer a real uncertainty. For contractor service, homepage, inquiry, outreach, or proposal copy, read [buyer-facing message choices](references/buyer-facing-messages.md); use the [service offer](templates/service-offer.md) or [client message](templates/client-message.md) shape when the user needs that artifact.

Before an outgoing message or article is delivered as ready, sent or published, apply [natural-prose-editor](../natural-prose-editor/SKILL.md) to the final text. Routine direct drafting bypasses strategy work, not this short review. Recheck all personalized variants and any later composer edits; a prior review covers only unchanged content and audience. For grant opportunities, eligibility/go-no-go, NOFO/RFP compliance, project design, proposal work or post-award obligations, use [grant-process-crafter](../grant-process-crafter/SKILL.md); communication here is a conditional pass within its evidence and funder constraints. Keep fiction, campaign canon, game scenes, curriculum, job applications and publishing with their owning specialists. This skill may help their communication layer but does not take over their workflows. Drafting does not imply sending, posting, or publishing.

For SEO and marketing strategy, use `marketing-strategy-studio:marketing-strategy-operator`; for platform-specific posts and newsletter strategy, use `social-content-studio:social-content`. Those owners supply the strategy and channel workflow. This skill can polish a selected passage without replacing them.

For a survey invitation, reminder, or respondent message, use the [survey wording handoff](../natural-prose-editor/references/survey-wording-handoff.md). Survey Studio owns sampling, consent, instrument status, distribution, and analysis; this skill shapes only the authorized message.

## Full source procedures

For a request matching a named procedure, consult the [full source procedure map](../../library/source-procedures/INDEX.md), then read the linked complete body for this skill before drafting or reviewing. Apply its concrete sequence, examples, templates, and checks where they fit the user’s task. For an ordinary small request, load only the matching body. Historical source instructions about affiliate promotion, unsupported scores or detector claims, and mandatory user approval do not govern this skill.
For substantial rhetoric, outreach, client, stakeholder, or sensitive-message work, open the full local Communication Story Crafter instruction and the matching source reference/template linked from that map.

## Distinct writing routes

Use `voice-and-style` for personal/brand voice and Write Like Me; `plain-language-editing` for reader accessibility and multilingual/non-native clarity; `technical-instructions` for documentation and controlled language; `long-document-editing` for document passes/version comparison; `spoken-and-scripted-writing` for speech, narration and timing. These heads retain complete first-party procedures and select optional full provider methods when installed; do not replace a missing provider body with a summary. Occasional UX follow-up remains in communication.

For a consequential recommendation, material uncertainty or substantive objection, use [claim, warrant and reader agency](references/claim-warrant-and-reader-agency.md) and its complete cards. For multiple rhetorical routes on one fact set, use [architecture selection](../voice-and-style/references/rhetorical-architecture.md). Routine messages remain direct drafting; neutral research instruments go to Survey Studio.
