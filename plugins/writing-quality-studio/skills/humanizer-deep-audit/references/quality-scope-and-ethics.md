# Release Boundary

- Improve specificity, evidence, structure, rhythm, reader fit, and the author’s own voice.
- Treat every pattern as a revision cue, never proof of authorship.
- Do not promise detector results or fabricate human experience, facts, quotations, sources, or deliberate errors.
- For submitted work, follow the applicable disclosure policy and preserve drafts and source notes.
- Preserve intentional dialect, register, repetition, and punctuation unless they conflict with the author’s stated goal.

Research and false-positive detail lives in `research-evidence.md` and `human-signals-and-false-positives.md`.
