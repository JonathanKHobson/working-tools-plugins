# Quality review and release boundary

Improve specificity, evidence, structure, cadence, reader fit and the author's intended voice. Patterns are revision cues, never authorship evidence. Do not promise detector results or invent human experiences, facts, quotations, sources or errors. Preserve intentional dialect, register, repetition and punctuation where they serve the task. Submitted work follows the actual assistance/disclosure policy; retain legitimate drafts/source notes in their owning private record.

## Requested quality ratings or scored draft comparisons

A routine rewrite needs no score. If the user requests a rating, use their supplied rubric when it fits the task. Otherwise declare a local editorial scale and the criteria before scoring. This is a subjective decision aid with evidence, not a validated measurement of human authorship, originality, detector success or engagement. Never call it a proprietary score.

Read the complete available text, reader/purpose brief and relevant source facts. For a comparison, keep the same criteria, source access and scale for each version. Name missing inputs and do not treat an unassessed criterion as zero or award credit for an unseen feature. A criterion can be unassessed when evidence or an authentic sample needed for it is absent.

A useful default criterion set is:

| Criterion | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Meaning and factual fidelity | Claims, quantities, conditions, negation, quotations and source support; unsupported specifics are defects, not authenticity |
| Reader clarity and action | Whether the intended reader can identify the point, needed distinction or next step without hidden context |
| Organization and progression | Paragraph jobs, evidence order, transitions, redundancy and an ending that performs a real function |
| Voice and register fit | Supplied author/brand samples or task brief, relationship, degree of certainty and deliberate expression |
| Sentence and format usability | Cadence, reference clarity, difficult clause chains, literal/technical integrity and structure appropriate to the medium |

For each assessable criterion, an optional local 0–3 rating means: 0 = fails a required condition; 1 = a material obstacle remains; 2 = serves the task with a specific repair needed; 3 = meets the inspected task requirement. Cite the exact passage/locator and reason. A numeric summary, if requested, reports earned over assessable points with coverage; do not silently normalize missing coverage into a complete verdict. Explain that a different reader or task can change the judgment. Any factual, privacy or permission failure is separately identified and cannot be canceled by pleasant style.

Give the requested assessment or comparison, strongest retained passages and prioritized concrete repairs. Show a short before/after only where it explains a real change. Recommend a version from its reader/evidence tradeoff, not from an invented naturalness percentage. When revision was requested, return the revised artifact and recheck affected criteria on the exact final text. Keep review, prepared revision and delivered/publication state distinct.

For measured sentence observations, use existing `style_baseline.py` or the local text-hygiene helper with their tokenizer/sample limitations; do not manufacture a readability grade or impose a universal sentence-length target. Source and false-positive support remain in `research-evidence.md` and `human-signals-and-false-positives.md`.

## Worked rating boundary

Two versions report a pilot: one says a change may help under the tested condition; the other declares a guaranteed outcome for everyone. The second may sound smoother but fails factual fidelity if the source supports only the pilot. A fair comparison retains the condition, flags the expansion and recommends a supported repair. A missing author sample makes authentic-voice matching unassessed; it is not proof that either version has weak voice.
