# Manuscript structure and revision

Use the requested discipline, document and actual author instructions. These are editorial questions, not a compulsory paper template.

| Part | Necessary work | Common failure to repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abstract | State the concrete problem, relevant method, supported principal result and its scoped meaning within the actual limit. | A claim stronger than the results section, invented novelty, or broad benefits replacing a specific result. |
| Introduction | Establish the unresolved problem, why it matters in this setting, limits of prior approaches and the aim or question. | Generic history, unsupported “no prior work” claims or a solution introduced before its need is clear. |
| Literature synthesis | Organize sources around questions, methods, agreement, differences and gaps; distinguish evidence quality and setting. | One summary per paper, duplicated evidence treated as independent support, or citations attached to claims they do not establish. |
| Methods | Explain the design, sample/data, operational definitions, procedure, analysis and required reproducibility detail. For mechanisms, connect the design choice to the problem it addresses. | A sequence of disconnected definitions, omitted controls, or prose polishing that changes what was actually done. |
| Results | Present measured observations with their conditions and denominators; connect figures/tables to the stated question. | Interpretations mixed with observations, changed precision or units, missing unfavorable findings, or a comparison presented as an isolated component effect. |
| Discussion | Interpret within design limits, examine plausible alternatives and limitations, and state implications proportional to evidence. | Causal or population expansion, defensive dismissal of contrary findings, or untested mechanism claimed as a measured outcome. |
| Conclusion | Answer the stated question within the demonstrated scope and identify the consequential unresolved issue if needed. | New results, larger populations or stronger promises introduced only at the end. |
| Captions/equations | Explain the relationship, symbol or comparison the reader needs; keep identifiers and references consistent with the source. | A disconnected equation, missing units or a caption that asserts more than the displayed evidence. |

## Build the argument without inventing a story

Ask what question each paragraph resolves and what information the next paragraph needs. Move from the technical need to the necessary definition or mechanism, then to its supported consequence. Do not force parallel problems into one linear causal chain. Separate the common backbone, the actual new contribution and any adaptive element so evidence is attributed to what changed.

For example, if a model changes its encoder and training schedule together, a better aggregate score supports that full-method comparison under the tested conditions. It does not isolate the encoder's contribution. A component claim requires a suitable control or narrower wording. A proposed diagnostic is an unrun experiment until evidence shows otherwise.

During compression, cut repeated rationale and redundant sentences before reproducibility detail, caveats or definitions. During expansion, use supplied explanation and evidence; mark a missing derivation or experiment instead of manufacturing it. Preserve necessary hedging, technical terms and distinctive disciplinary voice. Use a small consistency sheet for repeated symbols, method names, metrics and section claims when the document is long enough to need one.

## Reviewer responses and coursework

For each reviewer point, identify the concern, the supported answer, the edit actually made and its precise location. If disagreeing, give a reason tied to evidence or scope; do not claim that an experiment, citation check or revision happened when it is only proposed. Verify the updated manuscript and response agree.

For an academic essay, test thesis specificity, paragraph claims, provided examples, counterarguments and transitions against the assignment. Suggest where the author could supply a personal example; do not invent one. Preserve the author's learning objective and requested feedback-versus-rewrite boundary. Check permitted assistance or disclosure against the actual supplied policy when it matters.

## Final consistency pass

Compare abstract, introduction, methods, results and conclusion for the same research question, method identity, quantities and scope. Check each figure/equation/citation reference, all fixed terms and the requested format. Source verification is a distinct check from writing fluency. Record unresolved evidence and inaccessible sources precisely.
