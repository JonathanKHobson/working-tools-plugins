# Academic writing

Start with the actual manuscript, evidence, audience and requested operation: developmental feedback, a revised passage, shortening, a section draft, source/claim review or reviewer response. Preserve the stated language, discipline, author voice and target requirements. Infer routine choices; ask only for a missing constraint that would change the result. Editing supplied material does not require starting the research pyramid or a new literature search.

Identify what is fixed: numbers, units, equations, symbols, definitions, metrics, datasets, groups, protocols, baseline identities and evaluation scope. Separate proposed conceptual changes from prose edits. Do not silently repair a result, change a method, invent a citation or manufacture missing evidence for a smoother argument.

For manuscript structure and section-level writing, read [manuscript craft](references/manuscript-craft.md). For scientific reasoning or claims, read [evidence and tools](references/evidence-and-tools.md): connect the concrete problem, changed mechanism, expected observable consequence and evidence that actually supports the conclusion. For source verification beyond internal consistency, use the existing [evidence review](../evidence-review/SKILL.md). Commission new research only when requested or necessary to resolve an identified gap.

Use the installed Academic Writing Toolkit only for the relevant operation—citation consistency, BibTeX structure, reading-note templates, paragraph cues or requested British spelling. Its checks do not prove source existence, causal validity or journal acceptance. A tool failure must remain visible; use a labeled manual check when feasible.

For essays and coursework, inspect the assignment's actual requirements and requested level of help. Preserve the student's own argument and level; explain recurring corrections when learning is the goal. Do not fabricate personal reflection. If the requested help must remain feedback or coaching under the supplied rules, give that output rather than quietly completing a submission. Source paraphrases still need attribution. Never promise detector outcomes or invent an authorship history.

Verify current official author instructions before asserting a journal-specific formatting rule. Do not inherit punctuation bans, tense rules, section quotas or disclosure wording from a plugin's journal label. Keep an existing rubric or target brief authoritative.

Return the requested deliverable first. When revision is requested, supply revised text plus only the consequential changes and unresolved evidence issues; for feedback, give exact locations and concrete options. Cross-check material numbers, citations and term usage against the supplied originals. Report source/format checks actually performed. Editing, author approval and manuscript submission are distinct states; submission uses its authorized destination workflow.

Use Writing Quality Studio for a broader voice or ordinary prose pass, `career-studio:application-statements` for admissions and other application statements, and the current document/PDF/presentation/Sites provider for the requested artifact or delivery. Preserve their format, visual QA and publication requirements without duplicating their engines here.
