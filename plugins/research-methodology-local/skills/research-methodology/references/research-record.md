# Research Record Template

Adapt this record to the task; omit empty sections. Labels and content in the delivered record follow the user's output language. A conversation record is sufficient unless a file is requested.

## Required findings ledger

While collecting, keep this table in the conversation. One row per claim. A finding without evidence is a hypothesis; put it in a separate `Open` list, not in the ledger.

```
| # | Claim | Evidence (file:line / URL / version / commit / PMID / model+date) | Rank | Answers |
|---|-------|-----------------------------------------------|------|---------|
| F1 | … | … | 1–4 | Q1 |
```

- **Claim**: the statement the evidence supports.
- **Evidence**: locator that another person can open (file:line, URL, version, commit, attachment, PMID/DOI and study design, product model and capture date, filing type and date, photo or serial number).
- **Rank**: source rank from the main skill (1 originating source, 2 authoritative reference, 3 direct inspection, 4 secondary).
- **Answers**: charter question id (Q1, Q2, …).

The sections below are optional expansion when the investigation is large. They do not replace the ledger table.

## Evidence entries

For each material entry record:
- Stable local label matching the ledger row, for example F1.
- Claim or question addressed.
- Source title and URL, or attached file and actual locator.
- Source role: primary, secondary, or user-provided observation.
- Version/publication date and retrieval date if known.
- Evidence excerpt or observation, with context and units.
- Fact, observation, inference, assumption, or unknown.
- Access: full text, excerpt, abstract, inaccessible, or not checked.
- Applicability, contradictions, and limitations.
