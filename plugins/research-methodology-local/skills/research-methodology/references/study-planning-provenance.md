## Inherited vendor methods (inactive, retained unchanged)

Historical UI UX Designer research-plan/usability instruction bodies remain omitted because no redistribution grant was established. Prior inspections and adaptations are provenance, not current task dependencies. The full Studio study-planning method, guides and records operate directly through the [native map](../../../library/source-procedures/INDEX.md); they do not claim to execute the publisher's procedure.

Useful methods preserved in the active adaptation: decision/research questions, choosing interviews/surveys/analytics/competitor/field or diary methods by evidence need, eligibility/screening, neutral recent-behavior guide, affinity/pattern synthesis and opportunities, consent/minimization/anonymized sharing; and test goals/real target users, realistic tasks, consent/think-aloud/follow-up/closing, optional success/time/error/ease/SUS measures, impact/frequency/severity analysis, facilitator script/task list/notes/findings outputs. Sampling is replaced with explicit qualitative coverage/stopping rationale; estimates/comparisons require suitable statistical design. Survey instruments and standard-scale administration remain with Survey Studio. Method/guide/records retain these useful steps in full with stronger source-linked checks, not a synopsis of the vendor originals.

Source-specific design defaults are bounded: use truthful task/invitation wording, accessible participation, original authorized stimuli and safe data; route implementation of visual accessibility or brand assets to the actual design owner. Tool plans/prices are not research evidence; verify time-sensitive provider facts only when relevant. No unsolicited vendor recommendation or referral footer is part of a study output. This wrapper does not claim static review proves the absence of behavioral leakage.
