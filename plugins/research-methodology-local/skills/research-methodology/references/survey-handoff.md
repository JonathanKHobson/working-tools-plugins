# Survey Studio handoff

Survey construction is owned by the separate Survey Studio plugin. Resolve `survey-strategist` from the installed plugin. Install or select the maintained Survey Studio plugin in the current host; do not hardcode a cache version or local author path. If unavailable, name the missing dependency and preserve the brief rather than inventing a second builder.

Survey Studio retains item wording, response options, branching, bias review, accessibility, privacy, pretesting, analysis mapping, and export. Writing Quality Studio can make a bounded clarity or voice pass against the canonical instrument, then return edits by item ID for Survey Studio's method and programming QA; prose editing does not establish validity or field readiness.

Pass the decision owner and decision, target population and experience window, method rationale, sampling/interpretation limits, privacy constraints, desired burden, destination and any required measure. Receive a canonical versioned instrument, item-to-analysis map/codebook, validated requested exports and QA/pretest status. UX Design Toolkit can review respondent interaction and rendered output without taking ownership of the instrument.

For reuse across experiences or delivery channels, preserve context and version while evaluating whether constructs and populations support comparison. Distinguish self-reported perceptions from observed outcomes. A reference survey may inspire structure without establishing validity in a new context or supplying unobserved response options.

A research brief does not authorize deployment or sending invitations. Preserve current user authorization, implement only the requested slice, and distinguish designed, configured, persisted, published and distributed states.
