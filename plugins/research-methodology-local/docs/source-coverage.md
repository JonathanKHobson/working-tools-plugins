# Method and resource coverage

This package preserves the full maintained eight-skill task surface and its supporting first-party methods. The original six-step pyramid and three research modes remain in `skills/research-methodology/`; the author licensed the five supplied method files as first-party noncode under CC BY-SA 4.0 on 2026-10-10. This is a full method package, with separately identified provider and runtime boundaries.

| Skill | Complete bundled method and resources | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `research-methodology` | Pyramid, three type references, source strategy, record, study planning guides/records, qualitative branches/quality/example, task evaluation and survey handoff | The five supplied method files have a scoped author grant in `SOURCE-RIGHTS.md`. |
| `academic-writing` | Manuscript craft and evidence/tool audit, with full section, claim, reviewer-response and final-consistency procedures | Four named publisher procedures remain optional external dependencies. |
| `current-social-research` | Complete pre-migration source body, adoption reference and planner script, plus active head | Public current signals need live retrieval for current claims. |
| `evidence-review` | Active claim/source review, standing-answer freshness and cited source map | Critical Compass execution routes to Reasoning Toolkit. |
| `source-discovery` | Active source discovery procedure | Retrieval depends on current host capability. |
| `research-handoff` | Full research handoff and document-to-method procedure, compiler schema, synthetic example and local helper | Persona/journey provider procedure is external; source document rights remain separate. |
| `critical-reasoning` | Compatibility handoff | Reasoning Toolkit owns the full method and Critical Compass runtime. |
| `karpathy-guidelines` | Bundled working principles | The eight extensions are covered by the scoped author grant in `SOURCE-RIGHTS.md`. |

Source files retained but inactive: complete local current-social original, complete locally adapted Oppia interview/usability originals, shared quality gates, external-method review and full Book-to-Skill method/README/pyproject snapshot. Their licenses, roles and limits are recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and adjacent provenance. No Oppia corpus or book content is included.

Exact copied publisher files withheld: AIHumanizer academic-writing/essay-humanizer heads plus README; Research Writing Guardrails two heads and their two references; UI UX Designer personas/journey, usability and UX plan heads plus README. Their source paths and SHA-256 hashes are in `PROVENANCE.json`; the active extension routes and absence behavior are in `library/source-procedures/INDEX.md`. Publisher availability is host-specific: a Codex cache proves neither Claude installation nor user entitlement. The bundled methods preserve independent academic writing and study planning work, while provider-specific steps remain distinct.
