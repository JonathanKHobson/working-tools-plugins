# Independent use cases

Select the smallest task owner and read its complete required procedures, examples and templates. The plugin is a package; do not load every skill.

| Skill | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| [academic-writing](../skills/academic-writing/SKILL.md) | Draft, revise or audit papers, theses, abstracts, literature syntheses, technical manuscripts, academic essays and reviewer responses from supplied evidence. Use for section structure, evidence-bounded argument and submission requirements; use research-methodology for new investigation, and career skills for faculty job letters or admissions statements. |
| [critical-reasoning](../skills/critical-reasoning/SKILL.md) | Compatibility route for the former Research Toolkit critical-reasoning skill; use reasoning-studio:critical-reasoning for new argument or content audits. |
| [current-social-research](../skills/current-social-research/SKILL.md) | Research recent public community, social, creator, company, and market signals with dated sources and explicit coverage limits. Use for current sentiment, language, objections, platform norms, competitor movement, or a source-backed social/marketing handoff. Do not use for timeless lookup or private account scraping. |
| [evidence-review](../skills/evidence-review/SKILL.md) | Audit a research report for unsupported claims, citation mismatches, outdated evidence, contradictions, and overstated causality. |
| [karpathy-guidelines](../skills/karpathy-guidelines/SKILL.md) | Apply Karpathy-inspired principles when research leads to code, documents, protocols, purchases, repairs, or decisions that require clear assumptions, minimal scope, and verifiable outcomes. |
| [research-handoff](../skills/research-handoff/SKILL.md) | Turn existing research into a bounded implementation specification, action plan, protocol, decision record, or continuation brief without silently implementing it. |
| [research-methodology](../skills/research-methodology/SKILL.md) | Plan decision-linked interviews and usability studies; analyze supplied qualitative data; investigate products, literature, devices, markets or technology with traceable evidence. Use for study protocols, research questions, option comparisons and evidence reviews. Survey instruments route to Survey Studio; Oppia research retains its project owner. |
| [source-discovery](../skills/source-discovery/SKILL.md) | Find and assess sources for a bounded research question with explicit date, provenance and coverage limits. |
