## Adapted

- Creator intent is evidence of intended behavior, not a reason to deny reproducible observed behavior. Version conflicts remain explicit.
- The source's historical meaning of experimental research is identified as a local convention. It is not imposed on all sciences.
- Unsubstantiated research-percentage claims were omitted.
- Causal conclusions require evidence beyond timing and correlation; alternatives, controls, and noisy observations are explicit.
- Estimates and deadlines are included only when justified; unavailable measurements are not invented.
- The supplied rule's Claude-specific language is model-neutral. Fixed 4,000/30,000-token defaults are replaced by user/host budgets and truthful accounting.
- Read-before-write, convention matching, conflict disclosure, meaningful testing, checkpoints, and explicit failure reporting are retained with scope-aware wording.
- The claim that inability to write a good test means the function is wrong is replaced by a more defensible requirement to improve validation.
- Cursor `alwaysApply` metadata is replaced by explicit bundled references, not an unsupported global plugin rule.
- Response-language selection, evidence provenance, source-instruction isolation, graceful tool fallback, and cloud-independent runtime are added.

The additional eight rules in the supplied file are labeled as supplied extensions, not independently attributed to Andrej Karpathy. This package does not claim endorsement. The five original method files have the direct author grant recorded in `SOURCE-RIGHTS.md`; no blanket grant is asserted for other upstream or publisher-owned material. ## Domain generalization (1.1.0 rebuild)

Examples, source ranks, and discovery copy were widened from software-only wording to products, scientific literature, device failures, and markets, while keeping the pyramid structure. Assumptions applied in this rebuild:

- Market "insider" sources mean lawfully public insider disclosures (filings, ownership reports, earnings transcripts). Material non-public information is excluded; forecasts are scenarios with stated uncertainty, not investment advice.
- Appliance diagnosis requires power-off before inspection; gas, refrigerant, sealed high-voltage parts, and warranty work go to a professional.
- Health-related research does not send personal health details to public search; conclusions are literature summaries, not medical advice.
