# Visual reasoning on a shared board

Use Creative Studio's shared `draw-think` tools when spatial relationships, sequences, alternatives or a worked explanation will help the current task. This is a shared thinking surface the assistant can create and revise through backend tools, not just a human-operated viewer. Follow the installed Creative Studio `references/draw-think.md` full contract and its task-to-canvas examples. Require that owner to be installed and its tools available; do not invent availability or register a duplicate server here.

Choose one pattern: claim/evidence/objection cards for an argument; labeled arrows and uncertainty notes for a causal hypothesis; options against user-supplied criteria for a decision; or a partial worked example with one open step for teaching. Keep labels and relationships editable, distinguish observations from assumptions, and let the user's corrections drive the next small patch. Do not invent scores, probabilities or evidence to fill a layout.

Read the saved board and its accompanying PNG before interpreting unlabeled marks. Preserve human edits and current revision checks. Drawing does not automatically authorize a full Critical Compass audit. If image handoff is unsupported, use the editor's image download and prepared request rather than claiming the assistant can see the drawing.
