# Critical reasoning

Stress-test a claim, argument or research method with Critical Compass and explicit evidence/assumption boundaries.

## Complete procedures to load

Read the task-matching files below in full before applying that procedure. Load only the relevant variant and its required support. These contain the operative methods, examples and templates; this entrypoint does not replace them. A plugin is a package, not a requirement to read every skill.

- [Full Critical Compass contract](../../docs/critical-compass-integration.md).
- External prior-owner Evidence-review workflow.
- External prior-owner Research record.

For a lightweight check of a supplied source before relying on it, read [critical source evaluation](references/critical-source-evaluation.md). For question generation or optional contrasting research perspectives, use [preserved research perspectives](../research-methodology/references/research-perspectives.md). These focused branches do not require a full audit or research pyramid.

## Workflow

1. Separate supplied claim, supporting evidence, inference and uncertainty. Decide whether a quick scan, claim test or full audit fits.
2. Read the full bridge contract and use the actual available tool. Respect required human questions, consent, dissent and readiness state.
3. Check claims against source evidence. A local framework or diagnostic score is a reasoning aid, not external corroboration.

## Deliver and verify

Return the strongest supported conclusion, concrete objections and uncertainties. Validate a requested report payload before generating it.

## Example boundary

Good: identify an unsupported causal step and the evidence needed to resolve it. Bad: present a framework match as a verified fact.
