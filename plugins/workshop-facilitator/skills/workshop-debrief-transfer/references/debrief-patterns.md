# Debrief patterns

## After practice
1. “What did you try?”
2. “What happened?”
3. “Which evidence changed your next move?”
4. “Where will you use this?”
Ask sequentially. Use the actual artifact or transcript. If the learner's explanation conflicts with the performance, explore the discrepancy without shaming them.

## After a group decision
Read the proposed receipt. Ask what it misses, which concerns remain, and whether named owners accept the next action. Separate a recommendation from a final decision and willingness from commitment.

## After exploration
Capture the strongest observations and unresolved questions. Do not force prioritization if exploration was the agreed purpose. A concrete next research question can be the appropriate closure.

## Minimal closeout: 5 minutes
2 minutes: each person records a useful result or changed understanding.
2 minutes: name one next use, action, or unresolved question.
1 minute: confirm where the artifact lives and who will act, if known.
This is a proposed fallback, not an empirical ideal.

## Follow-through card
Result; next context; action; owner accepted/proposed; opportunity/date; support; evidence of use; review condition. For learning, distinguish a planned action from demonstrated transfer. For a workshop decision, distinguish a documented agreement from implementation.

## Optional message draft
“Here is the result we captured. The remaining question is __. The proposed next action is __, with ownership __. Please correct any part that does not reflect the session.”
Use this only as a draft unless sending is authorized. Do not create reminders or recurring monitoring automatically.
