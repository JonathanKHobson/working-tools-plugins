# Post-session operational closeout

Use after the session when the requested wrap-up includes operational work. Keep the existing debrief, participant action card and learning-transfer distinction. This is a conditional handoff, not a requirement to activate every neighbor or build a new tracker.

## Start with the actual session

Read the current session record and requested outcome. Capture only applicable artifacts, unresolved issues, accepted/proposed owners, next date or condition, and current permission. Preserve source links and disagreements. A recording is not permission to share it; attendance is not marketing consent; a completed session is not proof of payment or learning transfer. If an item is absent or out of scope, omit it. Complete authorized preparation while identifying any consequential missing fact.

## Finish at the requested level

Reuse an existing project record if a receipt is needed. For each applicable action report result/artifact, accepted or proposed owner, current state, supporting evidence and the one remaining dependency. A short paragraph may suffice; do not create a closeout board by default. Honor explicit send/publish authority already given without asking again, and verify the resulting destination. When only wrap-up or drafting was requested, leave prepared work at that state. Do not schedule new reminders or monitor transfer automatically.

## Desk examples

- “Wrap up today's session; we have a recording and attendee artifacts.” Preserve the debrief, prepare only requested recording/evidence work; no marketing campaign, invoice or public post follows from those facts.
- “Draft the promised recap to the attendees.” Writing produces the recap; recipient operations are conditional; Gmail review/send waits for send authorization. A useful draft is the requested finish.
- “Send the unchanged reviewed recap to these attendees.” Reuse its completed writing review, run the applicable Gmail send review, send under that existing authority and verify the sent receipt.
- “Prepare the agreed final invoice; a deposit was paid.” Solo prepares from approved terms and applies the deposit. Do not issue or send it unless requested; unresolved payment evidence remains unresolved.
- “Use this workshop as public portfolio proof.” Career checks specific disclosure permission and observed outcomes before a concrete public artifact; recording access or attendee praise alone does not establish permission or measured learning.
