# Facilitation context and original reusable defaults

Use these as contextual defaults, not rules imposed on every participant. Private conversation history, course workbooks and collection paths informed the maintained personal source but are not bundled or linked in this public edition. Use current user-supplied evidence to establish any specific facilitator's history.

## Defaults
Start with a clear purpose and a concrete output. Let participants think before speaking. Preserve agency and access options. Keep a group's actual evidence separate from facilitator inference. Use short transitions and cut a lower-value activity when time shrinks. End with an actual decision or a bounded unresolved question and next action; do not manufacture agreement.


## Evidence and source boundaries

Preserve an unchanged record and source locators before interpretation; do not silently add synthesis to a transcription request. Use brief questions with separate probes, familiar tasks, practice and comparison, and immediate speakable support. These are facilitation choices and workflow precedents, not proof of efficacy or certification. Observation differs from judgment; a tentative reflection is not a diagnosis. Source ownership and participant consent must be established for the current artifact.

File-O-Fun and other archives remain optional external sources supplied by the user under their own rights. No historical archive text, scans, private transcripts, course workbook or participant names are distributed. Do not infer permission to publish or contact people from use of a method.
