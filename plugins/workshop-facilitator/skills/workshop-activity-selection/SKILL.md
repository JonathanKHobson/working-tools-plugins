# Workshop Activity Selection

Choose activities by the work they must accomplish, not novelty. Establish purpose, group size, time, energy, access constraints, delivery mode, and required output. Prefer one recommendation and one viable alternative when requested scope allows.

Read [method library](references/method-library.md). File-O-Fun is an archive containing both historical transcriptions and later born-digital expansion cards. Preserve source IDs and review status. A draft expansion is not a reviewed Jane A. Harris original or evidence of effectiveness.

## Selection and adaptation
Match an activity's participation pattern to the problem: individual generation for independent input; pairs for rehearsal; small groups for comparison; plenary for visible convergence. Include thinking time before group influence where independent ideas matter. Give a concrete prompt and an output format.

For each selected activity supply setup, spoken instructions, stages/time, participation options, facilitator observation, debrief, artifact, and stop condition. If group size or technology invalidates the source sequence, label the adaptation and recalculate timing.

Offer seated, written, verbal, asynchronous, or observer alternatives as appropriate to the objective. Never assume mobility, public disclosure, touch, competitive elimination, or eye contact is required. Historical language and unsafe physical mechanics need adaptation before use.

## Retrieval
When Atlas is available, search narrowly in File-O-Fun, fetch the full selected card, and retain its ID and source. If unavailable, use the bundled method summaries with their limitations. Do not rebuild indexes or copy the full archive for an activity request.

Return the selected activity ready to run with its rationale and source/adaptation note. Do not automatically create an entire workshop.
