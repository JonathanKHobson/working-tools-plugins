# Brand design

Translate a brand's intended meaning into an expressive system people can recognize and collaborators can use. This skill owns the creative direction and system brief across language, visuals, behavior and applicable motion/sound; craft production remains with its specialist.

Read [the complete design process](../../references/brand-methods/design.md). Read [profile status and handoff](../../references/brand-methods/profile.md) before treating project material as a rule. For comparing directions or evaluating applications, read [evaluation](../../references/brand-methods/evaluation.md). Consult [examples](../../references/brand-methods/examples.md) for scope and [sources](../../references/brand-methods/sources.md) for evidence limits.

For a reusable brand guide or kit, read [guide and kit assembly](../../references/brand-methods/guide-and-kit.md). Establish whether the source is an approved identity, a proposed expression inferred from a template, or a newly selected direction before turning observations into rules.

Use the existing approved direction when supplied. If meaning or scope is unsettled, run only the necessary part of [brand-discovery](../brand-discovery/SKILL.md); provisional creative exploration can itself supply discovery evidence. Do not hold every sketch until positioning is final. Honor a requested single direction; use alternatives when they resolve a real choice.

Separate recognition from differentiation, personal fit from audience response, and brand meaning from a preferred aesthetic. A logo, palette or generated brand board alone is not the complete system. Preserve meaningful contextual variation and existing recognizable assets unless there is evidence to replace them. Do not impose archetypes, competitor novelty or universal color psychology as facts.

Route visual execution to `graphic-design-studio:graphic-design`, typography/layout to `composition-and-typography`, visual critique to `graphic-design-review`, motion to the existing Graphic/Video owner, product interfaces to UX, and verbal craft to `writing-quality-studio:voice-and-style`. Use an available palette/Figma/Canva provider when needed; verify its actual tools and access. Documentation does not establish an authenticated connection. If a provider is unavailable, complete the useful local brief or exploration with available tools and identify the remaining native-format gap. Do not represent a brief as an editable Figma file or install a provider merely because a method mentions it.

Finish the requested scope: a direction brief with demonstrable rules and examples, or rendered assets with specialist QA and checked exports. Record changed visual anchors, inspect target-size applications and distinguish technical checks from human acceptance. A prompt is not an image; a mockup is not a deployed brand. Never infer publication, account changes or spending from identity approval.

For a requested early-business identity/touchpoint brief, use the brand variant in the [Founder business methods route](../../references/strategy-methods/founder-business-methods.md) under the approved identity and this design process. Retain its naming checks, voice examples and five-touchpoint brief without treating public searches as legal clearance or generated concepts as finished assets.

For an explicitly selected reusable identity, **Brand Templates** owns the Brand Library catalog and complete kits when that Studio is installed. Use its source-verified `prepare_brand_handoff` only when the tool is actually exposed; inspect the selected kit and evidence before applying it.
