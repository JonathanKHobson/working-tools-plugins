Marketing analytics, attribution, experiment design, cohort analysis, ROI, performance diagnosis and evidence-backed reports. Start from supplied facts and evidence; the current user request controls scope, tone, external actions and acceptance criteria. Distinguish proposed, prepared, sent, scheduled, published and measured. The source's named agents, MCPs, CLI commands and account stores are not automatically installed or authorized. Use the actual host provider and disclose missing execution capability. Strategy methods remain usable with supplied data even when an optional provider is absent.

When this skill owns the whole requested deliverable, finish it directly. Use the strategy operator only when audience, offer or cross-channel priorities remain unresolved; do not cycle through sibling skills. Live source research belongs to Research Studio; channel content and previews to Social Content Studio; image-led craft to Graphic Design; general editorial work to Writing Quality; connector/security work to Master Security and Work Management.

The complete original local method also supplies [Verification](../../references/strategy-methods/verification.md). Load the relevant section for local templates or checks; it is preserved in full.

When evaluating message architectures or reporting cross-channel results, read the [complete channel-claims and measurement method](../../references/strategy-methods/message-cards/channel-claims.md). Specify outcome, source, denominator, attribution window and missingness; separate comprehension and expectation alignment from action metrics. A model critique or click difference is not evidence of causal lift.
