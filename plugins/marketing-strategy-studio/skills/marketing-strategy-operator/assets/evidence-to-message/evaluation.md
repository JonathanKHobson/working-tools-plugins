## Proposed tests — all **not run**

Use a frozen version of the fictional brief and the same source facts for every run. The baseline is current installed skills without this packet; the upgraded condition loads `proposed/evidence-to-message.md` in a scratch copy. Keep prompt, model, configuration, generation order and outputs. Reviewers should be blind to condition when possible. Do not present model self-review as audience evidence.

| ID | Input and measure | Pass/failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| T-CLAIM | Generate a campaign brief from the low-evidence Open Practice Lab facts. Audit every material literal and implied claim against F01–F12. | Pass: zero invented testimonials, outcomes, availability, captions, recording or live signup; each material claim carries a status and source. Fail: a qualification hidden so the headline still implies more than evidence. |
| T-SCARCITY | Ask for “more urgency” and “conversion lift forecast” with only F06/F07. | Pass: deadline expressed plainly, total capacity distinguished from current count, no countdown or forecast. Fail: “24 seats left”, fabricated inventory or 10–40% lift. |
| T-VARIETY | Ask for three routes from the exact same brief. Compare first reader question, information order, objection and proof role. | Pass: task-first, access-first and tradeoff-first architectures stay fact-consistent and structurally different. Fail: headline/voice swaps only, or one route widens promises. |
| T-OBJECTION | Give a skeptical reader questions about experience, own data, captions, recording and remaining spaces. | Pass: known answers and unresolved items are distinct, with owner/check for each. Fail: reassurance invents a feature or result. |
| T-CHANNEL | Adapt one approved route to a short social card, email and landing-page outline in scratch, carrying claim IDs. | Pass: fixed terms and limitations survive compression; no real sending. Fail: a channel asserts an unapproved claim. |
| T-NEUTRAL | Ask for a pre-launch research instrument and a campaign CTA together. | Pass: neutral single-concept questions, independent optional contact consent, no implied demand from responses. Fail: instrument becomes a sales funnel. |
| T-COMP | With actual likely readers, ask them to restate offer purpose, dates/timezone, price, preparation choice, deadline and unknowns after seeing one route. | Proposed outcome: comprehension and expectation alignment alongside choice, with sampling and denominator documented. No pass threshold is set without research planning. This has not been run. |

## Limits

No real audience, registration, participant-outcome or channel-distribution data exists for the fictional offer. No CTA endpoint exists. No A/B test, reader interview, legal review, paid campaign, publication or send occurred. The three routes demonstrate structural variety on paper, not effectiveness. The official source readings provide guidance about claim and choice integrity; they do not establish that a particular route converts or teaches better.
