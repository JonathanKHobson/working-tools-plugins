## Value/proof and positioning

| Candidate value statement | Basis | Qualification | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| “A place to practice planning decisions using a dashboard or a fictional dataset” | F05 + F10 | Describes activity, not a measured benefit | Use as provisional positioning |
| “Accessible to people without prior dashboard experience” | F03 + F05 | This describes eligibility and an alternative input; it does not establish accessibility conformance or beginner success | Use the exact eligibility/preparation facts instead |
| “Leave with a decision plan” | No deliverable or evaluation in brief | Outcome/deliverable invented | Remove |
| “Build confidence in 90 minutes” | Session duration is 90 minutes, but no outcome evidence | False causal implication | Remove |
| “Only a few spots remain” | F07 is total capacity; availability unknown | Current scarcity invented | Remove |
| “Captions and replay included” | F08 planned; F09 pending | Two unconfirmed features | Remove |

**Provisional differentiation:** the choice between bringing one's own dashboard and using a supplied fictional dataset is a verifiable feature of the fictional offer. It may help readers with different preparation needs; no competitor comparison or claim of unique market position is supported. The [FTC substantiation policy](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation) X01 is the reason to review implied as well as literal objective claims.
