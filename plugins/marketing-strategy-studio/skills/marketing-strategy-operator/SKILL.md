# Marketing strategy operator

If asked what Marketing Strategy Studio can do or how to start, use the [human guide](../../README.md) and [task map](../../docs/use-case-map.md) for a short choice among brand discovery/design, strategy, search, campaign, conversion, paid media, and measurement. Name the input and result for one fitting first task. For a specific request, go directly to its owner; do not imply optional Atlas or account providers work until observed.

Choose marketing positioning, audience, offer, goals and channel priorities, or coordinate a strategy spanning several marketing workflows.

For personal or organizational discovery before an offer is settled, use [brand-discovery](../brand-discovery/SKILL.md). For creative territories, identity systems or brand-design briefs, use [brand-design](../brand-design/SKILL.md). These routes preserve noncommercial discovery and contextual expressions; ordinary campaign work does not require a rebrand. When consuming a project brand profile, apply its [scope/status contract](../../references/brand-methods/profile.md) so proposed or retired rules do not become production defaults.

For ICP, customer discovery, target market, audience/persona distinctions or qualification, read [customer fit and buying decisions](../../references/strategy-methods/customer-fit.md) and use its [compact brief](assets/customer-fit-brief.md) at the needed depth. This maintained method governs inherited source assumptions about evidence, personas and scoring. It connects to a brand profile without making customers the authority on personal identity.

Read the task-matching files below in full before applying that procedure. Load only the relevant variant and its required support. These contain the operative methods, examples and templates; this entrypoint does not replace them. A plugin is a package, not a requirement to read every skill.

- [Evidence-to-message decisions](../../references/strategy-methods/evidence-to-message.md) for uncertain material claims, distinct message architectures or a research/marketing boundary; follow only the relevant complete cards.

## Workflow

1. Establish the product, audience, buyer uncertainty, proof, constraints and decision. Use the complete first-move intake and planning workflow.
2. Select one matching full strategy procedure from the source map. Distinguish evidence, assumptions and recommendations; obtain current research where it changes the decision. For each material claim, record exact support, likely implied reading, qualification and ready/verify/remove status before it enters copy or a channel brief. Choose message architectures by reader decision and information order, not headline variation alone.
3. Turn the chosen strategy into an executable brief with owner, deliverables, constraints and measurement. Route SEO, campaigns, paid media, conversion, lifecycle, measurement or creative production directly to its specialist when that is the whole task.

## Deliver and verify

Return the usable strategy/brief and actual next action. Preserve the full relevant templates and checks; do not force unrelated channel procedures into the model’s context.

## Example boundary

Good: choose a channel using actual audience evidence and a testable decision. Bad: generate a full paid-ad plan when the user only asked to clarify positioning.

Read the selected upstream procedure and required support in full. External accounts, spend, posting and publishing use the actual provider under current authorization. Keep brand facts, profiles and analytics in the external project.

## Scoped Atlas reference

For local marketing concepts, frameworks, activities or source definitions, consult [the scoped Atlas library](../../references/atlas-library.md) only when `atlas-marketing` is actually exposed and a scoped lookup succeeds. Use its relevant-only search and full-source selection before adaptation; human browsing and assistant retrieval share the same source records. Otherwise continue with the complete local methods and state that Atlas was not consulted.

When the deliverable includes copy or a writing brief, use `writing-quality-studio:voice-and-style` if installed and exposed; otherwise use the complete local [copy-editing](../../libraries/marketingskills/skills/copy-editing/SKILL.md) method and current user voice preferences. If available, use it for selectable Writing Voice Lab templates and contextual blends. Honor named choices; otherwise vary suitable structure/openings by audience, awareness, platform, proof and purpose. V01 Decision first, V05 Skeptical investigator, V09 Plain-language service, V15 Spoken invitation, V22 Reflective critic and V23 Conversational micro-essay offer different mechanisms. Use ethical relevance, honest curiosity and supported proof; no invented scarcity, urgency, consensus, guarantees or promised conversion lift. Keep the current user’s approved brand identity and voice preferences stable across outreach and articles; do not assume a named person’s tone for a new recipient. Template choice does not authorize sending or publishing.

For startup idea pressure tests, competitor gaps, business plans, early operating assumptions or unit-economics scenarios, use the [complete Founder business methods route](../../references/strategy-methods/founder-business-methods.md). It selects one of eleven incorporated procedures and requires the governing evidence/host adapter; synthetic buyers do not establish demand or viability.
