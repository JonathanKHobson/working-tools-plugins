# Local SVG draft contract

The SVG helper makes editable audit drafts when a user requests a local, provider-free artifact. Native image generation owns photographic ads. The helper's 1080 × 1440 viewBox is exactly 3:4. Its supplied raster references are normalized to embedded, metadata-stripped PNG pixels; vector text, shape panels and clipping paths remain editable. Valid embedded ICC is converted to sRGB before output. The unbranded palette is only a neutral draft surface.

The optional product label sits at x=64, baseline y=94; the authored headline starts at y=164. The photo field begins at x=64, y=260 and has a 952 × 760 viewport. Raster inputs are contained without stretching. The third creative may show two cited target images side by side when its hypothesis cites at least two; otherwise it uses one full image. A clear role label below the image at y=1075 identifies a personalization preview as a preview. The authored caption begins at y=1140. An optional action panel sits at x=64, y=1302. The helper rejects copy that does not fit; it never shortens evidence or ad text.

H1, H2 and H3 map in order to three authored hypotheses in the brief. The images used for each draft come from that hypothesis's observation references, not a fixed product/preview order. Consumer SVGs do not display internal H numbers or evidence IDs. The manifest and review page retain those IDs, subjective interpretation confidence and rationale, alternative explanation, concept extension status, proposed test and source hashes. A local draft cannot establish visual-observation truth, market demand, ad performance, product availability, rights or platform approval. Inspect the full-size renders before any downstream use.
