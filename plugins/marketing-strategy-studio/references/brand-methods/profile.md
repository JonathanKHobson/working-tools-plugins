# A brand profile that preserves decision status

A brand profile is a compact, reusable account of who/what is being presented, in which context, what is promised and supported, how it is expressed and which decisions remain open. It is neither a personality test nor a requirement to turn all interests into offers. The project owns one authoritative record or a linked family when separate scopes truly need it. Do not introduce a database or automatic personal-data capture to store Markdown.

## Minimum useful contents

For a person with several roles, use this as the core record. Link [audience/customer-fit briefs](../strategy-methods/customer-fit.md) per relevant context or offer; do not flatten employers, peers, learners, clients and beneficiaries into one ideal customer. These linked records inform positioning and expression hypotheses while the person retains identity authority.

Use the [blank profile](../../skills/brand-discovery/assets/brand-profile-template.md). Retain only sections that support current work, with explicit unknowns rather than invented filler.

1. Subject, decision owner, purpose, audiences/contexts, version and status.
2. Plain-language introduction; contribution or promise; proof and qualifications. Keep aspirations separate; a commercial promise/offer can be not applicable.
3. Recognized themes, values expressed as behaviors, tensions and counterexamples.
4. Audience relationship, relevant alternatives, useful differences and recognition cues.
5. Voice behaviors with attributable positive/negative examples; tone/context range. Link full voice work rather than copying another owner's entire system.
6. Visual/sensory direction and asset pointers; invariants, flexible choices and exceptions. No invented hexadecimal values or typography merely to populate fields.
7. Relationship among roles, projects and offers, including connection and separation rules.
8. Disclosure boundaries, rights/reuse permissions, accessibility needs, applicable AI-media choices and factual claim limits.
9. Decision register, unresolved questions, test results, and triggers to revisit.

## Rule-level contract

For a consequential claim or identity rule, record:

`id | statement | kind | source/evidence IDs | interpretation author | status | applicable audience/context | exceptions | chosen/approved by and date | review trigger | supersedes`

Kinds include observation, interpretation, aspiration, factual claim and design choice. Statuses distinguish proposed, exploratory, selected-for-test, approved-for-scope, rejected and superseded. Evidence strength and approval status are separate: three sources do not automatically make a rule true, and human approval of a slogan does not substantiate its factual claim.

Progression is not automatic. An explicit human selection can establish a creative choice within its stated scope. A test can supply observations; it cannot approve identity on the person's behalf. A later assistant, a saved file, a high model score or repeated reuse cannot promote status. Capture direct selection evidence without requiring redundant confirmation when it already exists. Retire outdated rules with a pointer and reason; preserve history rather than silently erasing or reactivating it.

## Consumer behavior

Load only the relevant scope and linked approved examples. Use approved-for-scope rules for production; use selected-for-test rules only for that test. Treat proposed or exploratory content as options. Exclude rejected/superseded rules. If metadata is absent, say the guide is unverified and use it as contextual input rather than authority. Current user instructions govern the task; do not ask the person to reapprove unchanged routine work.

When an unresolved choice affects the requested output, offer a provisional variant or ask the single consequential question. Do not silently apply an AI recommendation as the answer. Missing unrelated sections do not block an ordinary post, bio or mockup. Prefer source pointers and the minimum permitted excerpt over storing raw private corpora in the profile. Keep confidential source excerpts out of public profile exports, even when the underlying idea is approved.

## Handoff and maintenance

For a reusable guide or kit, use [guide and kit assembly](guide-and-kit.md) to preserve source authority, scoped rules and distinct human, machine and asset handoffs.

Give collaborators the approved scope, purpose, claims/evidence, exemplars, exceptions, asset paths/rights, deliverable and acceptance criteria. An asset manifest identifies the editable source, export, version, license, owner, intended context and superseded version. A public guide should link only shareable evidence, not private interviews.

Review when an offer, audience, relationship, source fact or design direction changes; do not mandate quarterly re-interviews. Keep updates proportional. Test whether a collaborator can create a second coherent artifact without merely copying the first or needing another discovery pass. Store rollout state and publication receipts separately from creative approval.
