# Research basis and limits

These are source pointers and an original practice synthesis, not a validated universal branding intervention. Recheck changing platform/tool claims before use. Academic findings, practitioner methods, agency cases and software prompts provide different kinds of evidence; do not count them as equivalent studies.

| Source | Useful contribution | Application limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Gorbatov, Khapova & Lysova, 2018 systematic review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02238/full) | Distinguishes personal branding processes and outcomes and surveys a fragmented interdisciplinary literature | Does not validate these worksheets or promise career success; personal identity is broader than a promotional practice |
| [Ibarra, Working Identity](https://herminiaibarra.com/working-identity-book/) | Experimentation, relationships and interpretation can inform professional identity development | Author's account of career-transition research; applying it to creative brand probes is an adaptation, not a personality instrument |
| [Marwick & boyd, context collapse](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/i-tweet-honestly-i-tweet-passionately-twitter-users-context-collapse-and-the-imagined-audience/) | Different audiences can meet in the same social setting | Twitter-era qualitative research informs contextual questions, not current platform performance or guaranteed audience separation |
| [American Marketing Association: branding](https://www.ama.org/topics/brand-and-branding/) | Identifying features and distinctions among brand, personal and corporate branding | A useful definitional entry point, not a complete account of personhood or a discovery protocol |
| [April Dunford on differentiated value](https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/the-no-differentiation-illusion) | Relevant value depends on actual alternatives; a combination can matter even if individual parts are not unique | Practitioner product-positioning argument, adapted conditionally for an offer; not compulsory personal identity framing |
| [Ehrenberg-Bass: distinctive assets](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/brands-of-distinction) | Recognition and competing associations need separate attention; new assets should not be assumed famous | Institutional practitioner account; a small prototype review cannot estimate population-level fame or uniqueness |
| [Design Council: Double Diamond](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/) | Explore and focus, then develop and test; iteration can change the brief | Design-process guidance, not a required waterfall or a universal effectiveness claim |
| [Pentagram: MIT Media Lab identity](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-media-lab) | Example of a coherent system supporting multiple related identities | Agency case, not causal evidence; use the structural question rather than copying its forms |
| [Mailchimp: voice and tone](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/) | Stable voice can coexist with context-sensitive tone | Organization-specific editorial guidance; do not import Mailchimp's personality as the user's identity |
| [W3C: text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) and [non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) | Evaluate real text/control pairings and the specific scope of logo exemptions | Normative criteria and technical explanations need application-level checks; a palette alone cannot establish conformance |

## Source-to-method interpretation

The discovery, profile, design and evaluation procedures join these concepts into original operational methods. The layer model, decision register, profile statuses, probe sequence and workload choices are local synthesis. They are not quotations from an academic scale or evidence of a measured business effect.

External skill inspection also informed questions about evidence attribution, scoped records and component handoff. Selected source snapshots were pinned and reviewed outside active discovery; their agents, connectors, scripts, defaults and installation commands were not imported:

- [Tribe Brand Voice, pinned source](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/tree/8444efcd48f7012f09797778a36a33e73d0861f4/partner-built/brand-voice): considered source attribution and contextual tone. Do not inherit source-count confidence, automatic application of unresolved recommendations, or unconditional preference for session-generated guidance.
- [magnus919 brand-designer, pinned source](https://github.com/magnus919/agent-skills/tree/96fbe0780a2fc2b7d063b613bfc1a5767e11b5d7/brand-designer): considered component/handoff coverage. No CLI/templates copied; scope by actual use rather than team size. Generated images do not guarantee exact colors or logos.
- [social-media-skills brand-profile, pinned source](https://github.com/social-media-skills/skills/tree/6e30eeb2f6736bda8683b6bbaa674af3641d7945/skills/brand-profile): considered reusable project context. Do not inherit forced profile setup before ordinary content, universal quarterly expiry, required controversial positioning or a vendor publishing dependency.

Brandfetch, Figma, Canva and palette providers can help acquire or produce assets when a task needs them. None discovers an authentic personal identity by itself. Brandfetch's current API-backed lookup uses authenticated services and some operations consume credits; it is an optional future acquisition capability, not a prerequisite. Prefer actual already-available tools and verified access; never treat a connector declaration as a working service.

Private research packets and current project decisions remain in the owning project. They informed this generic synthesis but are not redistributed in the plugin or treated as user approval.
