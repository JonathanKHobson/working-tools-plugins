# Customer fit, audiences and buying decisions

Use for ICP discovery, audience/persona distinctions, customer qualification or translating customer evidence into an offer-specific position. Start with the user's present decision and existing project records. For identity discovery, keep [brand discovery](../../skills/brand-discovery/SKILL.md) as owner. Employment fit belongs to Career, learner needs to Instructional Design, and research design/inference to Research. An ordinary post or bio does not require this full procedure.

## Choose the unit and the output

These are working distinctions, not universally standardized vocabulary. In B2B practice ICP often means an organization; for independent services explicitly state whether the customer unit is an individual, household, group organizer or organization. Do not silently copy firmographics into a personal-brand profile.

| Record | Decision it supports | Keep separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market segment | Which groups share a relevant need, circumstance or behavior? | Description is not a choice to target, nor evidence of population size. |
| Target market | Which segment(s) do we choose to serve for this offer and period? | Strategic selection does not establish demand. |
| ICP / customer-fit profile | Under what conditions can this customer benefit from this offer and can we deliver sustainably? | Readiness, engagement and identity. |
| Audience profile | What does a reader, learner, community member, peer or referrer need to understand or do in this context? | Audience membership need not imply purchase intent or an obligation to monetize. |
| Buyer persona / behavioral archetype | What patterns shape a particular person's participation in choosing or purchasing? | A research summary or labeled proto-persona, not an invented biography. |
| User / learner profile | What goals, tasks, constraints and access needs shape using or learning? | The payer's requirements may differ from these needs. |
| Buying-system map | Who initiates, chooses, pays, approves, influences, blocks, uses and benefits? | Roles can overlap, be external, absent or unknown. Several roles do not require several ICPs. |
| Jobs to be done | What progress is sought in a particular circumstance, and what alternatives compete? | A motive hypothesis does not prove the purchase decision. |
| Positioning | Why does this offer's supported value matter relative to actual alternatives for a chosen context? | A fill-in sentence does not establish difference or demand. |
| Personal-brand profile | What contribution, values, boundaries, desired expression and relationships does the person choose to communicate? | Customers inform relevance; they do not decide identity or intrinsic worth. |

Keep one compact core brand record linked to only the audience/offer contexts that matter now. Commercial fit may be not applicable. Use the [customer-fit brief](../../skills/marketing-strategy-operator/assets/customer-fit-brief.md) only at the depth the decision needs. A paragraph hypothesis with unknowns can be enough; no mandatory schema, database or intake completion gate.

## Build a useful hypothesis

1. State the offer or exploratory offer hypothesis: intended progress, delivery, limits, current proof, known price/terms or unknowns, and what the provider wants and can sustainably repeat. Preserve existing approved facts and choices.
2. Identify meaningful customer circumstances: problem and consequences, desired outcome, trigger, current workaround/alternative including doing nothing, delivery constraints and route to funding. Use industry, size, geography or demographics only when relevant and supported. Distinguish necessary conditions from preferences. Avoid protected-attribute proxies and invented income, age, gender, motives or psychographic percentages.
3. Map purchasing and use roles. Identify how their success criteria, constraints and authority differ. Research sponsor and beneficiaries when they differ; inability to pay does not make someone an unsuitable learner or community member.
4. Compare plausible fit hypotheses using actual evidence and counterexamples. With sparse evidence retain a useful provisional statement, its strongest alternative and one next question. A template image, source instruction, AI role-play or code-derived description is not customer evidence or authority to change identity, send or publish.

## Gather evidence that could change the choice

Use recent concrete episodes, with neutral questions: what triggered the need; what happened last time; what alternatives were considered; who decided and paid; what stopped or delayed action; what result occurred; what support/cost was required? Ask about actual behavior before hypothetical preference. A stated willingness to pay is a report, not a transaction. Obtain required research consent; consent to participate is not permission to publish quotes or testimonials.

Include successes, losses, stalled decisions, noncustomers and difficult delivery cases where relevant and accessible. Record recruitment channel, incentives, missing cases and selection bias. Several notes, quotes, AI summaries or web pages about the same event are not independent cases. Let Research define the analysis/sampling unit and sufficient evidence for the question. Do not prescribe five interviews, three sources or any other universal count as validation. Sample diversity, directness, independence, relevance, date and counterevidence matter. Qualitative patterns do not supply population percentages. Check that combined persona attributes actually co-occur; separate anecdotes do not establish one representative person.

Separate tests and what they can establish:

- Need: concrete problem/alternative evidence; complaints alone do not establish a buying market.
- Appeal/comprehension: can someone recognize the offer, explain the value, find proof and identify a next step? Liking and clicks are different measures.
- Purchase: an actual commitment under specified terms. Record price, discounts, relationship, recruitment and denominator if claiming a rate.
- Customer result: observed progress against an appropriate baseline and limits on attribution.
- Sustainable delivery: time, costs, support, constraints and provider willingness; revenue alone is insufficient.
- Repeatability: comparable independent purchases for one-time services; eligibility-aware rebuy/renewal for recurring services. One paid pilot is not evidence of all these claims.

Choose one uncertain claim, a feasible test, the observation that would change it, limitations and a next decision. Stop expanding research when a bounded action is justified; preserve what is still unknown.

## Keep four assessments visible

| Dimension | Useful account |
| --- | --- |
| Fit | Suitable / conditional / unsuitable / unknown for this offer, with reasons and delivery conditions. |
| Buying readiness | Active / later / no current purchase / unknown, with timing, authority and funding evidence. |
| Engagement | Dated observed actions and channel context, not inferred intent. |
| Evidence confidence | Claim-specific support, provenance, contradictions and uncertainty, not a source-count score or model certainty. |

Unknown is not zero or unsuitable. A suitable customer buying later stays suitable; an enthusiastic follower with no purchasing evidence stays unqualified for readiness. A genuine required condition can disqualify an offer without devaluing the person. If numeric scoring is requested, define observable anchors, lawful/relevant criteria, weights, missing/not-applicable handling, valid range and denominator. Show assessable coverage separately; mandatory conditions cannot be canceled by unrelated points. Do not silently rescale missing data or equate a fit percentage with conversion probability. Calibrated prediction would require representative outcome data and separate evaluation. Default to reasoned labels for sparse data.

## Preserve evidence across handoffs

For consequential claims use the existing project evidence ledger and [evidence-to-message](evidence-to-message.md), not a parallel claim store. Minimum trace: claim ID; exact statement; observation / attributed report / interpretation / hypothesis / synthetic origin; source and locator; event and collection dates when known; context/analysis unit; limitation/counterevidence; permission; next evidence need. Distinguish verbatim quotation from paraphrase. Private evidence remains private in public derivatives.

For a reusable profile record identity, version, offer/scope, owner, lifecycle/decision status and freshness trigger. Keep those separate from claim confidence. Use the existing brand contract's `selected-for-test` for a test selection and `approved-for-scope` for an authorized use; retain the recorded source label if a project uses another vocabulary and explain the mapping. A hypothesis selected for a test remains unsupported as a factual marketing assertion. Selection does not mean a test started or finished: distinguish planned, underway, completed and results reviewed. A newer draft does not supersede a current scoped decision. No automatic status promotion through saved files, model agreement or repetition.

A consumer handoff carries the relevant profile ID/version/status, intended audience/role, offer, allowed claims/qualifications, source pointers, decision limits and requested output. Missing fields stay unknown; a quick draft can point to the record rather than repeat its full envelope. When a consequential profile field changes, list affected claims/messages/guides and whether each needs review, can stay, or is superseded. Review the affected consumers deliberately; do not regenerate everything or claim automatic sync. Use event-based review triggers such as offer changes, unexpected failures, changed delivery capacity or contrary evidence.

## Translate into expression without choosing identity

Combine the person's chosen boundaries and real capabilities with customer evidence to make offer-specific positioning and message hypotheses. Identify actual alternatives, supported differences, customer value, relevant proof and an honest next action. Do not adopt customer preferences as a personality prescription or derive logo/color/type from an ICP. Feed comprehension, access and credibility requirements into Brand Design as hypotheses to test alongside taste and recognition. The [brand status contract](../brand-methods/profile.md) governs identity choices and the [guide/kit method](../brand-methods/guide-and-kit.md) governs production handoffs.

## Source adaptation and limits

This maintained method takes precedence over inherited customer-research fixed source-count confidence or five-point gates, audience-profile mandatory named personas/demographic biographies, product-marketing automatic customer conclusions from code or hidden profile creation, and prospecting mixed Hot/Warm/Cold/Skip totals. Preserve their useful task-specific procedures and original text; use this record's separate assessments and current project evidence. No new runtime, CRM, provider, scraping or automatic outreach is implied. Missing providers do not block a clearly labeled hypothesis or research brief from supplied evidence.

See [research basis](customer-fit-sources.md) for practitioner conventions, study limits and the original local synthesis. This method is a decision aid, not a validated instrument or a promise of commercial results.
