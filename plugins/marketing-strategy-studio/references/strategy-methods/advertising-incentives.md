## Conditional Ad Grants source errata

The preserved digital-marketing-pro nonprofit profile's universal “$2.00 max bid cap,” suggested 3–8% CTR range and implication that all grant budget must be used are not executable program requirements. [Current official account management policy](https://support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/117827?hl=en), retrieved 2026-10-01, requires conversion-based Smart bidding for campaigns in accounts created on or after April 22, 2019 unless using Smart campaigns, and sets an account-level monthly 5% CTR requirement for accounts not exclusively using Smart Campaigns. Meaningful conversion/tracking requirements depend on account date and strategy. Do not substitute a blanket $2 cap for this policy; verify exact current bidding restrictions for the actual account. The FAQ already confirms there is no requirement to spend the full monthly grant. Preserve the original profile while routing any grant plan through these current official policies.
