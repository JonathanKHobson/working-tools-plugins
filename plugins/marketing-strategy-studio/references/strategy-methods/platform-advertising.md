# Platform advertising lanes

Read after [advertising readiness](advertising-readiness.md), with only the selected preserved full method. This is a planning, execution and verification map, not a promise that any account or connector exists. Refresh official interface/spec/policy claims before account execution. Current capability evidence belongs in the project record, not in this durable method.

## Shared working record

Capture platform/account identity, account timezone/currency, objective, campaign/ad-group/ad IDs where known, asset/destination IDs, source hashes, approved cash ceiling, duration, credit record, audience, exclusions, billing/stop behavior, conversion/event definition, attribution and current authority. Preserve the before/after settings and readback. Do not place credentials or unredacted payment data in artifacts.

## Google Search Ads

Use for demonstrated search intent matching the resource or offer. Select the preserved [ads method](../../libraries/marketingskills/skills/ads/SKILL.md) and its targeting/copy/tracking support, or [paid-advertising Google reference](../../libraries/digital-marketing-pro/skills/paid-advertising/google-ads.md) for the requested depth. Their historic API examples and quantitative thresholds require current verification; source MCP checkmarks are not host capabilities.

Build one coherent intent cluster, truthful search copy and a relevant authorized destination. Review actual search terms before proposing exclusions. For free instruction, do not blindly exclude “free,” “how to” or “DIY” from preserved commercial templates. Define the event and value honestly, specify keywords/match strategy and landing-page continuity, and verify current ad limits/specifications. Separate brand versus discovery traffic where it changes interpretation.

For authorized execution inspect budget type, dates, network/location settings, bidding, conversion actions, auto-apply recommendations, credit/billing state and change/pause capability. [Google spending limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637?hl=en) explain that for most average-daily-budget campaigns billing can reach twice the daily average, with a monthly limit of 30.4 times that average. Verify exceptions, current campaign type and budget changes; choose controls that actually fit the approved total cash cap.

Read performance by search intent, clicks, costs, verified conversion/event quality and attribution window. Do not use a universal CTR/CPA threshold or treat Quality Score as measured learning value. Pause/read back the exact campaign and reconcile billed costs.

## YouTube paid promotion

Choose explicitly between Google Ads video campaigns and YouTube Studio Promote; neither is organic distribution. Use current [YouTube Promote instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16869935?hl=en-8) for that interface. Promote requires a linked Google Ads account with billing; it has a budget/end date and its own pause control. Its paid subscribers/watch time do not count toward YouTube Partner Program eligibility. Interpret paid and organic traffic separately. [Official Promote FAQ](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12199105?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en) states that promotions created on YouTube are managed only on YouTube, not through Google Ads. It separately defines Follow-on Views as subsequent organic views after exposure to promoted content; for Audience Growth promotions this metric counts toward YPP. Do not confuse these with paid-acquired watch hours or subscribers.

Google Ads supports distinct video reach/views and other objectives; verify the selected campaign/format/placement rather than treating all video ads as equivalent. Confirm whether the actual credit covers that account/product before planning its use. Ad Grants is search-only, not a credit pool for YouTube video ads.

Prepare a source-faithful preview or demonstration with independent learner value and a relevant CTA; verify ad video/thumbnail/text, rights, mobile placement and destination. Confirm objective and metric definition: reach/impressions, format-defined views/view rate, costs, clicks/website visits, subscriptions attributed by the platform, and downstream learning where observable. Do not compare ad view rate with an organic thumbnail CTR or infer organic recommendation gains from paid delivery.

Account handoff must state the exact Studio versus Ads Manager surface, linked account, chosen objective, video ID, audience, total budget/dates, credit record, metrics and pause instructions. Verify active/delivering and paused separately. Credit presence and prepared video do not establish activation.

## Meta: Facebook and Instagram

Select the preserved [Meta full reference](../../libraries/digital-marketing-pro/skills/paid-advertising/meta-ads.md) or the ads method's selected setup/tracking support. Recheck dated API versions, legacy audience/Aggregated Event Measurement advice, exact specs and unsupported claims about scaling, attribution or watch behavior. Never apply a recommended daily minimum when it exceeds the approved cap.

Choose objective and desired learner/commercial action; identify the intended Facebook Page/Instagram identity and placements rather than assuming both platforms are covered. Match creative to feed/Reels/Stories mobile presentation, with captions and safe-area QA; preserve the truthful teaching promise and landing-page continuity. Record current placement availability and restrictions.

For authorized account execution verify the business/ad account, identity access, payment/credit scope, current objective/event, privacy basis for Pixel/CAPI or audience data, attribution, deduplication, dates, spending-limit semantics, review status and exact pause capability. Budget controls and credit conditions vary; verify official/current account terms instead of promising that a displayed daily budget prevents additional charges.

Report reach/frequency, impressions, link clicks versus all clicks, landing-page views where available, verified downstream actions, costs and click/view attribution. Do not sum Meta attributed outcomes with other platforms without reconciliation. Check destination/tracking/billing before recommending creative changes; provide manual pause handoff when no genuine provider is available, and require account readback before reporting paused.

## Reddit Ads

Existing generic paid-media methods supply planning/testing/budgeting, but a complete Reddit-specific execution lane must verify current official [ad types](https://www.business.reddit.com/advertise/ad-types) and [billing/credits](https://business.reddithelp.com/s/article/Advertiser-credits). Select the ad objective, creative format and feed/conversation placement that fit the lesson/resource. Record interest/community/keyword targeting actually offered by the account; ads targeted toward communities are not organic posts or endorsements by moderators.

Use candid native copy, a clearly disclosed promoted resource, evidence-backed teaching payoff and a useful destination. Plan comment handling where the format permits it. Organic subreddit sharing belongs to Social, requires current community rules and publication authority, and must not be disguised as spontaneous learner praise.

For authorized execution verify account identity, currency, billing method, exact promotion/qualifying costs, dates, actual daily/lifetime budget semantics and overdelivery/billing terms, review status, objective/event, privacy basis for Pixel/CAPI, attribution and pause path. Do not assume a coupon prevents charges or run ads simply to unlock it.

Assess format/objective-specific impressions, billed click/view definitions, spend, destination visits and verified learning/registration outcomes. Community fit is a hypothesis until observed. Compare same-window data and preserve comment/feedback context. Read back the relevant paused objects and reconcile pending costs before closeout.
