# Customer-fit research basis and limits

Primary publisher pages checked 2026-10-08. Definitions vary across practice. The linked customer-fit method is an original local synthesis, not a psychometric instrument, validated prediction model, mandatory schema or claim of commercial effectiveness.

| Source | Supports | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Salesforce: Ideal Customer Profiles](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/ideal-customer-profile/) | Common B2B distinction between suitable companies and people involved in buying. | Vendor/practitioner convention. Extending the customer unit to individuals, households or organizers is an explicit adaptation for services, not a universal definition. |
| [HubSpot: lead scoring](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/scoring/understand-the-lead-scoring-tool) | Separates fit properties and engagement events; combined scores remain configurable. | Product documentation does not establish the predictive validity of a particular homemade score. Separate readiness and claim confidence are our method choices. |
| [NN/g: three persona types](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/persona-types/) | Distinguishes assumption-led proto-personas, qualitative personas and statistical personas. | Practitioner guidance; illustrative sample counts are not universal confidence thresholds. A profile image does not measure population prevalence. |
| [Christensen Institute: Jobs to Be Done](https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/) | Progress sought in circumstances and the forces shaping decisions. | A useful explanatory lens, not proof of a specific customer's motives or causality. |
| [April Dunford: positioning quickstart](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning) | Alternatives, capabilities, value, customer context and category; cautions about filling a statement without making the decisions. | Practitioner framework, not a guaranteed conversion intervention. |
| [GOV.UK: plan user research](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/plan-user-research-for-your-service) | Decision-focused rounds, relevant methods and inclusive user recruitment. | Service-design guidance; do not inherit an organizational cadence or participant count as a rule for every project. |
| [Strategyzer: test value propositions and business models](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/ways-to-test-your-value-proposition-and-business-model) | Behavioral tests complement what people say; observation can affect behavior. | Practitioner examples, not authorization for deceptive sales tests or evidence that one pilot proves a market. |
| [Chapman et al.: Quantitative Evaluation of Personas as Information](https://quantuxbook.com/papers/REPRINT-HFES08-chapman-love-milham-elrif-alford.pdf) | Six survey datasets and simulated datasets illustrate declining representation as randomly assembled persona attributes accumulate. | 2008 analysis of persona-like descriptions; it does not show that every research-grounded persona fails. Check joint support for the attributes actually used. |
| [Gorbatov, Khapova and Lysova: systematic review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02238/full) | Personal branding spans identity, expression and perceptions across a heterogeneous literature. | A 2018 synthesis does not validate this local template or establish any individual's traits or best market. |

The user-supplied ICP Marketing Studio handoff (2026-10-08) and five template screenshots informed the gap review. Their implementation prompts, personal examples, synthetic data, schemas and scripts remain inactive project evidence. No upstream runtime was imported. The active method retains relevant concepts through original prose and existing claim/profile contracts. The source packet's reported contract tests are not evidence that local methods were exercised.

Review consequential facts when applying the method. Retain dated actual customer evidence separately from public market context and method literature. Read only the material needed for the present decision.
