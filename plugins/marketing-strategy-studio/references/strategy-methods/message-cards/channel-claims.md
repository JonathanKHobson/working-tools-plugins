# Adopted marketing decision method

Integrated 2026-09-30. Read this complete card only for its actual trigger. Historical coverage describes the pre-integration audit; evidence IDs resolve in the accompanying source ledger. Example dates, capacity and activity describe the fictional Open Practice Lab, not a current offer. These are conditional methods and test proposals, not established conversion effects or a requirement to run every card.

## M05 — One claim set across channels and measurement

- **Trigger and task:** adapting approved strategy to a landing page, email, social card or partner blurb without drift.
- **Current coverage/owner:** L05/L06 and channel rules already route campaign, lifecycle and measurement; Marketing Strategy Operator owns canonical claims, channel owners own packaging. L02 requires source, denominator and review date for metrics. R02 demonstrates linked claim IDs and explicit status, but no tool import is proposed.
- **Mechanism:** a channel may compress a sentence but cannot widen its promise. Every adaptation must retain material conditions and point to a full information source once one exists.
- **Decision:** assign each ready claim an ID; carry IDs with each channel variant. Preserve name, dates, timezone, price, deadline, preparation choice and unresolved access facts. Define one primary decision per channel. For a future campaign, record exposures or delivered messages, unique readers if measurable, registrations with denominator, and a separate comprehension/expectation check; do not mix these into one “conversion” number or infer causality without a design.
- **Example:** a short social card may say “Free, two online sessions, 20 and 27 October 2027, 6–7:30pm Phoenix” and point to a future full detail page. It may not say “recording included” or “few seats left.”
- **Counterexample:** the landing page says “practice”, the email says “master dashboards”, and the social card says “limited places” while all share one source brief.
- **Conditions/tradeoffs:** a channel with too little space should omit a weaker benefit, not an important limitation. If the real CTA is unavailable, all examples remain placeholders. Channel effectiveness is unknown until measured on real distribution.
- **Effort and test:** small to medium. Proposed cross-channel diff checks factual fields and claim IDs; a later test reports denominator and audience context. Failure: any channel contains an untracked material claim.
