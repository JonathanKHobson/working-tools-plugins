## M03 — Select architecture by decision context

- **Trigger and task:** one offer must be explained to readers with different questions, or a request for “three options” risks producing three headline swaps.
- **Current coverage/owner:** L09–L10 provide copy formulas and templates; L01 chooses positioning/channel; lane04 owns voice and line craft. This card owns strategic sequence. Kyle's U03 rhetorical framing is a lens, not a measured effect. X07 requires actual user evidence before a route is called audience-validated.
- **Mechanism:** structure changes which question the message answers first. A route is materially distinct if its opening question, information order, objection and proof role differ while offer facts remain fixed.
- **Decision:** choose one of the proposed, unvalidated example structures: **task-first** (what decision will I practice?), **access-first** (can I participate without tools/experience?), or **tradeoff-first** (what is known, pending and not promised?). Record the audience assumption behind the choice, the alternate route, the channel context and the comprehension check. Do not demand a social-proof, guarantee or urgency section when facts do not support one.
- **Example:** the three complete Open Practice Lab routes in `examples/three-message-architectures.md` retain dates, cost, preparation choice and unknowns while changing the decision path.
- **Counterexample:** changing “Practice dashboard decisions” to “Make better dashboard decisions” and calling it a new architecture. The second also introduces an outcome implication.
- **Conditions/tradeoffs:** a familiar audience may need a shorter task-first note; a cold audience may need the purpose and limits up front. A longer tradeoff route may reduce ambiguity but cost attention. No route is declared the winner without reader evidence.
- **Effort and test:** medium. Proposed blinded comprehension test: ask likely readers to restate the offer, limits and next step for each route; log misunderstandings without using clicks as a proxy. Failure: route difference is only tone or layout.
