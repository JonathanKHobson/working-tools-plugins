# Founder business decisions

Use this route for a startup idea, early business plan, launch preparation, pricing/unit-economics scenario or a requested synthetic objection exercise. Read the [governing adapter](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/ADAPTER.md), then the complete selected method and its support. Each method can run alone. Do not force the eleven-stage sequence onto a narrow task.

| Decision or deliverable | Complete method and required support |
| --- | --- |
| Pressure-test an idea through three frameworks | [Board](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-board/SKILL.md) and [lenses](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-board/lenses.md) |
| Map competitors, substitutes, complaints and a proposed gap | [Competition](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-competitors/SKILL.md) |
| Generate synthetic objection/wording hypotheses | [Consumer exercise](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-consumer/SKILL.md), `panel.py`, customer JSON and pitch examples in that folder |
| Compare price sensitivity, competitor prices and margins | [Pricing](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-pricing/SKILL.md) and adjacent `van_westendorp.py` |
| Build and cost an offer stack | [Offer](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-offer/SKILL.md) with the Offers lens |
| Model contribution, ramp, cash, break-even and sensitivity | [CFO scenario](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-cfo/SKILL.md), `unit_economics.py` and `example.json` |
| Turn evidence into positioning and a dated campaign | [Marketing](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-marketing/SKILL.md) |
| Develop a requested name/voice/touchpoint brief | [Brand](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-brand/SKILL.md), under existing brand-discovery/design authority |
| Prepare day-one operations, suppliers, SOPs and risk | [Operations](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-ops/SKILL.md) |
| Prepare a real test, countdown and launch run sheet | [Launch](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-launch/SKILL.md) |
| Compile the sections, scenario checks and one-pager | [Business plan](../../libraries/founder-business-methods/skills/founder-plan/SKILL.md) and adjacent `compile.py` |

Synthetic buyers generate hypotheses. Their rates are neither measured demand nor conversion upper bounds; use 5–12 sequential cards by default. The compiler's scenario label is conditional on inputs and cannot establish viability. The adapter governs these distinctions across all eleven methods.

Marketing owns the full retained library and commercial strategy. Research owns real customer studies and evidence synthesis; Work Management owns project execution/dependencies; Solo Service Operations owns invoices, payments, bookkeeping and client records. Route a genuine specialist task to the available owner with the relevant complete method and source inputs. These handoffs do not require duplicate installations or authorize external actions. Keep current project outputs outside the plugin.
