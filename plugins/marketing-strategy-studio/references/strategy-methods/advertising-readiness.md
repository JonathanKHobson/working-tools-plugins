# Advertising readiness and incentive evaluation

Use this complete procedure for promoting free education, public videos or a paid offer; evaluating ad grants/credits; deciding whether a first paid campaign is ready; or working under a cash cap. Preserve the selected full advertising/creative method after these gates. Do not require a brand store, tracking installation or ad account just to answer a strategy question.

## 1. Extract the decision and cash boundary

Read current project facts and supplied evidence. Record the promoted asset and destination, intended audience/geography, learning or commercial objective, meaningful action, timeframe, execution authority and known account state. Choose one primary success measure. Free access does not imply nonprofit status, a financial return, or permission to advertise.

Record cash cap and currency separately from credit face value. Also record whether the cap includes taxes, fees, setup/management costs and deposits; whether a card or payment profile is allowed; whether any charge, hold, automatic continuation or new account is forbidden; and the approved account/platforms/time window. An unknown cash cap permits analysis and preparation, not spend. A zero-cash constraint rejects positive qualifying spend even when the resulting credit exceeds it.

Use four separate lanes:

| Lane | Entry criterion | Concrete result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organic promotion | No paid media needed | Bounded distribution/packaging plan routed to existing Social, Search, Video and Graphics owners |
| No-cash grant or unconditional incentive | Official terms plus eligible organization/account evidence and a verified no-charge execution path | Eligibility assessment and prepared plan; activation/redemption only if authorized |
| Spend-to-unlock incentive | Qualifying cash spend is within an explicitly approved budget | Cost/terms assessment; never label this free advertising under a zero-cash request |
| Ordinary paid campaign | Explicit approved cash amount plus measurement and billing controls | One bounded campaign preparation/execution lane, with evidence and stop rules |

Do not call an organic publication an ad campaign or a credit recommendation an account entitlement. No free offer verified is a useful result, not a reason to create accounts or chase speculative promotions.

## 2. Verify the exact incentive, not its headline

Consult [dated official evidence](advertising-incentives.md), then reopen the primary offer and applicable country/account terms. Record the actual retrieval date and scope. Do not treat this snapshot as permanently current. If sign-in or supplied account evidence is necessary, name the missing evidence and stop eligibility claims there. Do not infer an offer from old coupon lists, partner commissions or another country's landing page.

Use this record for each candidate:

| Field | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Provider, official offer URL, terms URL, offer ID where visible, retrieval date, country and billing currency |
| Eligibility | New versus existing advertiser; organization/program eligibility; account age and first-impression rules; prior advertiser history; recipient/account ownership |
| Award | Face amount, account/campaign/product scope, nontransferability and stacking restrictions |
| Qualification | Actual required ad costs versus deposits/payments; threshold, clock start, deadline, exclusions and successful billing requirement |
| Fulfillment | Credit application delay; visible billing/promotions status; pending is distinct from available |
| Expiration | Claim/redemption deadline, use-by date, timezone; reconcile conflicting general and specific wording |
| Cash exposure | Qualifying spend, deposit, payment-method requirement, taxes/fees, minimum purchase, post-credit billing, exhaustion alerts, outstanding charges |
| Control | Verified budget limit semantics, expiry/exhaustion monitor, stop trigger, authorized pause path and readback |
| Decision | Fits no-cash / requires approved cash / eligibility unknown / rejected, with reason and confidence |

Unknown means unknown. A single unclear required charge or continuation behavior prevents a claim that an offer fits strict zero-cash. Never fabricate financial, nonprofit or account eligibility. Do not open extra accounts to evade one-off restrictions.

Evaluate minimum cash outlay without subtracting future promotional credit from the spend threshold. In a hypothetical spend $500/get $500 offer, upfront qualifying costs remain $500 plus any excluded taxes/fees. A $500 credit is a future ad-cost offset, not a refund, revenue, or proof of profitable learner acquisition.

## 3. Prepare the smallest viable campaign

Select the relevant [platform lane](platform-advertising.md). Compare audience intent, learning/commercial objective, approved budget, asset fit and destination. Do not default to every platform. Reuse existing creative and a truthful promise; route new craft to its owning skill. A complete course rarely needs to be used unedited as the ad itself: prepare a short evidence-backed demonstration and direct interested viewers to the authorized lesson/course.

Define a meaningful observable event such as a verified course start, registration, qualified teaching inquiry or subsequent lesson engagement. Label proxies, estimated values and unmeasurable completion honestly. Do not assign invented revenue to a free course or upload learner/student/customer lists without authorization and a lawful data basis. Tracking setup is a separately scoped implementation; existing exports can support a preliminary analysis.

For a future paid budget, record approved maximum cash cost, duration, primary KPI, optional credit and qualification dates, audience exclusions, variants, attribution window, baseline/comparison limitations, monitoring owner, stop threshold and pause authority. Preserve enough headroom for billing lag, permitted overdelivery and taxes. A daily average is not a hard lifetime cap. Do not increase the ceiling to satisfy an incentive or an archived scaling rule.

## 4. Capability and execution gate

Inspect actual host tool schemas and platform/account identity. Verify separately: read/report capability, draft/preview capability, and exact write/pause/billing capability. An installed generic marketing method is not a connector; OpenAI/ChatGPT Ads Manager is not Google, Meta or Reddit Ads Manager. Never infer logged-in state from tool availability.

Use an existing genuine provider where available. Otherwise finish a reviewable handoff with asset/source IDs, campaign settings, target account/platform, cost ceiling, metrics, terms and stop plan; request the smallest redacted export/readback needed. Manual operation is a valid fallback. A unique provider gap may be assessed through Work Management and Master Security; it does not authorize installs, credentials, cards or account creation.

Before any authorized activation: confirm the current account, destination, asset preview, approved cash cap, credit status, billing method and uncovered-cost behavior, dates, targeting, policy status and working pause path. Distinguish prepared, configured, approved, active/delivering, billed and measured. Verify changed settings and serving state through a readback; a saved draft or preview is not delivery.

## 5. Measure, stop and reconcile

Use the same date window, timezone, currency, click/view definition and attribution model within comparisons. Separate paid from organic YouTube traffic and cross-platform attribution totals. Report spend and billed cash separately from credit application and served cost. For free resources emphasize qualified learning/engagement, not invented ROAS.

At the first approved checkpoint review delivery, destination/event reliability, credit status and cash pacing before interpreting creative performance. Missing/lagged conversions are not proof of failure. Escalate uncertain tracking or policy/billing anomalies; never preserve a learning phase while costs escape approved limits.

Pause only within current execution authority. Verify paused state at the relevant campaign/ad-group/ad level, note delivery/reporting lag, then reconcile outstanding fees and final bills. Pausing stops future serving after platform processing; it does not erase accrued charges. Report actual end state, measurement limits and one evidence-supported next decision.

## Acceptance scenarios

- Cash cap $0; hypothetical spend $500/get $500: classify requires approved cash and reject for this run. Produce organic alternative; no account/card/redemption/activation.
- Free course; nonprofit registration unknown: mark grant eligibility unknown, explain required organization evidence and search-only scope; do not call it free YouTube ads.
- Screenshot of Meta credit without terms: offer-specific country, spend, expiry and continuation stay unknown; prepare assessment only.
- Later authorized $100 total cash test: name exact platform/account, complete truthful creative, event definition and attribution, reserve taxes/lag/overdelivery headroom, verify bounded billing controls and pause capability; no automatic scaling. Return prepared handoff if provider absent.
- Authorized campaign pause: read paused state and subsequent billing/status; report paused versus final billing reconciled separately.
