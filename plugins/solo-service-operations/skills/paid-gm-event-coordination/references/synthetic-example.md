# Synthetic event example

All people, prices and conditions here are fictional. A venue offers a private room conditional on four registrations by Friday. The ticket is $30 per player; the room charge and GM pay are not yet specified. Six hypothetical paid seats would produce $180 gross ticket revenue, not GM income or venue profit.

The draft should acknowledge the room, ask whether its fee is flat or per attendee and already included, confirm setup/play/cleanup access, ask about any alternative time at the same terms, and clarify GM pay and prop/promotion support. Do not accept, book, charge, publish or promise attendance. A hypothetical send action would still need provider readback and would not establish mutual agreement.

Expected handoff: one draft, an internal confirmed/proposed/unknown terms table, and the next owner/action. The fictional minimum and deadline remain conditions in the draft.
