## Corrections made during synthesis

Marketing guidance has been reduced to the message decisions needed by a visual deliverable. SEO, CRM, outreach, calendars, campaign operations, and platform mutation are not imported. Prior approval remains effective; the package does not add ritual confirmations to authorized work.

Web-design conventions are not treated as poster rules. A typographic poster can be complete without an image. A dramatic campaign can use effects or asymmetry. Common fonts, centered type, gradients, and color combinations are judged in context, not prohibited because they appear in generic templates.

Image tooling follows available capabilities. The old universal chroma-green workaround is not carried forward; use native alpha when supported. Image generation or editing follows the current image tool and its instructions. Preserve exact copy and codes as deterministic design elements when repeat editing or reliable spelling matters.

Print specifications belong to the actual printer and placement. No universal 16-point minimum, 300-ppi pass, 3-mm bleed, QR size, or palette can certify every poster. A smaller email file can pass mechanical checks without becoming a press-certified master.

## Current-edition continuity

Inspect the current artifact and latest brief. Preserve portrait framing, separate editable type and imagery, event-specific codes with readable backup addresses, physical layering and the approved direction when they apply. Superseded dates, prices, URLs and approvals do not transfer to a new artifact. Verify the actual exported result.
