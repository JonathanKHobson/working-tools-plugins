## 0.4.0

Strengthen responsive logo-family and production-asset verification.

A large raster file or polished mockup does not establish small-size usability, recognizable variants or a production-ready logo.

Adds optical-master versus arrangement distinctions; apparent-size, pixel-density and transparent-padding checks; light/dark/one-color and crop verification; halo detection; evidence-based minimum sizes; and clear separation of visual resemblance from legal clearance. Exploratory and untested variants remain distinct from released assets.

Compatibility: Compatible procedural addition under the existing graphic-design entrypoint.

Upgrade: Update Graphic Design Studio and use the existing brand-guide production procedure when preparing or reviewing logo families.

Validation: Reviewed the complete added production guidance against the actual canonical/public diff; private brand assets, project bindings and personal defaults are absent. This text-only method update does not claim that any particular logo passed the new checks.
