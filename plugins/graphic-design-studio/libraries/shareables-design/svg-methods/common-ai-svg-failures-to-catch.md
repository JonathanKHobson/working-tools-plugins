## Common AI SVG Failures To Catch

- Generic symbols with no Shareables-specific personality.
- Same icon family recolored for every style source.
- Too many unrelated colors.
- Tiny details that disappear at 32px.
- Path noise that cannot be inspected or repaired.
- Inconsistent stroke width, corner radius, or visual weight.
- Off-center plates, motifs, or faces.
- Stray dots, unexplained accents, and weak decorative rings.
- Warning colors that read as accidental pink instead of intentional status color.
- Abstract personality motifs that do not communicate character, charm, or purpose.
- Dark-theme palettes that collapse into low-contrast navy-on-black.
- Unlabeled meaningful SVGs.
- Decorative SVGs exposed to assistive technology.
- Color-only state or risk meaning.
- Low contrast on dark or tinted surfaces.
- Overuse of glows, filters, blur, and texture.
- Pasted logos, copied brand marks, or third-party visual assets.
- Private local paths or unsafe URLs inside SVG or metadata.
