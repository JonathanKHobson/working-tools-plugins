## Concept Ledger

No SVG coordinates should be written until this ledger exists:

```text
style source:
sample role:
concept:
structural shapes:
decorative shapes:
palette colors:
geometry proof:
small-size drop rules:
why this is not generic:
```

Ledger rules:

- The concept must be a concrete object, symbol, or visual metaphor, not a vibe.
- Structural shapes must be the pieces required for recognition at 24px.
- Decorative shapes must be optional and explicitly droppable.
- Geometry proof must name center axes, important bounds, and any centroid or symmetry checks.
- Palette colors must be copied from the approved palette block.
- The final line must explain how the sample avoids generic icon-library output.
