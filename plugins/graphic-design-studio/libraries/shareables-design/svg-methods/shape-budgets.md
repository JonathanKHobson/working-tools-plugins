## Shape Budgets

Budgets apply to the visible art group, excluding `title`, `desc`, and wrapper `svg`.

| Tier | Max shapes | Hard rule |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Utility icon | 6 | Simple and consistent; no decorative noise. |
| Anchor mark | 3 | If the concept needs more, simplify the concept. |
| Motif silhouette | 5 | Must read as a one-color silhouette at 24px. |
| Future avatar | 14 | Deferred until mark and motif samples pass. |

Budget failure blocks generation output.
