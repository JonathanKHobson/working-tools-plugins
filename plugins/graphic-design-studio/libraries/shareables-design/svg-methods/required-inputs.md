## Required Inputs

Every SVG visual-language request should name:

- Style source: one of `core-utility`, `warm-editorial`, `structured-bridge`, `product-studio`, or `research-academic` for the next run. `dark-analytics` is paused until human design intent defines its palette and concept.
- Use tier: `utility-control`, `personality-mark`, `spot-illustration`, `diagram-mark`, or `future-library-candidate`.
- Semantic job: what the SVG should communicate.
- Audience: who will read the artifact and what credibility they need.
- Surface: docs page, packet, website, storyboard, dashboard, review artifact, or local proof gallery.
- Accessibility role: `decorative`, `meaningful`, `interactive`, or `data-visualization`.
- Size targets: at minimum 24, 32, 48, 96, and display size.
- Approved palette block: exactly 4 hex values for the requested style source.
- Approved concept spec: named anchor concept and named motif concept for the requested style source.
- Concept ledger: concept sentence, structural shapes, decorative shapes, geometry proof, palette colors, small-size drop rules, and why the result is not generic.
