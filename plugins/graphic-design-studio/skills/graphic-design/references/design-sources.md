## transfer a precedent without erasing its context

Use when a named work, movement, place, community or designer materially directs an artifact. This is a narrow addition to the existing source and visual review workflow.

**Record the precedent:** creator(s) including collaborators; exact work/title/date with discrepancies; institution/original publication URL; location, audience, production/distribution conditions; observed formal feature; documented purpose; rights status. Mark unverified fields unknown. A supplied designer list or search snippet is a lead, not a citation.

**Extract a mechanism:** state “because the reader must ___, the work uses ___, enabling ___.” Distinguish visible form from how participants actually contributed and who controlled final editing. If the mechanism requires participation, name how submissions are gathered, consented, edited, credited and used. If that cannot be done, drop the participation claim.

**Set the transfer boundary:** specify what can be carried to the new brief (e.g. a consistent route or open contribution slot), what must stay in its historical/political/geographic context, and what is legally unavailable (image, font, icon, portrait, quote, testimony). Generate original type, geometry and copy. Do not treat a culture, protest movement or marginalized community as a texture source.

**Test with a counterexample:** show one task where the mechanism fails. Equal modules mislead when severity is unequal; spatial typography fails when metric labels must be compared; an inviting ellipsis is hollow without a real contribution process. Check the finished artifact's facts, reading order, rights, and intended size.

**Worked example:** The [SFMOMA record for de Bretteville's *Pink poster*](https://www.sfmoma.org/artwork/2016.312/) gives a 1973 date and medium. A [contributed People's Graphic Design Archive entry](https://peoplesgdarchive.org/item/18072/pink-1974) dates *Pink (1974)* and describes invited women's writings; the date/process should be qualified. For a new fictional open house, original blank idea spaces may invite workshop proposals, with a visible submission/review plan. No historic poster elements or participants' writings are copied. If the event has no collection process, remove the co-author language and use a simpler catalog layout.

The complete [direction cards and selector](../../composition-and-typography/references/art-direction-brief-selector.md) retain original examples and transfer counterexamples. [This research source ledger](../../composition-and-typography/assets/art-direction/sources.json) records inspected extent and date conflicts. It is evidence for these cards, not a second general source catalog.

## On-demand asset and inspiration directory

When a brief needs a font, image, icon, texture, palette, mockup or specific precedent, resolve the installed `work-management` root through skill discovery and select a small relevant set from `references/operations/resource-directory.json`. Follow `references/operations/resource-access.md` for the supported access route. Prefer supplied and approved assets and identity rules. For each chosen item, record its creator, exact source, permitted use, attribution, format and modifications in the existing project record. A free preview or downloadable file does not establish commercial, derivative or redistribution rights. Build an original composition and inspect the rendered deliverable; do not treat a gallery screenshot as an editable asset.
