# Gallery to original graphic or presentation

Use [Godly](https://godly.design/) for visual direction and [Deck.gallery](https://www.deck.gallery/) for slide hierarchy, pacing and content-density examples when the brief leaves those decisions open. Read UX Design Studio's `design-reference-lookup/references/public-design-resources.md` through its installed or canonical skill location for the shared procedure, anchor packet and access/rights rules. If that owner is unavailable, browse the exact public source, record observed evidence/rights, and create original work; do not assume access or install another plugin.

For a deck, first define the audience, decision, narrative and evidence. Choose a few relevant slides: opening/context, evidence/explanation and action/conclusion. Identify how scale, whitespace, imagery, type and repeated motifs organize meaning; adapt those relationships to the actual content. Avoid copying a company's brand or decorative shell irrespective of meaning. Course slides retain the instructional sequence, practice and accessibility requirements; portfolio decks retain authentic evidence. Subject owners govern claims, Graphic Design governs composition, and the current native presentation/document provider creates the editable artifact.

Give the builder exact reference URLs and 3–8 observed anchors, with Change / Keep / Defer, owned assets, dimensions and checks. Create distinct slide roles with real content rather than repeated cards or borrowed wording. Inspect the exported deck at presentation size for overflow, hierarchy, contrast, image rights and consistency. Sources supply inspiration, not evidence for claims in the new deck.

A successful recurring original layout can become a template through the native Template Creator and Work Management's template portability procedure. Preserve editable fields, original masters and asset rights; test varied sample content. Gallery membership is reference access, not an editable-file license; the [Deck pricing page](https://www.deck.gallery/pricing/) distinguishes separately purchased marketplace templates. No paid access or purchase is required by this workflow.

For motion showcases of completed designs, route the owned images/screens to Video Production create-motion-graphics and its Animos procedure. Do not treat a motion preview as the editable design master.
