Design notes (not part of poster)

Concept: a locally posted editorial broadside. The large question establishes the public invitation; the dense course list functions as evidence of a real program. Type sizes and orientation change by role, not by random decoration. The angled idea invitation is the only interruption.

Transfer to dashboards: use the sectional scan rhythm and compact aligned facts, but remove the tilted callout and reserve expressive type for a narrative header.
