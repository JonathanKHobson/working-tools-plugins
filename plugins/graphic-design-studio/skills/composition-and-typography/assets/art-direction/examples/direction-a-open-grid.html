Design notes (reviewer-facing; not part of the poster)

  • Concept: the program is made with its neighbors, so every workshop and every blank invitation gets the same module. The grid is the image.
  • Audience relationship: co-author. On the printed sheet, the four dashed cells are meant to be written in or pinned over at the event.
  • Hierarchy: the headline states a fact (twelve) and an invitation. The logistics sit in one calm band, and nothing is ranked inside the grid.
  • Do not transfer: unranked equal modules to an exception-monitoring dashboard, where the reader needs the few items that need attention first.
