Design notes (not part of poster)

Concept: route through the program by day, with the open house as the entry point. The schedule itself supplies the visual rhythm. This is a navigational mechanism, not a copy of Olympic symbols, maps or pictograms.

Transfer to dashboards: this time-oriented grouping can support schedules, but real controls, status and units must remain explicit. Large day labels never replace detailed values.
