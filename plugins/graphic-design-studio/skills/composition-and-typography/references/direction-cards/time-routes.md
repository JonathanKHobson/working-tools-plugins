# Conditional art-direction card

Integrated 2026-09-30 as original transfer guidance. Read the full card for its task. These are contextual mechanisms, not named-artist presets or evidence of a universal aesthetic advantage. Historical interpretation, rights and user-test limits travel with the method.

## DC3 — Time routes

**Prompt:** Attendance choice depends heavily on day and availability. **Audience relationship:** planner seeking a path. **Hierarchy:** open-house entry point, then courses grouped by day and week range. **Type:** oversized day markers; small but explicit names, categories and ranges. **Image/material:** the schedule itself is the graphic. **Rhythm/density:** repeated routes have unequal item counts because source facts do; avoid filling empty days. **Color:** one color for navigation, one for event/action, with text carrying meaning. **Motion:** optional linked focus in a live interface; the static poster has none.

**Precedent/limit:** Aicher's Munich 1972 system applied consistent construction across multilingual communication materials [S08]. Its pictograms served specific sports and wayfinding needs. This card transfers systematic consistency and route testing, not pictogram forms or a claim that symbols are universally understood. **Example:** `examples/direction-c-time-routes.html`. **Counterexample:** sorting a dashboard's critical incidents strictly by weekday when severity should lead. **Transfer:** calendar and operational views may group by time, but must preserve state, filter, location, and exact time-zone/units. **Rights:** original marks/type; no Olympic glyphs. **Test:** a viewer with one available day locates a possible class and correct week range without being misled by the open-house date.

## Adapt the temporal unit

Day and week groups are the worked example, not the required granularity. For an arrival-period task, use confirmed periods and actual durations within the day. Equal band widths only imply equal durations when that is true. Establish whether the action is a preference, appointment, reservation or walk-in choice and how it is recorded. Do not draw selectable slots or imply guaranteed admission from a time band alone.
