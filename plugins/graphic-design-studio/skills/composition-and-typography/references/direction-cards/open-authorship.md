# Conditional art-direction card

Integrated 2026-09-30 as original transfer guidance. Read the full card for its task. These are contextual mechanisms, not named-artist presets or evidence of a universal aesthetic advantage. Historical interpretation, rights and user-test limits travel with the method.

## DC1 — Open grid, open authorship

**Prompt:** The program is still being shaped by its public, and participation is a real, resourced step. **Audience relationship:** co-author, with a named place and process for input. **Hierarchy:** an invitation and event facts first; many current offers can have equal area. **Type:** expressive invitation; calm labels, names and times. **Image/material:** submitted marks or words only with consent; blank cells can be written on at a physical event. **Rhythm/density:** repeated equal modules interrupted by intentional empty slots. **Color:** distinguish invitation from completed information without implying that missing data is an error. **Motion:** none necessary. **Mechanism:** the artifact visibly reserves space for future voices, with staff collecting and responding to ideas. The on-site process matters more than the shape alone.

**Precedent/limit:** *Pink* gathered women's responses to the color, according to the People's Graphic Design Archive [S03]. SFMOMA dates a *Pink poster* 1973 [S02], while the contributed archive dates its record 1974; do not collapse them into a certain single year. De Bretteville's public work and attributed remarks about ellipses address space for another voice [S04], but an ellipsis on a poster cannot prove actual participation. **Example:** `examples/direction-a-open-grid.html`; every current workshop gets equal area and four blank idea cells invite input. **Counterexample:** an operations dashboard with unequal exception severity; equal cards would obscure the urgent cases. **Transfer:** in a course catalog, use comparable modules and an explicit suggestion path; in a dashboard, retain clear priority, status and ownership. **Rights:** use only original copy and marks or consented submissions; do not copy *Pink* imagery, color scheme or written contributions. **Test:** ask visitors what they can contribute and who will see it; audit whether submissions can actually be handled.
