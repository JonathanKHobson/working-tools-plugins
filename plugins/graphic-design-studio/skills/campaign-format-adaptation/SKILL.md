# Campaign format adaptation

Identify the exact placement before exporting: a framed print, a social image, a story, a fixed banner, or a responsive ad asset set. Check current platform specifications or the printer's supplied requirements when compliance matters. Familiar dimensions are draft assumptions, not guaranteed current upload rules.

Separate **invariants** from **variables**. Invariants usually include the approved offer, exact facts, identity, main visual concept, and destination. Variables include crop, type size, line breaks, relative area, supporting detail, and CTA mechanism. Do not change facts to make a layout fit.

For a change of medium or reader task, read [medium content planning](references/medium-content-plan.md). It supplies a worked poster/feed/story/web/slide example with include, omit, priority, budget and check decisions, plus positive and deliberately failing review cases. Use existing facts and the approved identity; a different placement may need a different reading route and content selection. Keep essential qualifications with their claims and deliver any required companion copy.

Recompose around the new aspect ratio. Rebalance masses, preserve focal subjects, and relocate the action area. Shorten or move secondary copy with the user's intent intact; avoid shrinking a complete print poster into a tiny mobile image. A banner may need a different excerpt of the same idea, not all the poster's content.

Distinguish a complete designed graphic from assets a platform assembles. For Google responsive display assets, consult the current official guide: its image guidance discourages overlaid text and logos because the platform combines supplied components. Do not generalize that rule to all social graphics or fixed poster artwork. Start at [the official guide](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9823397?hl=en) and recheck the actual campaign type.

For screen graphics, inspect at realistic displayed size and account for the platform's crop and overlay regions. Supply important event text and an action route in accessible companion copy where applicable. A user viewing the ad on a phone may not be able to scan a QR on that same screen conveniently.

For framed print, confirm both paper dimensions and the frame's visible opening when known. Keep critical information clear of that opening; a trim-safe margin may still be covered by a frame. Do not introduce bleed or crop marks without a production reason.

Keep variants traceable to one factual source and the approved visual edition. Review each actual export; success in one aspect ratio does not establish success in another. Deliver only requested variants with editable source and exports as appropriate. Explain any deliberately omitted supporting information and where it remains available.

For video thumbnails or course-series packaging, read and apply [video thumbnail concepts](references/video-thumbnail-concepts.md), including paired titles, truthful proof, series invariants, meaningful variants, rendered feed-size QA and the native publishing handoff.
