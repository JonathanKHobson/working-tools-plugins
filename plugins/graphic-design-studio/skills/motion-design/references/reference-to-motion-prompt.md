# From a motion reference to an original visual brief

Use for an animated brand moment, kinetic headline or product demonstration whose composition needs direction before implementation. Preserve the existing temporal-composition and illustrated-vector procedures; this adds a reusable prompt shape, not another renderer.

Source inspiration: [Charlie Hills, Opus 5.5 Motion Graphics](https://charliehills.substack.com/p/opus-55-motion-graphics), public text accessed 2026-10-05. The initial article review inspected its public portion; subsequently supplied screenshots are covered in the recipes below; the gated collection and downloadable assets were not imported. The following prompts and checks are original adaptations, not a reconstruction of that collection. Its model and sample loop length are not requirements.

## Reference transfer

Keep production choices conditional: an eight-second loop and self-contained HTML are options for a requested standalone study, not requirements for a website or every motion piece. Preserve readable holds rather than applying a universal sub-300ms duration. For CSS/WAAPI, spring, path or mask choices, use UX Motion and Performance's `motion-technique-selection.md` when available; the production owner verifies the selected runtime. Do not impose a tools list or translate community taste rules into universal standards.

Additional user-supplied stills now inform [sixteen original motion recipes](motion-pattern-recipes.md), including source anchors and limits. They expand the available visual evidence without supplying the original prompts, executable board or observed timing.

Describe the observed visual mechanism separately from its styling: which object persists, what changes role, where attention moves and where reading time occurs. Mark inferred timing as unmeasured unless playback was inspected. Identify Change / Keep / Defer against approved source anchors. Preserve exact authorized copy and brand assets; references do not grant reuse rights to fonts, art or code. When a supplied board is accessible, isolate only the selected effect and inspect its dependencies before adaptation; do not claim to have copied an unavailable tile.

## Prompt for a visual sequence

> Art-direct [message] for [site region or motion deliverable] at [dimensions/viewing context]. Use [reference mechanism] to connect [opening state] to [payoff]. Establish the opening, transformation, reading hold and ending; specify focal point, hierarchy, continuity and crop for each. Keep [approved words/assets/tokens] and change [bounded choices]. Give important words enough time to read in playback. Provide [static/reduced-motion equivalent] that preserves the message. Label example claims/data. Hand the visual brief to [Sites/UX for interactive web behavior, Video Production for rendered scenes]. Review key frames and transitions in playback at delivery size; a styleframe board alone does not establish success.

## Prompt for a targeted visual repair

> In [identified beat/time and viewport], [observed overlap, unreadable hold, crop or broken continuity] weakens [message]. Fix that defect while keeping [approved composition/type/asset anchors]. Compare before/after at the same moment and inspect the adjacent transitions. Confirm the complete message remains readable and the ending or loop reset is intentional; report what was actually viewed.

For a website, return exact copy/assets, the chosen sequence, readable holds, responsive crop constraints and stable alternative to `ux-design-toolkit:motion-and-performance`. Its motion-prompts-and-sites reference owns the functional-versus-illustrative distinction and Site handoff. A decorative animation must not masquerade as a working control. For standalone rendered media, retain `video-production-workflow:create-motion-graphics` as production owner. Do not prescribe renderer, audio or a new site from an art-direction request alone.

Smallest useful test: implement and play one meaningful transition with its reading hold at the intended size before extending the visual system. More effects are warranted only if they improve the requested communication.
