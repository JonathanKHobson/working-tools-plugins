## Workflow

1. Inventory collected identifiers, sensitive fields, delivery mode, audience languages and accessibility needs.
2. Apply the complete relevant reviews; align notices and optional/required fields with actual storage and collection.
3. Check respondent-facing behavior and translated meaning; preserve unresolved governance questions rather than claiming compliance.

## Deliver and verify

Return precise findings and corrected text/behavior within scope, with verification and unresolved constraints.

## Example boundary

Good: disclose identifiable follow-up email collection. Bad: promise anonymity while storing respondent IDs.

Keep one canonical instrument in its owning project. Preserve decision → construct → question → coded value → analysis use. Never infer anonymity, scale validity, legal compliance or platform compatibility. For rendered deliverables verify the changed visual and respondent path; a schema check alone is insufficient.
