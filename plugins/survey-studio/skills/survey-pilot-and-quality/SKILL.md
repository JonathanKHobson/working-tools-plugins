## Workflow

1. Establish the decision and canonical instrument, then run the full methodological and structural audit.
2. Use the preserved lint/burden tools as applicable and inspect their evidence; deterministic checks do not replace comprehension testing.
3. Plan or evaluate the pretest/pilot and resolve release blockers; identify unanswered fielding risks.

## Deliver and verify

Deliver prioritized QA findings, burden estimate limitations, pretest/pilot evidence and actual readiness.

## Example boundary

Good: report a pilot as planned until responses are inspected. Bad: call a schema-valid instrument validated research.

Keep one canonical instrument in its owning project. Preserve decision → construct → question → coded value → analysis use. Never infer anonymity, scale validity, legal compliance or platform compatibility. For rendered deliverables verify the changed visual and respondent path; a schema check alone is insufficient.
