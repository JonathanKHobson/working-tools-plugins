# Survey strategist

If asked what Survey Toolkit can do or how to start, use the [human guide](../../README.md) to offer a small question-design task, a complete instrument, or an export with needed decision/input and reviewable result. For a specific survey task, route to its specialist and start; do not force an overview or infer a platform connection.

Frame a survey decision, choose the research method and coordinate an end-to-end instrument through the appropriate survey specialists.

Read the task-matching files below in full before applying that procedure. Load only the relevant variant and its required support. These contain the operative methods, examples and templates; this entrypoint does not replace them. A plugin is a package, not a requirement to read every skill.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision, population, experience window and analysis use; read the full intake and creation workflow.
2. Choose survey or another method based on the question. Create one canonical instrument and use only the specialist needed for the current phase.
3. For a full instrument, coordinate question design, sampling, flow, privacy, pilot/QA and export. Verify the actual requested delivery stage.

## Deliver and verify

Deliver the requested decision brief or complete instrument package. Use the full output contract for an end-to-end job; do not force every export or review on a wording-only task.

## Example boundary

Good: route a scale wording task directly to survey-question-design. Bad: require the entire 219-line orchestration procedure for a single export.

Keep one canonical instrument in its owning project. Preserve decision → construct → question → coded value → analysis use. Never infer anonymity, scale validity, legal compliance or platform compatibility. For rendered deliverables verify the changed visual and respondent path; a schema check alone is insufficient.


## Atlas research methods

For general research concepts and methods, use Research Methodology Studio’s scoped `search_research_atlas` / `read_research_atlas` when available. Keep this studio’s project/client evidence and internal research database authoritative for actual findings. Atlas is a supplementary method source, not Oppia observations or survey results. If the neighboring provider is unavailable, use the existing local method and state that Atlas was not queried. Do not start a duplicate global Atlas backend.
