# Pretesting and Piloting

## Why it is mandatory

Researchers know what they intended; respondents reveal what the instrument actually asks. Pretesting finds comprehension, retrieval, judgment, response-mapping, sensitivity, flow, and technical defects before they contaminate a full sample.

## Layers

### Expert review

Use methodology, domain, accessibility, privacy, localization, and platform experts to find visible defects. Expert review cannot substitute for target-participant testing.

### Cognitive interviewing

Use think-aloud carefully and targeted probes after an answer:

- What did the question mean in your own words?
- What period or examples did you think about?
- How did you arrive at the answer?
- Were any choices missing or overlapping?
- How did you choose between two scale points?
- Was anything uncomfortable or unclear?

Test a purposive range of target participants, including edge cases and accessibility/language needs. Iterate until major interpretation problems stabilize; there is no magic universal sample count.

### Usability/programming test

Observe completion on relevant devices and assistive technology. Test logic paths, validation, timing, save/resume, randomization, quotas, termination, incentives, and exported values.

### Soft launch/pilot

Field to a small operational subset under real conditions. Review recruitment, eligibility, response rate, breakoff, item missingness, duration distribution, straightlining, open-text quality, device issues, quota pace, and data schema before broad release.

## Pretest log

For each issue record:

- participant/context;
- item/path;
- observed interpretation or behavior;
- severity and likely data effect;
- proposed change;
- version changed;
- retest result.

## Change control

After fielding starts, avoid substantive wording, option, logic, or scale changes within one analysis wave. If a critical fix is necessary, timestamp/version it, preserve prior data, test the change, and analyze comparability.

## Launch criteria

- target respondents interpret primary items as intended;
- no critical accessibility/privacy/ethics issue;
- all paths and validation pass;
- duration and burden are acceptable;
- response data match the codebook;
- invitation, reminders, incentive, and support work;
- analysis pipeline can consume a test export;
- unresolved warnings have named owners and documented acceptance.
