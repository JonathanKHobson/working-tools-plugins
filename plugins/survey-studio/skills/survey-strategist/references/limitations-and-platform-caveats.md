# Limitations and Platform Caveats

## General

- Platform features, import syntax, APIs, and file schemas change. Pin the product/version and verify against current official documentation.
- A local syntax validator cannot prove a vendor will accept or execute a file.
- Generic exports cannot preserve every proprietary setting.
- PDF/DOCX/Markdown/plain text are review artifacts, not functional equivalents of a programmed survey.
- HTML generated by this skill is a prototype, not a secure production collection service.
- Burden estimates are heuristics; measure real completion distributions in a pilot.
- Linter findings are prompts for expert judgment, not automated validity certification.

## Qualtrics

- Advanced/Simple TXT cover only documented import behavior.
- Logic, quotas, scoring, translations, Look & Feel, contact-list behavior, embedded data, randomization, and specialty questions may require manual programming.
- The skill refuses to synthesize QSF.
- Import and response-export tests in the target Qualtrics tenant are mandatory.

## Other platforms

- XLSForm, SurveyJS, REDCap, and LimeSurvey require version-specific mappings and testing.
- The references explain safe workflows but the bundled exporter does not currently generate native files for all of them.
- Never rename an approximate/generic file with a native extension and claim compatibility.

## Research limits

- Survey responses are self-report unless linked to observed records.
- Cross-sectional associations are not causal effects.
- Large samples do not eliminate coverage, nonresponse, measurement, or processing error.
- Convenience/opt-in samples do not automatically support population inference.
- Open text in a survey does not replace probing qualitative research.
- “Validated” scales lose comparability when changed without evidence.

## Legal and ethical limits

References are general operational guidance, not legal advice, IRB approval, security certification, accessibility conformance certification, or a substitute for jurisdiction-specific review.

## Delivery rule

Always include a warning manifest or limitations section that distinguishes implemented features, respondent-facing projection, manual programming, untested behavior, and unresolved risk.
