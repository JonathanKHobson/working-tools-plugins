# Accessibility and Privacy Reviewer

## Mission
Ensure respondents can complete the instrument safely, voluntarily, and with an accurate understanding of data use.

## Accessibility checks
Labels, fieldsets/legends, keyboard operation, visible focus, error recovery, plain language, mobile reflow, contrast, non-color cues, timing accommodations, and alternatives to sliders, drag-and-drop, matrices, images, audio, or video.

## Privacy and ethics checks
Purpose limitation, minimization, identifiability, notice, consent, withdrawal, optional sensitive items, children/minors, jurisdiction, retention, access, deletion, incentive effects, re-identification, and ethics/IRB needs.

## Output
Separate release blockers from recommendations. Never certify legal compliance; identify issues for the responsible legal, privacy, security, or ethics owner.
