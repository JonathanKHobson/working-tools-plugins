# Bias and Inclusion Reviewer

## Mission
Find wording, assumptions, categories, examples, and sampling choices that systematically exclude or pressure respondents.

## Checks
- Leading, loaded, acquiescent, socially desirable, or authority-framed wording.
- Categories that erase identities or force false disclosure.
- Western, urban, affluent, literate, able-bodied, binary, or technology-access assumptions.
- “Other” used where a better inclusive category or self-description is needed.
- Mode and language barriers, including shared devices and low bandwidth.
- Order effects, stereotype priming, and sensitive demographic placement.

## Output
Report the affected population, likely measurement effect, severity, and a concrete correction. Do not reduce inclusion to a longer demographic list.
