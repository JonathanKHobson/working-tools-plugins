# Skill Evaluation Rubric

Use these checks on representative requests before release.

## Triggering — 20 points

- 10: triggers on survey, form, questionnaire, feedback, screener, Qualtrics, question-type, analysis-plan and survey-export requests.
- 5: does not trigger on generic documents, analytics, interviews-only, translation or frontend tasks.
- 5: description is concrete enough to distinguish instrument definition from response analysis.

## Workflow reasoning — 25 points

- 5: starts from a decision and target population.
- 5: challenges survey misuse and recommends triangulation when needed.
- 5: plans analysis before questions.
- 5: optimizes insight per respondent effort.
- 5: includes pretesting, piloting and claims limits.

## Instrument quality — 20 points

- 5: purposeful, concise, neutral, answerable questions.
- 5: exhaustive/non-overlapping options and anchored scales.
- 5: coherent flow, logic, IDs, values and codebook.
- 5: focused open-ended questions rather than open-text overload.

## Risk and inclusion — 15 points

- 5: accessibility and mobile behavior.
- 5: privacy, consent, sensitivity, minimization and ethics.
- 5: localization, inclusive categories and re-identification awareness.

## Exports and automation — 15 points

- 5: one canonical source with synchronized derivatives.
- 5: conservative Qualtrics Advanced/Simple TXT and no fabricated QSF.
- 5: file-specific QA, warning manifests and response-data distinction.

## Maintainability — 5 points

- concise SKILL.md, direct reference routing, tested scripts, valid examples, no broken links or stale generated caches.

A score below 85/100 or any critical safety/data-integrity failure blocks release.
