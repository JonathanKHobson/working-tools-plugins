# Survey sampling

Plan survey population, sampling, recruitment, incentives and response coverage for an explicit research decision.

## Complete procedures to load

Read the task-matching files below in full before applying that procedure. Load only the relevant variant and its required support. These contain the operative methods, examples and templates; this entrypoint does not replace them. A plugin is a package, not a requirement to read every skill.

- [Complete sampling-recruitment-and-invitations procedure](../survey-strategist/references/sampling-recruitment-and-invitations.md) — read for population, frame, invitations or incentives.
- [Complete method-selection-and-triangulation procedure](../survey-strategist/references/method-selection-and-triangulation.md) — read when deciding if a survey or another method answers the decision.
- [Canonical instrument contract](../../references/instrument-contract/instrument-contract.md) — read when reviewing or changing stable question IDs, codes and analysis lineage.

## Workflow

1. Define target population, decision and feasible sampling frame.
2. Apply the full sampling/recruitment method and make eligibility, invitation, incentive and nonresponse assumptions explicit.
3. Check who is excluded, expected response limitations and whether the resulting data supports the claimed comparison.

## Deliver and verify

Deliver sampling/recruitment plan and invitation copy with limitations; sending or fielding remains a separate authorized action.

## Example boundary

Good: state convenience-sample limits. Bad: label an opt-in group representative without supporting design.

Keep one canonical instrument in its owning project. Preserve decision → construct → question → coded value → analysis use. Never infer anonymity, scale validity, legal compliance or platform compatibility. For rendered deliverables verify the changed visual and respondent path; a schema check alone is insufficient.
