# Oppia Netnography

## Overview

Use public community discourse as contextual, non-representative evidence for a
specific Oppia research gap. Sampling, ethics, interpretation, and
triangulation must remain visible in the output.

## Maturity

This skill is provisional. Oppia has used multi-community netnography to
triangulate persona evidence, but the reviewed source set does not yet contain a
dedicated method standard. Use this workflow for bounded work and revisit it
when the project owner supplies the planned external methods research.

Source anchor: `drive.oppia.persona-handoff.v1`.
Cross-cutting ethics, inclusion, counterevidence, and AI-assistance rules are
adapted from `external.ux-research-skill.v1.0.0`; that source is not a dedicated
netnography standard and does not change this skill's provisional maturity.

## Workflow

1. Search the Oppia corpus and state the specific gap that community discourse
   could illuminate.
2. Define the decision, community type, inclusion criteria, date range,
   language, search terms, unit of analysis, and relevant cultural/platform
   context before collecting posts.
3. Use only content accessible within the authorized research setup and
   consistent with platform terms. Treat public availability as a minimum, not
   automatic ethical permission for identifiable quotation.
4. Keep a sampling log: community, date, query/path, items screened, items
   included, exclusions, and observed moderation or demographic cues.
5. Capture contextual observations, recurring language, tensions, workarounds,
   and negative cases. Separate a post author's statement from researcher
   interpretation.
6. Pseudonymize by default. Avoid searchable verbatim quotes in shared outputs
   when paraphrase preserves the finding.
7. Compare across communities before calling a pattern. Report silence and
   source concentration. Examine counterpatterns, platform norms, moderation,
   language, access, time, and geography before integrating disagreement.
8. Triangulate with Oppia interviews, usability evidence, analytics, or other
   direct sources before revising a persona or product recommendation.
9. If AI assists translation, coding, or synthesis, confirm the content may be
   processed, minimize identifiable text, retain source links, test a
   human-reviewed subset, and disclose material use.

## Output Contract

- decision and corpus gap;
- community and sampling frame;
- ethics/privacy boundary;
- collection log and coding frame;
- patterns, counterpatterns, and contextual examples;
- source concentration and representativeness limits;
- triangulation status;
- language/cultural context and AI-assistance status where applicable;
- provisional implications and next evidence needed.

## Stop Conditions

- Stop if the work requires joining a private group, deceptive participation,
  scraping beyond authorization, or exposing sensitive identities.
- Do not infer population prevalence from post counts.
- Do not update an Oppia persona from one community or anecdote.

## Verification

- The output states that the skill is provisional.
- Sampling and exclusions are reproducible.
- Identifiers and searchable quotations are minimized.
- Claims remain contextual and confidence-labeled.
- A persona or design implication includes triangulation or is marked as a
  hypothesis.
