# Oppia Competitive Analysis

## Overview

Build a traceable comparison that informs one Oppia decision. Use direct,
indirect, and analogous products deliberately; popularity is not a selection
criterion by itself.

Source anchor: `drive.generic.competitor-template.v1`. Method-fit,
counterevidence, inclusion, and AI-assistance rules are adapted from
`external.ux-research-skill.v1.0.0`.

## Workflow

1. Define the Oppia decision, product surface, target user, geography/device
   context, and what comparison could change.
2. Search the Oppia corpus for known user needs and constraints that should
   shape the criteria.
3. Select a bounded set:
   - direct alternatives serving a similar learning goal;
   - indirect alternatives solving the goal differently;
   - analogous products with a relevant interaction pattern.
4. Record inclusion and exclusion reasons.
5. Define observable criteria and anchored rating scales before collecting
   evidence. Use metrics such as path length, discoverability, content model,
   offline behavior, accessibility, language, trust cues, recovery, or cost only
   when relevant to the decision.
6. Capture dated evidence with URL, surface/version, device, region, account
   state, observation, and screenshot/reference when permitted.
7. Separate observation, score, interpretation, and implication for Oppia.
8. Compare patterns, gaps, tradeoffs, outliers, and counterexamples. Check
   whether language, accessibility, connectivity, account state, or region
   changes the comparison rather than averaging across unlike conditions.
9. If AI assists collection or synthesis, preserve original evidence and URLs,
   verify consequential claims manually, and disclose material use.
10. Avoid winner-takes-all
   rankings unless the decision truly requires one.
11. End with a small set of implications or hypotheses for Oppia, not copied
   features.

## Output Contract

- decision and scope;
- comparison-set rationale;
- criteria and rating anchors;
- source-labeled evidence matrix;
- cross-product patterns and counterexamples;
- capture dates, versions, contexts, and evidence freshness limits;
- Oppia constraints from historical evidence;
- implications, hypotheses, limitations, and next action.

## Stop Conditions

- Stop when each criterion has enough evidence to distinguish meaningful
  patterns.
- Do not expand to unrelated pricing, brand, employee, or market metrics merely
  because a generic template contains them.
- Do not recommend copying a pattern without checking Oppia user/context fit.

## Verification

- Every score points to an observation and source.
- The comparison set is justified and bounded.
- Product/account/device differences are documented.
- Claims do not confuse feature presence with effectiveness.
- Oppia implications are labeled as implications or hypotheses, not user data.
- Relevant accessibility, language, connectivity, and regional differences are
  represented or named as gaps.
