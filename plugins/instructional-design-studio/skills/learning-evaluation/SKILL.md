# Learning Evaluation & Transfer

State the decision the evaluation should inform: revise an activity, remove a barrier, choose delivery, or assess transfer. Use existing data where appropriate before proposing additional collection.

## Evidence plan

Use [learning beyond the activity](../instructional-design/references/addie-decisions.md#preserve-learning-beyond-the-activity) to check whether the measured response represents the intended understanding or transferable capability; completion of the practice vehicle alone is insufficient.
Separate reach/enrollment, participation/completion, satisfaction, immediate performance, delayed retention, transfer, and organizational outcomes. A positive survey is not evidence of mastery. A before/after difference without a comparison is not automatically caused by instruction.

A posttest alone may show current proficiency, not improvement caused by the course. Self-reported confidence or retrospective ratings are not direct performance evidence. Record simultaneous coaching, policy, tool, or workflow changes when interpreting organizational outcomes.

For mixed knowledge and feedback instruments, use [the assessment/survey boundary](../assessment-rubric-design/references/survey-boundary.md). Survey Studio owns experience-item construction and exports; Assessment & Rubric Design owns knowledge tasks and keys. For item-level pilot response review, use [quality and validation](../assessment-rubric-design/references/quality-and-validation.md). No confidence or enjoyment points belong in a mastery score.

Use parallel pre/post performance tasks when appropriate, a common rubric, and clear time points. For transfer, specify destination task, opportunity to perform, support available, delay, and observable evidence. Distinguish an unobserved opportunity from failed transfer. Keep any delayed check proportionate to the decision and learner burden.

Track missingness, denominator, attrition, help received, practice exposure, and access failures. Avoid extracting small-group sensitive characteristics or presenting identifying results. For qualitative feedback preserve exact source excerpts; distinguish participant statements from interpretation.

## Output
Produce an evaluation plan or analysis with questions, measures, baseline availability, timing, responsible person, decision rules, limitations, and the next revision. Use [evaluation patterns](references/evaluation-patterns.md) for pilots, claim limits, or reporting language. Report data status: planned, collected, analyzed, or independently verified.

If data are insufficient, say what can be concluded now and what remains unknown. Do not fabricate learner gains, use enrollment numbers as effectiveness claims, or build a dashboard when a brief with a few measures answers the decision.
