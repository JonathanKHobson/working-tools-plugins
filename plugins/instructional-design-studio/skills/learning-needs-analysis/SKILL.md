# Learning Needs Analysis

Identify the action people need to perform, where performance breaks down, and what evidence demonstrates the gap. Establish the difference between requested training and the actual performance outcome without dismissing the user's chosen deliverable.

## Diagnose
Compare expected and observed performance using examples, work samples, interviews, error patterns, or existing research. Distinguish missing knowledge or practice from inaccessible tools, unclear ownership, incentives, time, policy, or missing resources. If people can perform with a short job aid, explain why a job aid may be enough; if the user still needs a course, include the aid within it.

Separate the sponsor's organizational target from the learner behavior the intervention can plausibly change. Choose evidence for the unresolved question: records for patterns, observation or a task probe for performance, interviews for context, surveys for broader self-report. Do not default to collecting all four.

When supplied customer or audience evidence informs a learning brief, retain its project profile ID, version, operating status, sources and claim limits. Keep sponsor, payer and learner roles distinct; a sponsor's buying objective is not an observed learner need, and learners need not be buyers. `marketing-strategy-studio:marketing-strategy-operator` owns commercial fit; this skill owns performance diagnosis, learner prerequisites and access conditions. Reuse available context without requiring an ICP or new paperwork for ordinary needs analysis.

Record:
- Audience groups, prior knowledge and prerequisite tasks, language, access conditions, and stakes.
- Critical decisions, common mistakes, cues experts notice, and consequences.
- What is known from evidence, what is inferred, and what must be checked.
- The smallest intervention that addresses the diagnosed gap and how its effect would be observed.

Use short single-purpose SME questions: “Where do beginners get stuck?” then a separate probe for an example. Do not treat expert intuition as representative learner evidence. For a supplied policy or regulated task, retain exact source/version and named content reviewer.

## Produce
Create a decision brief with performance gap, evidence table, learner/task analysis, recommended intervention, exclusions, assumptions, and one initial performance probe. Include a draft observable outcome so the brief can proceed directly into design.

Use the relevant [diagnosis and design](references/design-patterns.md) section for method choice, surveys, conflicting evidence, or operational dependencies. Stop research when the next design decision is sufficiently supported; do not invent a research program to delay the artifact. Carry unresolved dependencies into design with a proposed owner and readiness check.
