# Writing voices for learning content

Use Writing Quality Studio's `writing-voice-library` for a deliberate prose approach, and `voice-and-style` for an authorized instructor or brand voice. Load one selected canonical guide and a relevant synthetic example. Keep the corpus with its writing owner; ordinary lessons need no extra dependency or voice-selection stage.

Patient coaching can support a meaningful action-and-reason sequence; Socratic exchange can support worked questioning; technical reference can support expert retrieval. Choose by the learner's task and prior knowledge, not by a claim that a voice is proven to improve learning. Bases, candidates, modules, aliases and merged extensions have different statuses; preserve the selected record's evidence limits.

Instructional Design retains the learning goal, conceptual difficulty, practice, feedback, assessment and access decisions. Preserve supplied facts, exact task conditions, quotations, source locators, uncertainty and consent. Fictional voice examples do not supply observations, participant evidence, instructor biography or learner outcomes. A warmer tone must not promise success or label a difficult task easy.

Use Humanizer or No AI Slop checks for a specific clarity or cadence problem. Retain purposeful repetition, rehearsal cues, technical terms, learner-facing instructions and the instructor's effective style. Never add invented anecdotes or variation merely to seem human. Check the actual task and rendered artifact; an attractive voice is not evidence of learning.
