# Worked example: an evidence-led learning sheet

This example shows how to turn one stable science source into a small editable explanation and an actual learner task. Open the [HTML source](../assets/condensation-learning-sheet.html) in a browser; its SVG, text and answer key are editable in that file. The diagram is original and schematic. It is not a photo or measurement of the NOAA activity.
Its [design note](../assets/DESIGN.md) records the visual roles and safe adaptation points.

**Learner and outcome.** For a novice learner who has seen droplets on a cold cup: after reading the sheet, identify where the *outside* droplets came from and explain the gas-to-liquid change in one sentence. The open response and answer key support practice and self-check. No learning outcome has been measured or validated with learners.

**Source and claim ledger.** NOAA's [“Sweatin' to the Coldies” lesson](https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/ll-sweatin), reviewed 2026-10-07, provides the source. The sheet paraphrases it; it uses no NOAA images or copied lesson text.

| Claim ID | Source locator | Sheet use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | NOAA, Overview, paragraphs 1–2 | “Condensation: water vapor becomes liquid water”; cup example | The diagram shows a simplified process, not individual molecules. |
| C2 | NOAA, Procedure steps 1–4 and Discussion paragraph 1 | Cold cup, air at cup surface, drops on *outside* | The picture is illustrative; no actual experiment was performed. |
| C3 | NOAA, Discussion paragraph 1 | Answer: vapor in surrounding air supplies the outside drops | Do not infer water leaking through the cup. |
| C4 | NOAA, Discussion paragraph 2 | Optional note that amount can vary with humidity | No amounts, rates or temperature thresholds are supplied. |

**Design transfer.** Preserve the learner question, source locators, exact state change, and the separation between an observed droplet and a proposed explanation. Keep the answer hidden until the learner attempts the response. For a new topic, replace the source ledger and diagram labels together; do not reuse the cup arrows for an unrelated mechanism. If the requested final asset is a designed visual, use Graphic Design's source-to-visual method for composition, provenance and rendered QA while this skill retains the learner action and feedback.

**Accessibility and production.** The HTML provides a text explanation outside the SVG, descriptive SVG title/description, explicit labels, keyboard-accessible answer reveal, visible focus, responsive layout and a print style. Text and diagram are original; no remote scripts, fonts or images are required. Check the rendered sheet at intended and narrow viewports, verify the answer remains readable, and check source claims again if the NOAA page changes. The browser page is a worked example, not an LMS package, accessibility certification, or evidence that practice improved learning.
