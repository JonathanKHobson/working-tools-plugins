Where do the outside droplets come from?

A chilled cup can become wet on the outside. Follow the three labeled parts, then explain where that water began.

  1. NoticeWater appears on the outside of a cold cup.
  2. ExplainWater vapor in nearby air cools at the surface.
  3. Name the changeVapor becomes liquid water: condensation.

The droplets began as water vapor in the surrounding air. Near the cold cup, that vapor became liquid water on the outside surface. The change is called condensation. The drawing alone does not prove a particular cup did not leak; the source explains this common cold-cup case.
