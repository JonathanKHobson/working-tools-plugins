## Targeted live checks

These pages were opened and relevant passages inspected on September 20, 2026. This was selective verification, not a systematic review of every citation in S1 and S2.

- [CDC training development](https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/about/index.html): ADDIE as one development framework, with feedback during stages. Professional guidance for public health training.
- [CDC needs analysis](https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/about/assess-training-needs-conducting-needs-analysis.html): identify the gap and consider existing evidence and nontraining responses.
- [CDC measuring effectiveness](https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/about/evaluate-training-measuring-effectiveness.html): learning and transfer measures; posttest-only proficiency differs from measured change.
- [AAPOR survey practices](https://aapor.org/standards-and-ethics/best-practices/): method fit, sampling, neutral questions, and response choices. Professional guidance, not a guarantee of representativeness.
- [GOV.UK research privacy](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/managing-user-research-data-participant-privacy): consent, access, purpose, retention, and identifiers. Its legal context is not assumed universal.
- [CMU formative and summative assessment](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/basics/formative-summative.html): assessment purpose and later use of findings.
- [W3C visual description](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/description/): essential visual information and planning descriptions in media. This does not certify a produced asset's conformance.

All additions are concise paraphrases or labeled local applications. The older research reference retains its original check dates and access limitations.
