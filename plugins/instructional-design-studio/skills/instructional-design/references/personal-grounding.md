# Teaching context and original reusable defaults

Apply these defaults only when they fit the learner task, approved source and current instructor's preferences. The maintained personal edition used private proposals, course workbooks and conversation history as authoring context; those private locators, excerpts and historical account observations are excluded from this public edition. Their existence is not evidence of learning gains or a transferable license.

## Defaults to use
Start with the why of a meaningful task, then make the next learner action clear. Use familiar scenarios to lower domain overhead; state the transferable principle. Invite comparison and revision, and have learners retain a usable artifact. Introduce terminology when it helps describe an observed decision. Keep questions short and avoid multiple questions hidden inside one sentence.

Keep authoring rigor behind the learner interface. A learner should see what to do, why it matters, and how to check it, rather than a wall of methodology names. A small scaffold is useful when it leads to practice; do not keep adding a system when the learning asset is ready to ship.


## Evidence boundaries

Use the instructor's supplied or authorized material for a specific teaching history. Keep learner records, raw communications, course media and private workbooks outside the plugin. A proposal, platform checkbox, enrollment count or facilitator preference does not prove learning. Local evaluation scaffolds are proposed controls, not validated psychometric instruments. The complete generic lesson, assessment and course-production methods remain available without a connector or personal profile.
