# Quality review, pilot and interpretation

An author can identify threats; response evidence is needed to assess actual performance. Scale review to use and stakes. Do not force a research study for an informal practice question or call an unpiloted certification bank validated.

## Separate states

`draft` → `content-reviewed` → `piloted` → `approved-for-stated-use` are distinct. Record dates, people/roles, item versions and evidence; a software checker cannot confer these states. Planned, author-estimated and observed fields stay separate. A later edit to the key, source, format or scoring may invalidate prior review evidence.

## Adversarial author review

| Gate | Try to find | Repair/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Alignment | A learner can succeed without the target capability; target facets absent | Adjust task and coverage; explain inference limits |
| Source | Key requires an unsupported assertion or suppresses conflict | Verify source/version/locator; revise or flag unresolved key |
| Key | Second defensible answer under stated conditions | Clarify criterion or accept alternatives; independent solution |
| Distractors | Joke/filler, implausible error, or an obviously more careful key | Write coherent misconceptions; label their evidence status |
| Cueing | Grammar, length, absolutes, option position, or another item reveals answer | Review across bank; separate dependent variants |
| Scoring | Select-all exploit, unstated penalty, mismatched total, ambiguous bands | Work boundary/mixed cases and disclose rules |
| Access | Irrelevant literacy, color, timing, speech or dragging requirement | Equivalent route, clear language and rendered interface check if produced |
| Transparency | Hidden resource/AI rule, deliverable or criterion | Complete learner brief |
| Consequences | Score used beyond evidence, arbitrary mastery threshold | Specify decision rationale and review appropriate to stakes |

For accessible forms, use the W3C source in [sources](sources.md); a text review does not prove keyboard, screen-reader or mobile behavior. When an actual interface is requested, test it with the intended controls and error paths.

## Pretest and pilot

With target-like learners, inspect interpretation: “What is this asking?”, “Which evidence mattered?”, “How did you choose?”, “What alternative seemed defensible?” Avoid coaching before observing the initial response. Record ambiguous wording, help needed, resource/access failure, time burden, surprising answers and scorer disagreement. Then revise and recheck materially changed items. Participation in a pilot is not evidence of representativeness.

If learner access is unavailable, deliver a reviewed draft with a concrete pilot plan; do not invent participants or mark it piloted. Stakes determine the required human/subject-matter and measurement expertise before real use. The authoring task itself does not authorize recruiting or collecting learner data.

## Interpret response evidence carefully

- **Proportion correct:** correct responses divided by the stated eligible/scored denominator. Show n and handling of omitted/unobserved responses. It is sample- and context-specific; easy does not mean bad.
- **Option selections:** inspect counts and reasons for unused or popular distractors. A small sample cannot establish that an option never functions.
- **Item–total relations:** use an appropriate corrected relation when justified; dependent items, multidimensional outcomes and sample size affect interpretation. Do not apply universal cutoffs as deletion rules.
- **Rubric consistency:** compare independent ratings on the same version and actual evidence. Agreement is not validity; disagreement can expose unclear criteria or genuine borderline work.
- **Fairness:** investigate access and interpretation differences with adequate evidence and privacy protection. Do not certify subgroup fairness from wording checks or unstable tiny-group statistics.
- **Change/transfer:** parallel tasks, timing, practice exposure, assistance, attrition and opportunity to perform matter. A posttest shows current performance; a before/after change alone does not establish cause.

Return observed counts/status, interpretation, uncertainty and one actionable revision. Don't calculate reliability, discrimination, calibrated difficulty or learning gains from an authored bank with no response data.
