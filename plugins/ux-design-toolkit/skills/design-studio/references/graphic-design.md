# Shared graphic principles and Graphic Design Studio handoff

Graphic Design Studio owns posters, flyers, campaigns, compositing, format adaptation and print production. Keep that package separate and usable directly. This reference brings its relevant craft principles into product UI and defines a handoff, rather than creating another full graphic production workflow. For a graphic-only task, load that specialist without routing through product/web guidance.

Clarify the medium, audience, venue, dimensions, essential facts, brand, and call to action. A poster seen across a room needs a stronger reading order than a social tile seen on a phone. Establish a visible hierarchy: event or offer, emotional hook, date/place or key terms, and one action. Preserve exact prices, times, names, capacity, and restrictions from the current authoritative source; do not invent urgency or availability.

Compose for the final field of view. Check first-glance legibility, type contrast, line length, spacing, alignment, image focal point, and safe margins. Use imagery because it carries the tone or explains the offering, not to fill space. Keep critical words out of busy image areas. Give the main action a clear home, and avoid making a QR code the only route for people viewing a digital image or using assistive technology.

For assets, record whether each is original, supplied, generated, licensed, or a historical reference. Maintain a clean master for each size. Adapt layouts to each format rather than stretching a single canvas; check cropping, effective image resolution, and small-type readability at actual output size. A screen preview does not prove print quality.

For print, obtain printer-specific bleed, trim, color, format, and resolution requirements. Inspect the exported file, not just the editor view. Check that important content stays inside safe space; fonts render; images are sharp enough; and the exported dimensions match the order. If a QR code appears, decode the exported code and open its actual destination. Confirm the destination is the intended current page, not just a syntactically valid URL. For digital output, inspect mobile-size reading and any platform crop.

Use the current specialist graphic-design and print provider skills for production mechanics and preflight tools. The outcome is the visually checked deliverable in its requested format, with an honest statement of which physical or platform checks remain unperformed.
