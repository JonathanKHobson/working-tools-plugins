# Research as an input to design

For standardized questionnaires or feedback collection, route instrument design, coding and exports to the separate **Survey Studio** plugin (`survey-strategist`). Resolve its installed skill first; resolve the maintained skill in the separately installed Survey Studio plugin. If unavailable, state the missing dependency rather than silently building another survey system. Pass the decision, population, experience window, required measures, privacy constraints and destination. Bring back the canonical instrument, analysis map and QA status. UX Design Studio owns presentation/interaction review; Research Methodology owns method fit and evidence interpretation.

Use research when a material design decision depends on an unresolved user, problem or market assumption. Start from the question and the decision it will change. Separate observed behavior, participant statements, interpretation, competing explanations and proposed design. A persuasive persona or synthetic scenario is not participant evidence.

Choose the smallest method that can answer the question. Investigate needs and language through suitable qualitative evidence; evaluate task completion through usability work; use behavioral data for actual usage patterns. Competitor and market evidence can inform alternatives, positioning and opportunity, but cannot prove that this interface works for its users. Specify participants or segment, context, source dates, coverage limits, analysis approach and the observation that would change the decision.

Load the current research-methodology/evidence-review specialist for study or evidence work. Keep project-specific research in its owning package and preserve source references. Use current-social-research only when current public discourse is relevant, and keep that evidence distinct from representative user research. Use market-sizing for an opportunity estimate with traceable units, assumptions, calculation chains and ranges; use marketing strategy for positioning/audience decisions. Do not run a full market study as an automatic design preflight.

When supplied ICP or audience evidence informs a design task, retain its project profile ID, version, operating status, sources and claim limits, and distinguish the intended buyer, user or beneficiary. `marketing-strategy-studio:marketing-strategy-operator` owns commercial fit. A buyer-task test can observe whether someone finds the offer, understands its value, inspects proof and locates the next step; comprehension is not purchasing demand or market validation. Use existing study methods without requiring an ICP or extra records for routine design work.

Carry a small evidence-to-decision record into UI work: source or observation, confidence/limit, implication for the user's task, proposed change and test. Keep unsupported proposals explicitly provisional. Research follow-up writing is a separate communication step; drafting does not send anything or create approval.

Verify references against their actual source, check that the method answers the question, and test important contradictory evidence. For quantitative estimates, check units and one reproducible calculation; for a design hypothesis, state and exercise the relevant user task when feasible. Report where evidence ends rather than converting assumptions into facts.

For method selection: observed task friction suggests usability testing; navigation/category uncertainty suggests tree testing or card sorting; motivations and context suggest recent-event interviews or observation; prevalence requires suitable sampling and quantitative evidence; change over time may require diary/longitudinal work. Choose sample and method for the question and population rather than using a fixed participant count. A second method is useful only when it reduces a material uncertainty. Historical [UX Oracle notes](../../../libraries/ux-oracle/references/methods-deep.md) are optional background; their fixed sample sizes, mandatory method stacks and output templates are not current rules.
