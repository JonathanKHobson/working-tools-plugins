# Writing voices in product copy

Use a writing voice only when it helps the reader's actual task. Writing Quality Studio's `writing-voice-library` owns selection, full guides and synthetic examples; its `voice-and-style` owner handles an authorized personal or brand voice. Retrieve the chosen record from that library instead of copying the corpus into UX Design Studio. If unavailable, work from the brief and existing product language.

Keep the control's action, object, consequence and actual state literal. A patient coaching voice may suit onboarding; plain-language service may suit help; technical reference may suit expert lookup. Short labels need not exhibit a prose fingerprint. Errors and serious incidents suppress comic, lyrical or promotional framing. Preserve exact interface labels, quantities, uncertainty, consent and permission boundaries.

Select one base and only the modules a task needs. Distinguish retained bases, candidates, modules, aliases and merged extensions through the canonical record; an alias is not a new independent voice. Read the complete selected guide and a relevant example. Examples demonstrate a mechanism; they supply no customer evidence or approved product claims. Voice separation and human preference remain unvalidated.

Humanizer or No AI Slop review may flag unclear agency, empty claims or repetitive scaffolding. Fix the concrete reader cost, keep useful conventions and purposeful style, and never infer authorship. UX Writer still owns copy correctness, interaction fit and accessible wording; the design owner verifies the rendered state.
