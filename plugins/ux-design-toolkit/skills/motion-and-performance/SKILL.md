# Motion and performance

When orientation helps, briefly name this skill’s outcome, needed input and expected result, then proceed. Broader help uses the [plugin introduction](../../README.md); skip maintainer notes unless needed.


For implementation choice or a supplied technique/tool research report, use [motion technique selection](references/motion-technique-selection.md). It checks CSS/WAAPI, springs, shared-element transitions, SVG paths, masks and particle tradeoffs against behavior rather than fixed tool, timing or node-count rules.

For card/dock motion, kinetic type, reveals, perspective layers, focus bands, flow pulses or particle marks, use [motion pattern web adaptation](references/motion-pattern-web-adaptation.md) for usable states, stable alternatives and checks, with Graphic Design's original recipes when art direction is needed.

For reference-inspired website animation, product-demo prompts or a Sites motion handoff, read [motion prompts and Sites](references/motion-prompts-and-sites.md). It turns a selected mechanism into explicit states and bounded checks while distinguishing real controls from illustrative animation.

Design purposeful UI motion or diagnose rendering performance with reduced-motion and interaction checks.

This is the UX motion-design branch. Own the behavior of interactive state transitions, feedback, interruption, focus and `prefers-reduced-motion` in the actual product. Read the [shared motion-design principles](../design-studio/references/motion-performance.md#shared-motion-design-principles) when the visual sequence also needs art direction. Graphic Design Studio owns styleframes, kinetic type and continuity for a motion piece; Video Production's `create-motion-graphics` owns code-authored scenes, rendering, audio and narration. Do not route a channel intro or narrated explainer here solely because it moves.

## Workflow

1. Identify whether motion communicates state, adds requested expression or addresses a measured performance problem.
2. Read the complete selected playbook. Honor platform conventions and reduced-motion behavior; use profiling evidence for optimization.
3. Test changed interaction timing and responsive behavior. Keep ambitious effects within the requested design and device constraints.

## Deliver and verify

Deliver the motion/performance change with measurements or observed behavior, not an untested “faster” claim.

## Example boundary

Good: preserve focus and reduced motion during a panel transition. Bad: add heavy animation to every hover without a task purpose.

Use the owned native adapter in the runtime guide for detector/live/hook commands; do not execute an upstream downloader or silently register a second hook.
