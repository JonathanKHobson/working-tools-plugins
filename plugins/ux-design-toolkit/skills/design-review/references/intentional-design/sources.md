# Source index

Research access date: **2 October 2026**. IDs are stable throughout this pack. Dates marked “not stated” were not verified; do not invent publication dates from crawl timestamps. This is an annotated index, not a claim that every source received full methodological appraisal. Bibliographic titles identify the works; external full texts are not redistributed.

## Evidence categories

Standards define applicable requirements. Understanding pages are informative explanations. Published experiments support bounded claims about their tasks and populations. Preprints and practice guides inform hypotheses and implementation judgment. Proposed guidance in this pack is original synthesis, not a finding attributed to these sources.

## Search and selection record

The targeted searches combined the screenshot motifs with terms for design homogenization, design fixation, divergent thinking, cultural homogenization, design rationale, dashboard patterns, presentation comprehension, typography, accessibility, card and icon use, brand voice, and agent-skill evaluation. Official standards, originating research, author-hosted papers, institutional abstracts, and first-party design-system documentation were preferred.

This review did not preregister an exhaustive database strategy, screen every possible paper, compute pooled effects, or count the prevalence of these motifs on the web. Search snippets alone were not used as quantitative evidence in the report. Unsupported trend lists and marketing statistics were excluded. Inaccessible alternatives were not substituted for the readable evidence described above.

Some sources are older because the design or accessibility question is foundational. The directly relevant 2026 preprint is treated as exploratory, not stronger simply because it is recent. Published research on a narrow task is not automatically evidence about current production design agents.
