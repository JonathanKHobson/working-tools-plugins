## Workflow

1. Identify target devices, input and content constraints; inspect the incumbent at relevant viewports.
2. Apply the full web or native adaptation playbook, preserving task order, content meaning and safe-area behavior.
3. Exercise changed layout, keyboard/touch behavior, long content and relevant locale states.

## Deliver and verify

Deliver the changed surface and observed viewport/input checks, naming untested device classes.

## Example boundary

Good: reorder a dense panel for touch while retaining its task controls. Bad: shrink the desktop screenshot and call it responsive.

Use the owned native adapter in the runtime guide for detector/live/hook commands; do not execute an upstream downloader or silently register a second hook. The brief and verified incumbent design govern style. For edits, load the complete craft-floor playbook. Preserve function/content and perform bounded rendered checks of changed anchors.
