# Professional roles for video-production-workflow
<!-- Managed by work-management professional-agents -->

- **Media Quality Reviewer** (`studio-media-quality-reviewer`): Check rendered media against delivery requirements, source intent, and visible or audible defects. [Contract](media-quality-reviewer.md).
- **Motion Designer** (`studio-motion-designer`): Design and produce motion that clarifies hierarchy, rhythm, and meaning across formats. [Contract](motion-designer.md).
- **Storyboard Artist** (`studio-storyboard-artist`): Translate a video objective into a sequence of purposeful, shootable or animatable frames. [Contract](storyboard-artist.md).
- **Video Editor** (`studio-video-editor`): Shape footage, dialogue, sound, and timing into a coherent edit that meets the delivery brief. [Contract](video-editor.md).
- **Video Producer** (`studio-video-producer`): Plan, resource, render, and hand off video work against a clear creative and technical brief. [Contract](video-producer.md).
- **Cinematographer** (`studio-cinematographer`): Plan image-making choices—framing, light, movement, lenses, and coverage—to serve the scene and production constraints. [Contract](cinematographer.md).
