# Browser showcase templates

Use [Animos](https://animos.app/) when a short looping showcase of already-owned artwork is the deliverable and a browser template fits the animation slot. This complements the existing motion renderer selection; it is not a default for narrated lessons, complex editing or every video.

1. Define the communication job, owned source images/video, audience, ratio, duration, required resolution and readability. For portfolio/product evidence, keep the actual work legible and accurately represented; do not fabricate a product screen merely to fill a template.
2. Browse the live editor with existing browser tools. Inspect a few appropriate templates, choose one by movement/pacing/crop, and record the selected template URL/name and relevant settings. No API/export automation has been verified; use supported visible controls. A blocked or changed UI requires an honest handoff, not an invented export.
3. Verify the current free/export and use terms before importing assets. As checked 2026-10-04, the homepage advertises all templates and 720p exports without an account, with HD paid. Do not buy Pro, downsample a required HD delivery silently, or upscale 720p and call it native HD. If free output cannot meet the brief, use an existing suitable local renderer.
4. The publisher says media remains in the browser. This has not been independently network-audited. Use public/non-sensitive owned assets by default; for confidential client material verify actual processing/retention and existing authorization before use. Public template access does not establish permission to extract/resell its source. Keep the original editable artwork and use licensed export rights; check unclear terms before external delivery.
5. Import the appropriate assets through authorized browser/file controls, adjust crop/margins, movement, speed and background to the story, and preview a full loop. Check readable dwell time, clipping and motion intensity. For a static placement, use the original still or an appropriate poster frame rather than forcing animation.
6. Export via the visible supported flow, verify the actual saved file's codec, dimensions, frame rate and duration, and inspect first/middle/final frames plus the loop seam. Keep a rendered evidence receipt. Normalize for the editorial manifest and pass through validate-video-quality before calling it final. Exporting/previewing does not publish it.

Preserve source media, settings and a reproducible brief locally because the service says it does not keep projects on its servers. Do not promise native editable project export without verifying the feature. Prefer brief/manifest entries over a new mandatory artifact bundle.

Source: [Animos homepage and FAQ](https://animos.app/), checked 2026-10-04. Current pricing, privacy and export statements are publisher claims; the editor was subsequently browsed with stock previews, but uploads, export success and network privacy remain untested. ## Browsed editor choices (2026-10-04)

[The editor](https://animos.app/editor) loaded without sign-in after declining optional analytics. Its visible categories included 3D & Perspective, Multiscene, Isometric, Orbit, Carousel & Flow, Grid, Spotlight & Focus, Reveal & Wipe, and Stack & Scatter. The live category counts exceeded the homepage's older 25-template description; consult the current editor rather than treating that marketing count as the catalog.

Choose by the communication job:
- A broad collection of portfolio/UI screens: compare Grid, Carousel/Film Strip or Isometric choices; maintain recognizable screen content.
- One focal design or a before/after explanation: inspect Spotlight/Focus or Reveal/Wipe; preserve enough dwell time for comparison.
- A short expressive campaign teaser: consider Multiscene, Orbit or Stack/Scatter; avoid movement that obscures the actual work.
- A readable tutorial or detailed walkthrough: a showcase template may be a poor fit; use the existing editorial/motion renderer and narration workflow.

Observed controls included aspect ratios (landscape/square/portrait), media slots and reordering, loop duration, text/logo, background, keyframes and template-specific geometry. The default Showcase Stream preview arranged stock artwork around a tilted ring. These controls and sample output establish available editing affordances, not a tested production export. No personal media was uploaded during inspection. Do not use stock preview artwork as original authored work.
