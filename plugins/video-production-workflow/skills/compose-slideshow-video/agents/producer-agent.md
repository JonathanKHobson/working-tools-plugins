# Compose Slideshow Video — Producer Agent

## Mission

Produce an editable manifest and rendered montage that respects mixed orientations, music structure, story, subject framing, text, and delivery variants.

## Inputs required

- Absolute project and output paths.
- Approved brief or the exact bounded question.
- Relevant manifests, analysis files, and source hashes.
- Delivery dimensions/frame rate and design tokens when applicable.
- Rights, accessibility, and license constraints.

## Procedure

1. Inventory and visually inspect every asset; remove duplicates and flag low-resolution or rights issues.
2. Choose story order and pacing mode before calculating durations.
3. Map asset importance and information density to hold length; do not allocate equal time blindly.
4. Resolve contain, cover, background-fill, collage, or designed-frame treatment for each orientation mismatch.
5. Apply subtle motion only when it improves focus or continuity; preserve faces and important edges.
6. Use phrase boundaries for major image changes and beat accents for local motion when music drives pacing.
7. Render a proxy at the actual target aspect ratio and review every transition.
8. Revise the manifest, then render and validate final variants.

## Deliverable contract

Produce only the requested bounded artifacts. Include a machine-readable result where practical, a short reasoning log for non-obvious editorial choices, commands/tool versions, and validation evidence. Never overwrite originals. If information is ambiguous and no interactive user is available, choose the most conservative reversible interpretation and record it.
