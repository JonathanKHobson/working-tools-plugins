# Article or source to a concise video

Use only when a video or motion companion is requested or chosen for the explanation. This conditional route joins existing storyboard, motion and assembly stages; it does not require another format, new renderer or publication. Begin with Graphic Design's [source-to-visual brief](../../../docs/OPTIONAL_INTEGRATIONS.md) when format or evidence selection is still open.

## Bind the source to the story

Reuse the project's brief or manifest. Record the actual source URL/path, article revision/date or file hash, intended audience, one takeaway, placement/aspect/runtime, approved identity, and attribution. Read the relevant full source, not just a title or snippet. An authored article is evidence of its author's argument; it does not independently prove every assertion it makes. Preserve measured, inferred, illustrative and uncertain statements distinctly.

Before shots, map each selected claim to a page, section, figure or record anchor and to its caveat. Choose a self-contained visual argument rather than reading every paragraph aloud. Keep any qualification that changes the conclusion in the video itself. If a source changes after planning, recheck affected script, chart, shot and caption spans; do not silently retain a stale conclusion.

A small table in the existing storyboard is sufficient: beat/time → claim and source anchor → visual or spoken representation → caveat/illustration label. Unsupported details can be resolved, omitted or explicitly labeled; never fabricate data, quotations, outcomes or causal arrows to make an attractive animation.

## Route the chosen treatment

- **Story:** `design-video-storyboards` makes timed beats and target-aspect compositions. Use drawn boards, generated keyframes or a saved Draw and Think board where useful; those references do not establish motion quality or portable editability.
- **Motion:** `create-motion-graphics` follows its code-authored storytelling or animated-diagram method for transformation, causal explanation or sequential evidence. Preserve source units, scales, direction, uncertainty and semantic timing. Motion must clarify what the source says.
- **Assembly:** `compose-slideshow-video` assembles approved diagrams, images and short clips; `edit-dialogue-footage` owns recorded narration or presenter edits. Use these existing pipelines rather than adding an article-specific rendering engine. A still image with camera motion is not evidence of an implemented explanatory mechanism.
- **Text and sound:** existing typography/audio owners preserve readable holds, intelligible speech, captions and meaningful visual explanation in narration or accompanying text. Keep a transcript and output-timed captions where speech is present; for silent films provide the meaningful text/visual equivalent. Review timing against the actual render.

For personal voice, use the user's authorized recording or explicitly authorized voice/likeness treatment. An article by the user does not by itself authorize voice cloning, a synthetic impersonation or invented personal testimony. Use text-only or a permitted generic treatment when personal media is not available; ask only if missing authorization is necessary for the requested result.

## Review and editable delivery

Check the final sequence against source anchors: labels, quantities, attribution, before/after, ordering and caveats. Inspect target-size frames and the actual temporal transitions; review speech/caption alignment and audio through the existing quality owner. Technical success is not evidence fidelity. If an actual render or listening check was not performed, state that limit.

Deliver the requested render alongside the real project/timeline or code, required assets/fonts/dependencies, transcript and captions. Apply Work Management's [editability handoff](../../../docs/OPTIONAL_INTEGRATIONS.md): distinguish editable text, data, layer timing and effects in the master from baked media in MP4. Identify flattened diagrams, unavailable fonts/plugins and external services; do not infer an editable timeline from the extension or a still preview. Verify a representative edit/reopen in the destination tool when that editability is part of the requested outcome.

For an article/Substack companion, preserve the article revision/link and hand the reviewed assets to Social Content's existing editorial/release owner. Prepared, reviewed, uploaded and published are separate states. This method update or a request to create a video does not authorize a new content queue, upload or publication.

This is an original cross-studio method informed by public Skillry capability descriptions. It imports no Skillry implementation, dependency, service or paid execution path.
