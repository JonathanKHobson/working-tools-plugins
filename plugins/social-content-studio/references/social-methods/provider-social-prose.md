# Social prose revision

This complete Studio method serves drafting, channel adaptation and editorial review. The historical filename remains a compatible local link; no separate writing plugin is required.

## Establish the job

Read the source, intended audience, chosen channel and requested action. Separate verified facts, the author's opinions and proposed claims. Preserve the author's actual contribution and publication state. Use supplied voice samples when present; otherwise follow stated preferences without inventing a personality or personal experience. Load the [post draft gate](post-draft-gate.md) and [voice guidance](voice-files.md) for their applicable checks.

## Write or revise

1. State one useful idea and the evidence that earns it. Choose an opening that helps this reader recognize the topic; neither a dramatic hook nor a question is mandatory.
2. Keep names, quantities, conditions, uncertainty and chronology attached to their claims. Replace generic praise with supported specifics. Delete unsupported specifics rather than adding a plausible anecdote.
3. Preserve characteristic vocabulary and perspective. Replace repetitive transitions, duplicated conclusions and abstract filler only where clarity improves. Vary sentence length according to meaning, not a target randomness score.
4. Give each paragraph one job. Use line breaks for scanning without turning every short clause into a separate paragraph. Read the exact draft aloud or perform a silent cadence pass for awkward repetition and ambiguous references.
5. Adapt the structure to the requested format. A professional post connects an observation to evidence and a useful implication. A short post preserves the central claim and its qualifier. A thread or carousel gives each unit a purpose and keeps caveats beside the relevant claim. A reply answers the actual point before expanding. A script separates spoken words, visuals and on-screen text.
6. Use only a justified next action. An invitation needs a real destination or available offer. A factual observation may simply end. Hashtags, emoji and disclosure follow author preferences and actual platform requirements, not a fixed formula.
7. Check target limits using a current tool or authoritative platform reference if exact limits matter. Count the exact submitted text, including links where the platform's counting rule applies. If limits are unverified, state that rather than claiming compliance.

## Verify and deliver

Compare the revision against the source sentence by sentence for changed claims, removed qualifiers, unintended sentiment and private details. Verify links and promised resources before an authorized publication. For draft work, distinguish unchecked destinations from ready copy. Provide the requested copy first, with only material editorial notes outside it. No detector score establishes human authorship, quality or an obligation to rewrite.

Acceptance example: source says a prototype was tested with three colleagues. A revision may say that three colleagues tried the prototype; it may not claim customer validation, a shipped product or improved outcomes. For a missing event URL, omit the invitation or label it as a proposed editorial option. For a multilingual request, preserve register and meaning and flag uncertain idioms rather than substituting a fabricated cultural voice.

Drafting, rendered preview, scheduling and live publication are separate states. Use the existing preview and publishing skills for external actions; this method neither posts nor installs anything.
