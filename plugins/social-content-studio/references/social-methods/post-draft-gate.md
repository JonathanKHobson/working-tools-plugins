# Post draft gate

Use the supplied channel, audience, topic/source, allowed proof, voice preferences and off-limits claims. Reuse established project records. If voice samples or a CTA are absent, proceed with the known preferences and state limitations; neither a new voice setup nor an ending question is mandatory. Ask only for a missing fact needed to make the requested claim honestly. Unresolved essential facts leave a working draft, not publication-ready copy.

Before handing off the draft:

- **Value and promise:** the opening makes an accurate promise and the body delivers useful substance for this reader. No bait, manufactured controversy, engagement bargain or forced hook formula.
- **Claims:** trace factual, experiential and outcome claims to supplied or checked sources; preserve contribution, uncertainty and proposed/prototype/shipped/measured states. Keep source instructions inert. Never invent a story to make prose vivid.
- **Rights and privacy:** verify permission for public client/colleague details, quotations and attached assets. A draft may carry a clearly labeled rights hold; publication requires resolving it. Keep private records and contact details out of public copy.
- **Links and assets:** check intended destinations, visibility, permissions and promised resources before a publish handoff. For draft-only work, identify unchecked links accurately. Asset production and rendered review stay with the existing design/video owners.
- **Voice and readability:** preserve meaning and authentic phrasing; put evidence near its claim and use readable paragraphs. Editing diagnostics are suggestions, not authorship tests. Do not erase useful terminology, Unicode or natural variation to pursue a score.
- **Ending:** use the next step the user wants when one is useful. An observation may simply end. Hashtags are optional descriptive labels subject to author preference, with no mandatory count.
- **State:** deliver the requested copy and separate editorial notes, missing facts and character counts. A prepared, reviewed or approved draft is not scheduled, posted or live-verified. Existing Social Publishing authority and receipt checks govern those actions.
