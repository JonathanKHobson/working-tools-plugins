# LinkedIn posts and hooks

Use for one idea, a complete post, hook alternatives, or a named li-post/li-hooks request. [Source and research](../../libraries/linkedin-agent/PROVENANCE.md). Drafting is separate from publication; current authorization and Social Publishing's account/state checks govern external actions. No posting tool, bot or new provider is introduced by this method.

## Choose the evidence and voice

Use the real reader, desired outcome, one specific claim and its source. Read [voice files](voice-files.md) and the relevant existing private voice profile. A current user sample outranks old preferences; an AI draft or reshare does not prove personal voice. Without samples, use the supplied preferences and disclose that exact voice matching is unverified; do not make onboarding mandatory. For a thin prompt, use a defensible teaching example or ask one question about what actually happened. Never invent a firsthand incident, number, opinion or emotion.

Separate source facts from the author's interpretation. For proof-led content, use Career's evidence-story-bank when needed; preserve contribution and proposed/shipped/measured labels. A truthful process/artifact can be the proof when impact is unknown. Mark working placeholders outside final-ready state, and never publish them.

## Pick an opening and deliver its promise

Read [21 hook forms](hook-formulas.json): each has an ID, template, illustrative example, use and failure mode. They are craft options, not validated algorithm winners. Choose three meaningfully different forms when hook options are requested or useful for a new full post. Keep every option within the same facts; reject a formula if it needs an unsupported number, invented confession, fake contrarian belief or fabricated quote. State one recommendation briefly. Do not turn a tiny edit into three variants.

Write one full draft using the chosen opening. Let the first sentence work by itself and pay off its promise promptly. Preserve the argument in short readable paragraphs, a meaningful example and a useful ending. A question is optional; use one only when its answer genuinely matters. Length follows the idea and reader; 900–1,300 characters is an optional starting range, not a quality test. Short observations and substantial articles are valid. Verify actual composer limits and truncation when publishing; no fixed mobile character count is guaranteed.

Useful forms include a real observation, evidence/limitation, a small teachable procedure, a documented decision, a source-grounded disagreement, or an honest offer. Do not force every post into hook → contrarian turn → question. Vary compatible structures across a series while retaining the author's voice. A result without a defensible causal basis remains an observation.

## Review and output

Use Writing Quality's natural-prose-editor for meaning/voice checks and its local-text-hygiene reference when deterministic cleanup or a batch diagnostic helps. The checks are editing cues, not AI detection. Never add numbers/contractions or strip meaningful characters to improve a score. Respect the author's explicit typography preferences through a reviewed rewrite, especially punctuation that affects meaning.

Run [post draft gate](post-draft-gate.md): factual support, rights/privacy, accurate links, readability, nonmanipulative attention, and clear reader value. Keep links where the reader needs them; there is no blanket first-comment rule or guarantee that links suppress reach. Hashtags are optional descriptive labels, not a mandatory count. No comment-for-keyword gate when the resource can simply be provided.

Return requested hook options, recommended choice, one copy-ready block, exact character count, source/claim notes, and actual state. Do not put internal evidence IDs or editorial instructions in the copy block. A drafting approval is not publication evidence; save state as draft/approved when appropriate and log posted/scheduled only from the actual receipt. Existing calendar policy wins over source cadence suggestions. Do not create `~/.claude/linkedin` or overwrite a shared plan.

## Reverse-engineer successful openings

For a hooks-analysis request, use supplied posts plus comparable dated metrics. Identify the actual promise, specificity, audience, tension and payoff; connect to a form only where helpful. Distinguish a visible writing feature from an inferred cause of performance. Extract the reusable mechanism without copying another author's distinctive language or claims. Test one variable on the user's next truthful draft; do not declare a universal winner from a handful of posts.
