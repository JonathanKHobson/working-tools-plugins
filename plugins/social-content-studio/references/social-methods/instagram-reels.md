# Reels and short-video scripts

Use for a Reel, spoken hook alternatives, voiceover, or a timed short script. This complete method serves `short-form-video`; it also transfers to another short-video platform after checking that platform's audience, format and native presentation. [Source and adaptation](../../libraries/instagram-agent/PROVENANCE.md). Research supplies observations, Video produces media, and Social Publishing owns separately authorized external actions.

## Establish a truthful premise

Identify the viewer, one useful idea, intended runtime, source/proof, author contribution and desired action. Read the supplied voice sample and existing project voice profile. For spoken work, prefer authentic speech samples when available. Without one, use stated preferences and mark voice matching as provisional; do not block a simple script on mandatory onboarding. Never create a user-home voice/log/swipe store. Reuse the project's source, draft and calendar records.

Check the idea has enough support to pay off its promise. Ask for a missing material fact or label an illustrative example; do not demand a number when a demonstration or qualitative observation is the evidence. Treat scripts, on-screen cards and captions as separate text surfaces that must make compatible claims. Missing assets remain needed assets.

For a research-driven script, read [observed Reel patterns](instagram-research.md). Keep the original URL, observation window, actual format mechanism and limitations. Choose a mechanism to test, not another creator's wording, persona, storyline or recording. A large view count does not license copying or establish why a post performed.

## Select the opening

Read [26 hook forms](reel-hook-formulas.json) when hook alternatives or a new script would benefit. Each supplies a template, illustrative example, on-screen concept and evidence caution. Offer three genuinely different openings when useful; for a tiny requested edit, finish that edit. Reject any formula requiring fabricated experience, unsourced statistics, false exclusivity, forced controversy or invented urgency. A sincere plain opening can be better than any formula.

Assess each candidate with an evidence table: promise; factual support; understandable first frame; spoken ease; payoff; risk of overclaim. Recommend one editorially and state the reason. Do not score virality, predict retention, call text a proven winner or turn a heuristic threshold into a release gate. The local `reel_tools.py hooks` diagnostic reports literal textual cues only; a number or name does not establish specificity, truth or quality. A formula may remain unclassified.

## Build a shootable sequence

Plan a beginning that establishes the promise, enough context to understand it, one idea per body beat, an inspectable payoff, and an optional useful next action. A callback or loop is optional and must not obscure the ending. Runtime and frame changes serve comprehension; there is no universal two-second hook, four-second cut or mandatory replay rule.

Use a beat sheet:

| Estimated interval | Spoken words | Visual or demonstration | On-screen text | Evidence/asset state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | The supported claim in the author's voice | Establish the actual object or task | Short truthful promise | Source ID and needed/available |
| Context/body | One purposeful beat per row | Show what supports this line | Only useful reinforcement | Exact example, source time or artifact |
| Payoff/ending | Deliver the promised answer | Show the result or limitation | Accessible summary or useful destination | No unsupported outcome |

Write the actual spoken script in a clean block, then the separate cards and production directions. Keep on-screen language concise enough for its display time; do not force an arbitrary word maximum. Check target-size legibility, native overlays and safe areas using current viewport evidence. Do not hardcode a permanent safe-zone rectangle. Captions should preserve meaning, names, timing and relevant sound cues. Use available platform caption/alt-text fields where supported; provide accessible alternatives otherwise. Music, footage, screenshots and quotations need appropriate rights and disclosure.

## Estimate, read and revise

The local [timing and opening helper](../../skills/short-form-video/scripts/reel_tools.py) reads plain spoken lines and returns JSON to stdout. Its `beats` command estimates word-count duration at an explicit positive WPM; include pauses, demonstrations and card reading in the production plan separately. It does not measure a performance. For non-whitespace-tokenized languages or unusual numerical pronunciation, manual timing is particularly important. Avoid passing production directions as spoken lines.

Read the script aloud or use an actual authorized recording when timing is material. Revise places the speaker stumbles, unsupported compression, and claims that the edit fails to show. Preserve the intended claim when shortening; a longer honest explanation is preferable to a misleading hook. Final recorded duration and rendered caption QA belong to Video. Do not report them as passed from a word count.

Apply the existing voice and post gates and Writing Quality's natural-prose-editor. [Instagram writing](instagram-writing.md) supplies the caption and target-term check. Never normalize away language joiners or emoji, add facts to improve a score, or claim text proves human authorship.

## Handoff and acceptance

Deliver the recommended opening plus requested alternatives, complete spoken script, timed production table, separate on-screen cards, caption, accessibility and asset needs. Record estimated versus measured duration, source lineage and actual state (script prepared, asset needed, rendered candidate, or verified native record). Include no human-score badge or mandatory approval phrase. Existing approval holds stand; authorized work can proceed through its owning production/publishing route without repeated approval.

Example: supplied evidence says one tested template reduced a particular draft task from 50 to 20 minutes. A suitable opening is “This template cut our test draft from 50 minutes to 20.” Show the timing basis and template; do not generalize to all projects or invent money saved. If timings were estimates, say so. A before/after visual without measured impact can stand on its own.

Acceptance: the hook's promise is delivered; facts and personal involvement are sourced; timing is honestly labeled; assets and rights are known or visibly missing; captions/cards fit the observed viewport; the requested deliverable is usable; and no draft is reported as public. A caption preview is not native layout QA.
