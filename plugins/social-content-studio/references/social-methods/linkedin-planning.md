# LinkedIn week: sustainable proof and useful conversations

Use for li-plan intent and LinkedIn calendar selection; retain the full [connected publishing](publishing-ecosystem.md), [account learning](account-learning.md) and [content matrix](content-matrix.md) methods. [Source](../../libraries/linkedin-agent/PROVENANCE.md).

Read the existing project policy, voice, source inventory, native queue receipts and publication log before picking slots. Identify the current goal, audience, time budget and real developments. Career traction, consulting enquiries, teaching partnerships and peer relationships may need different proof; choose one emphasis rather than promising all of them in every post. A current plan beats the upstream four-post default. Treat cadence and times as experiments with the audience's timezone; keep saved Phoenix/local distinctions explicit. Never replace an established shared calendar with a second plan.

Select grounded angles across useful proof, teaching, opinion, story and occasional offer. Not every week needs every type. Each candidate records content ID, specific angle, evidence/source span, claim status, reader benefit/action, format, hook form if useful, needed asset, source dependencies, owner, proposed date/timezone and actual state. Drop weak items rather than filling a quota. Avoid repeating the same claim merely because format changed. Preserve existing reservations and schedules; a local reservation is not a platform receipt.

Use requested analytics through the performance method. Choose one small test (opening, format, example or CTA) with a target such as qualified replies, relevant visits or enquiries, not a promised causal lift. Set its review date and keep other major variables stable where feasible. A no-post week can be the correct result when evidence, time or queue capacity is insufficient.

When an engagement shortlist is useful, select up to ten verified people from the existing authorized relationship list or user-supplied candidates. A mix of relevant established voices, peers and prospective partners/buyers is an optional planning aid, not a 5/3/2 quota. For each give a genuine connection, source/post to read, possible contribution and contact/opt-out context. If names are unavailable, provide research criteria rather than invented people or fictitious familiarity. No scraping, contact harvesting, bulk messaging or reciprocal-engagement scheme. A suggestion is not an instruction to publish a comment unattended.

Deliver a feasible weekly view plus concise engagement candidates and next action. Save only to the existing project plan if requested/established; retain identifiers and state rather than overwriting other owners' work. Planning creates neither scheduled posts nor reminders. The project's automation owner handles requested recurring-prompt changes separately.
