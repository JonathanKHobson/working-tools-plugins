# Channel adaptation

Repurpose a grounded source into platform-fit posts, threads, newsletters or carousel copy without changing its claim.

For an authorized course/video/article distribution workflow, use [connected publishing](../../references/social-methods/publishing-ecosystem.md) to retain source lineage, avoid thin duplicate articles and space downstream LinkedIn/Substack variants. The project owns channels and cadence; an attached source alone does not authorize publication.

For Substack craft and editor handoff, read the conditional [Substack procedure](../social-publishing/references/substack-publishing.md); provide a useful standalone article, source-linked resources and purposeful formatting before native scheduling. Its optional source-grounded companion method selects a helpful visual/video or no companion, retains evidence and editable sources, and routes production to the existing Graphic, Video or Data Visualization owner without creating a new queue.

## Workflow

1. Choose source truth, target channels and the reader’s action.
2. Apply the complete channel rules and voice constraints, changing structure and length without inventing new evidence.
3. Check mobile readability, link targets, alt-text requirements and each variant’s claim/proof relationship.

## Deliver and verify

Return ready-to-use channel variants with clear labels and shared source basis.

## Example boundary

Good: turn one evidenced lesson into a LinkedIn narrative and short thread. Bad: inflate results to make the short version more exciting.

Private voice samples, accounts, calendars and analytics belong in the user’s project. Current authorization governs external actions; a request to draft does not authorize posting. Use Research Studio for live social evidence and Marketing for strategy/SEO. Do not claim engagement outcomes without measured data.

Before substantial channel drafting/adaptation, use `writing-quality-studio:voice-and-style` for the shared writing-template catalog and selection/blending rules. Honor a requested template; otherwise vary suitable openings, cadence and information order by reader need and platform. V23 Conversational micro-essay, V17 News brief, V11 Patient coach and V04 Scene-led feature are useful starting points, not a fixed rotation. Kyle’s articles/newsletters keep his voice through most of the body while blending compatible styles; direct correspondence keeps his formal/informal voice throughout. Preserve claims and brand identity across variants; novelty never justifies bait or invented urgency.

For LinkedIn document/carousel posts or a week from one source (li-carousel/li-repurpose intent), read the complete [documents and reuse method](../../references/social-methods/linkedin-documents-and-reuse.md). Finish requested assets/drafts without an artificial approval loop; preserve requested draft-only holds and use actual rendered QA for a PDF.
