# Short form video

Write a social reel or short-video script with spoken lines, visual beats, captions and accessible asset handoff.

## Complete procedures to load

Read the task-matching files below in full before applying that procedure. Load only the relevant variant and its required support. These contain the operative methods, examples and templates; this entrypoint does not replace them. A plugin is a package, not a requirement to read every skill.

- [Channel Adaptation Rules](../../references/social-methods/channel-adaptation-rules.md) — read when changing a source into platform-specific wording or format.
- [Post Draft Gate](../../references/social-methods/post-draft-gate.md) — read when checking factual support, privacy and readiness of post copy.
- [Voice Files](../../references/social-methods/voice-files.md) — read when matching the supplied voice or channel cadence.

## Workflow

1. Set audience, channel, runtime and one supported idea; distinguish spoken content from production directions.
2. Apply the full reel/channel rules. Write a timed beat sequence with actual proof and a clear ending.
3. Check read-aloud pacing, captions and asset needs; route image/video production to Graphic or Video without claiming footage exists.

## Deliver and verify

Deliver script, visual beat list, caption and accessible handoff. Test timing by actual read-through when timing is material.

## Example boundary

Good: mark a demonstration shot as needed until produced. Bad: describe a stock clip as footage from the user’s project.

Private voice samples, accounts, calendars and analytics belong in the user’s project. Current authorization governs external actions; a request to draft does not authorize posting. Use Research Studio for live social evidence and Marketing for strategy/SEO. Do not claim engagement outcomes without measured data.

## Motion-designed originals

For an animated intro, motion-designed explainer or an occasional animation-led post, route production to `video-production-workflow:create-motion-graphics` and its **code-authored motion storytelling** workflow. `graphic-design-studio:motion-design` owns composition, kinetic type and visual continuity. The short may be silent, sound-led or built under authentic recorded narration; never imply that a narration mix has passed because a silent example was approved. Social retains source-faithful hooks/post copy, cover-versus-caption distinctions and accessible handoff.

An original platform-only video can have its own authorized release basis; do not invent a YouTube dependency for a standalone work. For shared-source derivatives, retain the existing source-publication dependency. Record the variant/output hash, approval, actual audio/caption QA and intended channel in the owning project. Occasional motion posts use that project's existing cadence and queue policy; the method does not add posts, move scheduled work or authorize uploading. Scheduling and publication remain with `social-publishing` and separate native receipts.
