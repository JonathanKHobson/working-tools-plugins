# Professional social proof

Package verified work into social proof campaigns and public derivatives with assets, links and follow-up tracking; Career owns LinkedIn profile positioning and section audits.

## Complete procedures to load

Read the task-matching files below in full before applying that procedure. Load only the relevant variant and its required support. These contain the operative methods, examples and templates; this entrypoint does not replace them. A plugin is a package, not a requirement to read every skill.

- [Voice Files](../../references/social-methods/voice-files.md) — read when matching the supplied voice or channel cadence.
- [Channel Adaptation Rules](../../references/social-methods/channel-adaptation-rules.md) — read when changing a source into platform-specific wording or format.

## Workflow

1. Identify the proof object, public-safe facts and actual destination links.
2. Use the full campaign template: copy, asset order, alt text, reply bank and tracker. Use Career Studio for resume/application claims.
3. Verify public disclosure boundaries and actual asset state; keep profile edits and publishing within authorized scope.

## Deliver and verify

Return the complete campaign or requested profile copy plus its source and state record.

## Example boundary

Good: link the delivered artifact and explain the supported contribution. Bad: imply a proposal shipped or a client endorsed it.

Private voice samples, accounts, calendars and analytics belong in the user’s project. Current authorization governs external actions; a request to draft does not authorize posting. Use Research Studio for live social evidence and Marketing for strategy/SEO. Do not claim engagement outcomes without measured data.

Career Studio's resume skill owns complete LinkedIn profile scoring, professional positioning and section rewrites. Use its current profile method when those are the requested outcome, then return here for proof campaigns and public derivatives. Reuse the private career evidence/story record rather than constructing another biography.
