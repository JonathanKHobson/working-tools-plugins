# Social editorial review

Review social copy for source support, voice, channel fit, privacy, readability and actual publication readiness.

## Complete procedures to load

Read the task-matching files below in full before applying that procedure. Load only the relevant variant and its required support. These contain the operative methods, examples and templates; this entrypoint does not replace them. A plugin is a package, not a requirement to read every skill.

- [Scoring And Linting](../../references/social-methods/scoring-and-linting.md) — read for an editorial or readiness audit with its stated limits.
- [Post Draft Gate](../../references/social-methods/post-draft-gate.md) — read when checking factual support, privacy and readiness of post copy.
- [Output Contract](../../references/social-methods/output-contract.md) — read when reporting a reviewed draft and publication status.
- [Social prose revision](../../references/social-methods/provider-social-prose.md): apply the complete included method for voice, structure, format adaptation and claim-preserving revision.

## Workflow

1. Read the draft, supplied evidence and intended channel before scoring.
2. Apply `writing-quality-studio:natural-prose-editor` to the exact final copy; substantial articles/newsletters also use its editorial gate. Its correspondence house style covers newsletter email editions. Then apply the complete scoring/lint method and relevant full social prose procedure. Distinguish observed issues from subjective preference.
3. Correct only within requested scope. Use supplied analytics with dates and denominators; otherwise make an editorial assessment without numerical reach claims.

## Deliver and verify

Return usable corrections or prioritized findings, evidence gaps and the actual readiness state.

## Example boundary

Good: flag an unsupported customer metric with the exact sentence. Bad: promise virality or label writing AI-generated from style alone.

Private voice samples, accounts, calendars and analytics belong in the user’s project. Current authorization governs external actions; a request to draft does not authorize posting. Use Research Studio for live social evidence and Marketing for strategy/SEO. Do not claim engagement outcomes without measured data.

For a template-based draft, apply `writing-quality-studio:voice-and-style` only as needed to check its selected mechanics and voice continuity. Preserve purposeful variation and reject unsupported attention hooks. Do not mark an intentional style shift as an error merely because adjacent sections have different cadence.

For published-post results rather than draft quality, use content-calendar's complete [performance method](../../references/social-methods/linkedin-performance.md). For local text/Unicode checks, use Writing Quality natural-prose-editor's local-text-hygiene method. No editorial score proves human authorship or predicts reach.
