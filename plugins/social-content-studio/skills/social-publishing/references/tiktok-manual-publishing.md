# TikTok production handoff and attended native scheduling

Read for an authorized TikTok candidate, curated backfill or native future schedule.
Use the existing source inventory, channel policy and release ledger. This method
adds no posting API, external scheduler service, bot, unattended UI loop or automatic LinkedIn fanout.
A prepared TikTok candidate is useful even when publication is held.

## Authority and evidence

Standing user policy requires TikTok video posts to be scheduled out through TikTok's
own native feature. Immediate/Now publication is prohibited. The user or Codex may
select the exact file, upload, review and save a future native schedule in an attended
browser session when authorized. Schedule at most one TikTok video per calendar day
in the account timezone recorded by the project. This is house policy, not a
platform maximum; use the project's IANA timezone rather than assume browser local
time. A preparation/approval hold still governs. No source
attachment, new Short or reusable method grants account action authority by itself.
For shared-source derivatives, verify the upstream source is already publicly
available at its actual URL before the TikTok scheduling action; a future source
schedule, prepared file or unlisted source does not satisfy that public dependency.
Preserve existing published posts and unrelated native records.

Keep three evidence classes distinct:

- **Documented platform behavior:** official sources below, with retrieval date and
  stated limitations. Documentation is not proof of account eligibility.
- **House craft:** selection, clean-master reuse, packaging and QA choices in this
  method. These are communication practices, not ranking guarantees.
- **Account capability:** dated observations in the owning project: account/type,
  region, surface/device, visible controls, accepted limits and receipt evidence.
  Unknown values stay unknown. Never turn one account's numeric limits into a
  universal native rule or borrow an API/ad specification for an organic upload.

## Assess each Short and curate backfill

Whenever an authorized Short is made, assess a **TikTok candidate** in the existing
handoff: selected / defer / unsuitable, with a reason, source/output hash, standalone
payoff, rights state and needed adaptation. This assessment does not require a second
render or a post. For existing work, inspect the requested backlog and publication
history; curate useful clips and supported variants within the requested scope.
Both unchanged source exports and custom TikTok adaptations are supported. Reuse
or overlap is acceptable when authorized; do not claim a reach penalty as fact.

1. Reuse verified transcript, source spans, word alignment, rights and approved assets
   by hash. Establish intended viewer and one complete answer, demonstration or
   decision. Record the actual evidence and caveat that must survive the edit.
2. Check the whole idea: understandable entry, enough context, honest explanation or
   action, visible payoff. Repair “as mentioned earlier” with a wider source range,
   truthful contextual label or authorized pickup; reject it when context cannot be
   restored. Do not fabricate a replacement speaker line or dependent UI action.
3. Judge the source on its merits. No clip quota, fixed duration, compulsory trend,
   hashtag formula or virality score determines selection. Judge repeated or overlapping
   backfill choices against current reuse authority; reject stale claims, misleading
   teasers and a course trailer that withholds the
   useful answer. Respect existing holds and other channels' release plans.
4. Record an optional search question and natural relevant terms only when the source
   answers them. Dated Creator Search Insights or regional trend evidence may inform
   the angle; a content gap or popular sound is not an outcome forecast.
   Keep Studio Inspiration and Creator Search Insights distinct: verify the actual
   research surface per account/device and record its name rather than infer access
   to one from the other.

Video's existing dialogue/course-Shorts owner controls selection, EDL, rendering and
media QA. Social owns cover wording, opening promise, post caption and platform action.
If an approved YouTube Short works unchanged, reuse its clean reviewed master; recut
only for a concrete TikTok readability, context, sound or packaging need.

Keep direct source-export and custom-edit variants under the same stable source ID
with distinct variant IDs, exact media hashes and separate native schedule/public
receipts. Never overwrite a published variant with another rendition. When a source
was posted to another owned account, record that account/post ownership and reuse
basis; ownership does not clear third-party music, imagery or course restrictions.
Keep source accuracy and rights checks. Refine the next authorized edit incrementally;
reuse approval is not permission to revise existing published media.

## Prepare one complete posting payload

Reuse the project record rather than create another manifest system. Carry:

- source ID/version, variant ID/type (direct source export or custom edit), prior
  owned-account reuse pointers and retained intervals; exact reviewed video path/hash, duration,
  dimensions and caption/transcript paths/hashes;
- chosen clean or captioned variant, native finishing still required, voice/music/SFX
  rights, credit, privacy and disclosure decisions;
- **cover:** useful frame timestamp in the final output, short subject wording and
  optional reference still; **hook:** actual first visual/spoken beat that begins
  delivering value; **post caption:** context, attribution and a proportional next
  action. Keep these distinct and aligned rather than repeat one headline three times;
- substantive final post caption with useful context, instructions, a grounded example
  or next step that extends the clip. Preserve necessary attribution/qualifications;
  be thorough where the content warrants it. Use 4–5 relevant hashtags when each is
  justified and accepted by the live composer; this is a house target, not a verified
  universal platform cap. Avoid padding, keyword stuffing and unrelated discovery
  tags. Check the actual accepted text/counter without treating one account's limit
  as universal or claiming that TikTok rewards maximum-length descriptions;
- account/surface, requested visibility, comments/reuse/download choices when present,
  reviewed disclosures, authority reference, shared-source public receipt, proposed
  future date/time/timezone, readiness and final native schedule receipt fields.

A “link in bio” or lesson destination must exist, be usable from this account and be
verified. A caption containing a URL does not establish that it is clickable.
Every TikTok video needs an intentional cover. Reuse approved YouTube cover art by
asset/hash where available, preserving its supported promise and recognizable design.
Review the actual native crop at profile/search size, including readable text and
subject framing; approval of the YouTube art does not validate a new TikTok crop.
Use the supported native cover path, and read back the selected cover. If that path
cannot accept the art, retain the reference and prepare a faithful supported frame
through Video/Graphic rather than claiming the image was applied.

A separate cover JPG/PNG is a reference until the chosen native surface accepts it;
frame selection before posting is the documented baseline. Do not insert an awkward
static opening solely to manufacture a cover, or promise cover editing after posting.
[Official cover guidance](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/editing-posting-and-deleting).

## Preserve the media and check accessibility

Keep the clean master free of another platform's watermark or native sound attachment.
Preserve original source and separable voice/music/SFX when available. A clean variant
for native captions and a locally captioned alternative are choices, not mandatory
exports for every post. Label which exact file will upload.

Captions use the final output timeline after trims, joins and retimes. Review words,
negation, numbers, names, speaker changes and meaningful non-speech audio. Retain the
corrected transcript and SRT for reuse/archive without promising subtitle-file import
in TikTok. If native captions are available, correct them and inspect for duplicate or
obscuring burned text; rendered captions and a native caption track are different.
[Caption accessibility rationale](https://www.w3.org/TR/UNDERSTANDING-WCAG20/media-equiv-captions.html).

Consider native captions for their accessibility function separately from burned text.
Where available, correct names, terms, wording and timing, then verify the actual
native caption behavior and readability. Do not assume burned captions establish
an accessible native track. Record when the needed caption/editing control is
app-only or unavailable on the current account/device, with date and exact surface;
this does not prove a universal inability to edit TikTok posts.

Consider native features for a teaching purpose, not as a checklist. A poll, sticker
or mention is optional when it helps the actual explanation, activity or attribution
and the current surface supports it. Avoid promotional clutter, invented interactivity
or gratuitous tagging; preserve a complete useful video when no native feature helps.


Do rendered visual QA through Video, then native-preview QA through Social. Map 3–8
anchors with ID, source/output time or viewport, region, node/selector where available,
observed fact and intended action. Inspect the opening, cover crop, decisive control
or prompt, dense captions, transitions and ending on a phone-sized view. Keep essential
controls and text readable around actual interface overlays and expanded post text;
no permanent pixel-safe-area template proves every preview. Watch/listen to the exact
final render; honor explicit quiet review and record any listening limitation.
Changing media, native timing or captions invalidates affected QA and hashes.

## Review sound and disclosures

Confirm speech intelligibility and the rights of each music/SFX asset for this use.
A TikTok sound attached natively is distinct from rendered audio. TikTok commercial
music permission does not establish YouTube or other-platform rights. Use a voice-only
version when music is unresolved. Businesses are directed to the Commercial Music
Library for commercial TikTok use or appropriately licensed other sound; inspect the
selected track's region/placement and terms.
[Commercial Music Library](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/commercial-music-library?lang=en).

Review the actual commercial relationship. TikTok's guidance requires disclosure
for promotion of one's own brand/business and third-party incentivized promotion;
Your brand yields Promotional content, third-party branded content Paid partnership.
On the documented web path, Upload → Show more → Disclose post content exposes the
choice; verify current labels and save/read back the actual setting. Do not silently
grant ad/anchor authorization or tag a brand partner as a side effect.
[Commercial disclosure](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service).

Record what AI changed: planning/transcription, minor cleanup, generated imagery,
synthetic voice or substantial realistic alteration. Assess the actual audiovisual
output. TikTok requires labels for realistic AI-generated imagery/audio/video;
routine AI-assisted planning alone does not establish that condition. Review teaching
demos showing realistic generated media. Appropriate labels do not make prohibited
misrepresentation acceptable. Verify the native AI control for the selected surface.
[AI-generated content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content).

## Attend native scheduling and verify it

1. Read the current account identity, publication history and native scheduled queue.
   Reconcile an uncertain previous attempt before upload. For a shared-source post,
   read back the upstream public URL before starting the TikTok scheduling action;
   keep the derivative prepared if its source is not yet public.
2. Inspect the current organic upload and future-scheduling controls. Record only
   needed dated account capability: accepted media, cover/caption controls, visibility,
   interaction/reuse settings, disclosures, link behavior, native scheduling horizon,
   timezone and receipt location. Verify the horizon in the live account; never guess
   it or transfer API/ad limits. Native editing, Smart Split and AI Outline are
   optional and use only authorized media. Their documentation establishes neither
   an external API nor account access.
3. Check the existing native queue and publication history in the account's recorded
   IANA timezone. Choose future slots on different calendar days, with at most one
   TikTok video per day, and apply any additional project spacing. Reuse/overlap is
   allowed when authorized; do not invent a platform penalty or compulsory cooldown. Preserve
   unrelated schedules and existing published posts. A locally reserved slot remains
   queued/prepared until an actual native schedule is saved. If the slot lies outside
   the live native horizon, hold it prepared and reconcile at the next attended
   handoff; do not add an external scheduler, timed UI run or background loop.
4. Select/upload the exact reviewed file. Inspect processing/checks and actual preview.
   Apply final caption, cover, disclosures and requested settings. Native edits need
   renewed preview/timing review. Select future Schedule mode and confirm the intended
   date, time and timezone before the actual save/confirm action. Never use Now or
   immediate Post/Publish as a fallback. If scheduling or a required capability is
   unavailable, preserve the complete prepared handoff and name the exact gap.
5. Read the saved native scheduled record: account, exact content, caption, cover,
   visibility, disclosures, scheduled date/time/timezone and native ID or record URL.
   Confirm it appears in the actual scheduled queue. Upload completion, a success
   toast, a local reservation or a scheduled agent run is not sufficient evidence.
   An uncertain result stays uncertain: inspect the native queue/content records
   before any retry to avoid a duplicate schedule.
6. Verify eventual publication separately at the next authorized attended review:
   read the actual public URL, account, content, caption, cover, visibility,
   disclosures and public playback where feasible. Distinguish visible hashtag text
   from native tag linking/indexing; inspect actual behavior where possible and
   record unavailable verification without claiming discoverability from caption
   text alone. A due date alone does not prove publication. Record missing playback
   or caption evidence and reconcile a late/failed release before changing dependents.
7. Update only this channel's existing ledger and connected index with the native
   schedule receipt and later public receipt for the distinct variant. Do not create a LinkedIn companion
   unless project policy/current authority explicitly selects one. Hand back the
   browser under the host's task-session cleanup rules.

Status distinctions: candidate → prepared/locally queued or reserved → uploaded/native
draft where evidenced → native scheduled → published → publicly verified. Held/deferred,
failed and uncertain remain explicit. A local reservation is not a native schedule;
a native scheduled record is not publication; a past planned date is not a receipt.
Record account, exact selected hash, source public receipt where applicable, native
ID/record URL, future date/time/timezone, authority and evidence time. Publicly verified
requires separate readback at the actual public URL after release.

Follow the owning ledger's schema. Where it uses explicit scheduling states, distinguish
`ready_for_native_scheduling`, `schedule_pending_verification` and `scheduled`. A
scheduled record needs a future `scheduled_at` ISO timestamp with UTC offset, IANA
`timezone`, existing `schedule_receipt` and `public_source_receipt` evidence paths,
and no unresolved hold. Preserve separate public URL/date/readback evidence. Native
scheduled release is allowed; external/API posting and unattended UI action are not.

## Separately authorized pins

Pinning or replacing older pins is a separate native account action and may proceed
when explicitly authorized. Inspect current pins and the exact public variant, apply
the requested change through available controls, then read back the profile's pin
state and post IDs. Preserve the published media and receipts; do not treat a pin
change as a new publication or infer an engagement benefit. Keep chosen pins and
current authorization in the project rather than encode account IDs in this method.

## Learn from observed outcomes

During the next authorized review, collect available native metrics with post ID,
observation date, post age, duration/format, window, traffic source and definitions.
Missing values are null/unavailable, never zero. Keep exposure, viewing/retention,
completion, saves/shares, substantive comments and qualified inquiries separate.
Views cannot prove teaching or income; different formats/audiences are not a causal
cross-platform experiment. Choose one useful next change from evidence and effort,
without automatically recutting approved work or adding reminders.
