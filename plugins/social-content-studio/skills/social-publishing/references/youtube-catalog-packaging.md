# YouTube catalog packaging

Use this reference when improving existing lessons, Shorts, playlists, and their paths through a channel. Social Publishing owns platform edits and receipts; the video workflow owns source renders, timed captions, and media QA. Platform guidance below was checked against official YouTube Help on **2026-10-01**. The live account UI and saved record decide current availability. Keep this method separate from a project's current titles, dates, and course facts.

## Inspect and preserve

1. Inventory each target by stable video or playlist ID, channel, URL, visibility, scheduled time and zone, current title, thumbnail, description, captions, cards, end screen, playlist memberships and order, and any running title/thumbnail test. Match the source render and caption file by SHA-256. Do not infer identity from a similar title or retry an uncertain upload before checking its ID.
2. Record the exact requested change and the fields the creator already edited. Preserve scheduled publication, visibility, manual copy, links, playlist order, and user-selected art unless the task explicitly changes them. A scheduled upload is private until publication; a catalog pass does not automatically publish it to make an experiment possible. [Scheduling](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1270709?hl=en).
3. Prepare a small before/after record for each video: stable ID, source hashes, changed fields, unchanged fields that need readback, and any feature eligibility or uncertainty. Preserve `unknown` for facts or controls that have not been inspected.

## Package and verify

- **Title, description, thumbnail:** Lead with the real teaching task and use accurate imagery. After uploading a custom thumbnail, verify the saved image on the exact video ID and inspect how the title and thumbnail read at mobile size. Record three to eight visual anchors across the relevant list, watch page, or preview: ID, viewport, region, observed content, intended change, and changed-anchor readback. A file picker, uploaded filename, local preview, or save toast alone does not prove the live thumbnail changed. The root Details Save can be asynchronous: wait for **All changes saved** and a disabled Save before reloading, then verify the saved image. Preserve approved source artwork and claims; a faithful portrait adaptation still needs the user’s approval when approval is held. Where this account’s desktop upload supports a Shorts custom cover, prefer a full-text 9:16 adaptation and inspect the saved portrait crop. Do not make a blanket mobile-only assertion. Desktop Studio documents custom image upload for verified accounts, including Shorts; the Shorts mobile guide documents selecting a frame in the YouTube app and has conflicting language about Studio. Inspect the native UI before claiming either path is available for this account. [Custom thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en); [Shorts frame selection](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10343433?hl=en).
- **Playlist:** Select by stable playlist ID; verify the target video IDs, membership, order, and live playlist URL after saving. Do not create a lookalike playlist or move a video merely because a title resembles a course sequence. Record whether the playlist and each video are public, unlisted, scheduled/private, or unknown.
- **Title/thumbnail A/B test:** Record the native control shown, video ID, visibility, format, audience/age restrictions, advanced-feature availability, experiment type, variants, test state, and saved readback. Official Help allows up to three title and/or thumbnail variations on desktop, excludes Shorts and private videos, and says changing title or thumbnail during a test stops it. It explicitly excludes scheduled *live streams* and Premieres; it does not say that ordinary scheduled uploads cannot expose a setup control. If a scheduled video's UI offers **A/B Testing** and **Set test**, inspect and record the exact saved behavior without changing its publication state. Treat test configuration, running, and completed as separate states. In the checked Studio account, a scheduled ordinary upload accepted and saved three title/thumbnail variants, then displayed **Ineligible — Your video is not public**. Do not describe that as a running test or assume it will automatically activate at publication; verify its state when the video becomes public. A winner is based on watch time share, not a promise of more views. [A/B testing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en); [scheduled privacy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1270709?hl=en).
- **Cards, end screens, and links:** Use a relevant internal video or playlist path where the native controls permit it. End screens require a video of at least 25 seconds; standard 16:9 videos allow up to four elements. Cards allow up to five. External website links in either require YouTube Partner Program membership; record account eligibility as observed rather than assume it. If Studio presents External links Terms and Conditions, obtain explicit acceptance authorization at that action boundary; do not accept merely because adding cards was authorized. Continue unrelated catalog work while this answer is pending. A Short's Related Video is a separate feature requiring advanced-feature access and a public or unlisted channel video. Read back the exact destination ID/URL, placement or timing, and saved state. [End screens](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en); [cards](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6140493?hl=en); [Related Video](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en).
- **Education and recording metadata:** Use Education Type, Level, Academic system, Problems, and Exam/course/standard only when supported by the lesson. Problems is available for the problem-walkthrough Type and asks for a timestamp plus question. Search the platform database for a real course or standard; leave inapplicable or unverified values unset/`unknown`. The checked official Help does not establish an 800-character Education field limit or a discovery benefit from recording date/location. Enter recording date and filmed location only from source evidence; never invent either. [Video settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404?hl=en).
- **Source quality and captions:** Keep source resolution, YouTube processing status, and observed playback quality distinct. An original 720p file is HD; later HD processing may take time, while YouTube cannot restore detail that was absent from the source. A Studio SD badge alone is not proof of SD-only playback: inspect the actual watch-page quality menu. In the checked account, a source with only an SD badge in Studio offered 720p playback and both manual English and automatic English caption tracks. Mute or pause any preview before inspection; do not disturb the user with autoplay. Match the caption transcript/timing to the exact final render hash, upload or correct the timed subtitle track, and read back its language and published/draft state on the stable video ID. Burned text and Studio auto captions do not by themselves prove a complete reviewed subtitle track. [Resolution](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6375112?hl=en); [processing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/58134?hl=en); [captions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734796?hl=en).
- **Hashtags and excerpts:** Use a few directly relevant hashtags when useful; up to three description hashtags may appear by the title, while more than 60 causes YouTube to ignore them all. A share Clip is a 5–60-second loop on the source watch page. Studio's Clips & Shorts tool can instead make a separate upload, with auto-caption styling for Shorts where available. Do not label a share Clip as a separately published Short. [Hashtags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en); [share Clips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10332730?hl=en); [Studio Clips & Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15824265?hl=en).

## Native card editor readback

These are conditional lessons from the checked account’s native Studio UI on **2026-10-01**, not universal platform guarantees. Preserve title, description, approved thumbnail, visibility, and scheduled time while changing cards. Read the sidebar duration and root metadata before opening Cards: Chrome accessibility may expose only the active modal, so missing channel or visibility fields inside that modal do not establish a metadata change.

- **Commit fields before saving.** Playwright `fill` can make card fields appear changed without committing them. In the observed editor, native accessibility `setValue` followed by Tab committed custom message and teaser text. For the time input, use native keyboard select-all, enter seconds (for example, `482` for 8:02), then Return and Tab. Use the same native select-all → seconds → Return → Tab sequence for the preview playhead (for example, `1363` for 22:43); Playwright `fill` was unreliable there too, and timeline geometry seeks could miss by roughly four seconds on longer lessons. Verify the observed slider playhead is within about 1.5 seconds of the intended preview time. Read the cue input’s ARIA description for the intended minutes, seconds, and frames. Save, reopen the editor, and verify the persisted cue, copy, destination, and disabled Save separately from preview seeking.
- **Separate cue evidence from preview behavior.** A selected card’s teaser can remain visible at every playhead position in the editor; that display is not proof of its actual cue timing. Verify the persisted time independently. If a large end-screen preview obscures the lesson’s closing text, use a small relevant playlist card where that satisfies the requested path, then inspect the closing lesson frame.
- **External-link boundary and required fields.** The checked channel exposed **Add a link** after per-channel External links Terms and Conditions acceptance. Obtain the user’s explicit action-time authorization for that acceptance; preserve it in the project receipt, not as standing authorization for another channel. After entering the URL, blur with Tab, then Apply. An external Link card requires a title, call to action, and image. Failure to find a valid image on the site is an asset requirement, not proof of an eligibility block. Upload a square adaptation of approved artwork where a wide headline would be cut off; preserve its claims and source file. Save and reopen, then open the link selector to verify the complete URL, including any course query parameter, and Cancel without changing it. Verify the saved cue and copy as well.

## Receipt

For each changed item, record the stable ID/URL, account, before/after fields, source and caption hashes, thumbnail and playlist readback, native feature eligibility and state, exact visibility and scheduled time/zone, and the live page or Studio evidence. Separate prepared, saved, scheduled, test configured, test running, published, and publicly verified. If a control or save behaves differently from the documentation, preserve the observed state and date; do not silently replace it with a documented assumption. For paired LinkedIn releases, continue with [YouTube and LinkedIn scheduling](youtube-linkedin-scheduling.md).
