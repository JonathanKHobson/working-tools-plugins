## Storage Notes

- Resolve the live volume identity and filesystem using the
  [storage layout](../../skills/mac-environment-conventions/references/storage-layout.md)
  before externalizing runtime/app-support recovery backups. Historical mount
  names do not prove that the intended drive is present or suitable.
- Do not invent new storage paths. Check the receiving operator's Mac conventions first.
- For cleanup or active-project moves, use
  [cleanup and migration verification](storage-cleanup-and-migration.md).
