# Private evidence corpus and downstream handoff

Use when the user requests organization of an existing evidence backlog or a source-supported handoff to portfolio, teaching, publishing or relationship owners. A backlog inventory is not permission to collect every session, publish a source or accept a claim.

Keep originals in their owning project on the authorized storage volume. Use the existing private evidence area for stable case IDs, exact source pointers, source dates, hashes/revisions, human versus assistant contribution, support, contrary evidence and missing proof. Hashes establish byte identity, not authorship or truth. On a changed/missing source, mark the pointer stale and review; do not silently replace its lineage. Deduplicate original sources while retaining derived summaries as lineage, not independent witnesses.

Index a bounded known corpus and disclose coverage: grouped cases, artifacts, historical source leads, date range and unreviewed stores. Historical resumes and Drive indexes remain leads until source-tier review; newer reviewed sources retain priority. Career/topic tags describe relevance, not proof strength. A useful human index may use an existing Obsidian vault with native scalar properties and pointer notes. Do not mirror binaries, private transcripts or client/participant records into the vault, and do not create a new vault or registry unnecessarily.

Record acceptance, retention, public clearance and publication separately. Human claim acceptance must reference exact accepted claim IDs; an AI inventory/review does not grant it. Public clearance records the authorized approver, date, exact claim/artifact IDs and permitted audience/scope. A public source label, open-source organization or previously published redacted case does not clear its underlying employer, participant or third-party material. Keep valuable client-work evidence privately, then use approved high-level lessons or exact public-case assets within their clearance.

Publication history records channel, case/topic IDs, source revision, content/post URL when known, receipt, observed date, scheduled date and actual state. Distinguish prepared, scheduled, sent, published and verified; elapsed time never promotes scheduled to published. Attribute dated owner checks and leave current live state unknown until refreshed. Existing content/scheduler owners consume this index; capture does not become a posting trigger, new quota or duplicate automation. Space repeated topics according to the user's policy, while recognizing materially new evidence or active changes. Substantial multi-source stories can support articles, lessons, videos or occasional useful HTML handoffs.

Private contacts stay in their existing communications owner's records. Link only needed canonical record and delivery receipts, with allowed topics, exclusions and report/engagement restrictions. Do not duplicate channel credentials or infer permission from a relationship. Exclude contact linkage and raw evidence from public exports and Git paths unless explicitly privacy-reviewed. Verify the exclusion boundary in the actual project, not just a note that the file is private. A relationship handoff, a recurring report and an attended comment are different permissions; neither scheduling nor reports authorize bots or automatic engagement.

For an authorized friend or collaborator handoff, match supported work to the intended recipient's private preference/topic tags, permitted audience and current purpose. Check the communications owner's sent history and the existing source/social publication histories before selecting material; combine related updates, omit unchanged repeats and follow the project's spacing or opt-out preferences. A contact match nominates relevant material, never permission to send, publish or create a report schedule. Names, contact details and current private permissions remain in project records.

Use a brief digest with original links for a few updates. Create a substantial artifact only when it adds a useful explanation, demonstration, resource or decision the recipient can use. When hosting is authorized and suitable, prefer a verified accessible GitHub-hosted page or existing cloud share; keep sensitive material in an access-controlled share permitted for that recipient. Use a local HTML attachment as a fallback when appropriate, checking that required assets travel with it. Verify the final rendered artifact and actual intended-recipient access conditions, including sign-in, grants and working links; an owner's preview does not establish recipient access. Do not promote a private or password-only case as public work, or treat viewing access as redistribution permission.

Route delivery through the existing communications owner and authorized channel. Record the exact source revision, recipient scope, delivered link or attachment and actual shared/sent state with its native receipt in the project history; preparation and upload alone are not sharing. Distinguish verified access configuration from the recipient actually opening the artifact, leaving the latter unverified without evidence. Pass only the needed delivery delta to existing source/social history owners so later updates stay relevant and do not bombard the recipient.

Verify pointer existence/hash state, stable IDs, source/claim links, history states, privacy exclusions and recipient scope. Updates are manual with editor/date. No index edit automatically changes profile, claim cards, memory or publication. Return exact paths and coverage gaps to the authorized owner; do not fabricate completeness, human acceptance or outcomes.
