# Sources and method ownership

Career Studio's nine heads, first-party workflows, local original resume/cover-letter/portfolio methods, references, templates, roles, privacy checker and Atlas launcher remain in the canonical owner and the public successor. See `PROVENANCE.json` for exact per-file source lineage and the private baseline archive receipt for recoverable pre-transition bytes.

MIT source packs are preserved under `libraries/ai-job-search/` and `libraries/linkedin-agent/`, each with its original license and provenance. Compensation source concepts and the newly written method are distinguished in `references/compensation-provenance.md`.

The pre-transition source contained 38 byte-preserved marketplace files from AIHumanizer, CoverLetterWriter and UIUXDesigner without a recorded redistribution grant. All 38 source hashes matched the corresponding official installed provider files at the reviewed versions. Their exact paths and hashes are retained in `PROVENANCE.json`; the bodies are removed from both local and public distributable packages. The [conditional provider map](library/source-procedures/INDEX.md) names all 36 complete installed skill entrypoints. The first-party task workflows remain usable if a provider is absent, and the missing depth must be stated rather than imitated.

Job Application Compass is an existing optional private stdio runtime documented in `docs/job-compass-integration.md`; it is not a bundled or advertised public server. Career Atlas requires a separately installed Activities Templates runtime and recipient-owned approved catalog. Cross-studio output providers remain separate owners.
