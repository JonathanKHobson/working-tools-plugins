# Offer worksheet and practice example

Use in the existing private career workspace. Fill only known values; `unknown` differs from `none`. Scale to the request: one early expectations answer does not need a full two-offer spreadsheet.

## Record structures

Decision: role/stage; requested deliverable; deadline/time zone; priorities; hard constraints; actual alternatives; minimum/target/ask; draft versus authorized send state.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Employer posting, captured date | Current posting | Exact role; level pending | Annual base USD | … | Band may span locations |

| Offer field | A | B or current option | Evidence/unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| State, deadline and employer | … | … | Advertised/verbal/written/revised |
| Function, scope, level, location | … | … | Actual duties; title alone insufficient |
| Recurring guaranteed annual cash | … | … | Payment conditions |
| Variable target cash | … | … | Eligibility/quota/discretion |
| One-time sign-on and repayment | … | … | Timing and clawback |
| Equity instrument, amount and vesting | … | … | Value/liquidity separately |
| First-year cash scenario | … | … | Show formula/assumptions |
| Later-year recurring cash scenario | … | … | Do not recur the sign-on |
| Benefits, costs, hours and travel | … | … | Verified documents |
| Manager, learning and growth | … | … | Evidence versus impression |
| Top priorities and hard constraints | … | … | User's values |
| Missing terms and next question | … | … | Material to the decision |

Counteroffer record: interest; current written terms; priority request; grounded rationale; alternative; channel; exact draft; response practice; action authorization. Status record: prepared → reviewed → sent → reply received → revised written offer → accepted/declined, each with actual evidence and date. Never skip stages in the report.

## Synthetic worked comparison

These are invented teaching numbers, not the recipient's offers or market data. A is a written employee offer with $100,000 recurring annual base, a $10,000 sign-on subject to repayment if leaving within one year, and a 10% discretionary target bonus. B has $108,000 base, no sign-on and a 5% discretionary target bonus. Assume full-year service, no other cash and identical currency.

| Calculation | A | B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Recurring guaranteed cash | $100,000 | $108,000 |
| First-year cash including conditional sign-on | $110,000 | $108,000 |
| First-year target cash if bonus achieved | $120,000 | $113,400 |
| Later-year target cash if bonus achieved | $110,000 | $113,400 |

A's sign-on is separate and repayment-contingent; neither bonus is guaranteed. B has $8,000 more recurring guaranteed annual cash. The preferred offer still depends on schedule, benefits, manager, verified constraints and priorities. Any private options offered by A belong in a separate upside scenario; they do not raise its cash floor.

Suppose the candidate prioritizes recurring cash and A's learning opportunity, and current role-matched evidence supports a request for $108,000 base. A usable draft is: 'Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about [verified role opportunity]. Given [verified scope] and the comparable base ranges we discussed, could you bring the base to $108,000? That would address my main concern about the recurring package. I'd also appreciate clarification of the sign-on repayment terms. I'm happy to discuss the options.' Omit the market sentence if no such evidence exists. Do not claim a competing B unless B is a real disclosed offer.

If A says base is fixed, test whether an actual schedule benefit or other flexible term changes the decision; do not assume its first-year sign-on solves a recurring-income gap. Before acting, read back the revised written offer. A recruiter saying 'we can do that' is a verbal response until corrected documents arrive.

## Expectations and follow-up structures

- Band clarification: ask for budgeted base and role level, express fit interest.
- Required expectations answer: researched range/point, currency and base definition, relevant scope/package condition.
- Call opener: interest → one request → short rationale → pause; prepare truthful answers to likely questions.
- Written follow-up: thank them, summarize only agreed terms, list unresolved fields, ask for revised document/date.
- Extension: real decision reason plus specific requested deadline; do not invent another process.

The deliverable can be chat, document or spreadsheet according to the user's request. Use existing providers for rendered files and actual account actions; this worksheet does not install a tool.
