# Evidence-Led Package Delivery

Use this reference when assembling or refreshing a coordinated portfolio site,
resume, and deck. Reuse the current project and evidence map; do not add a new
tracking system. Finish one requested role set before adapting the next.

## One Story, Different Reading Speeds

- Define the primary stakeholder's hiring question. Make role, problem,
  contribution, work stage, and a strong real artifact visible in the first scan.
- Build case depth from decisions and evidence, not a long list of methods. Use
  this plugin's [portfolio evidence guide](../../portfolio/references/evidence-to-case.md) for bounded PAR/RAP framing.
- Put essential findings in native text/tables and show adequate real screenshots
  at readable scale. Use optional disclosures for alternatives and deeper method
  detail. A text-only process essay and a wall of illegible screenshots both fail.
- Maintain a compact job-specific packet of requirements, reviewed evidence IDs,
  claims, chosen artifacts, reuse locations, and gaps. Role variants change
  emphasis; they do not rewrite facts or promote proposals into shipped outcomes.
- Use actual first-page PDF previews for download cards when useful. Label PDF
  viewing separately from editable downloads; show accurate page count and size.

## Sources And Stage

Keep exact Figma frame/node, recording timestamp, PDF page, or slide locators in
the private evidence map. Preserve originals and document crop/redaction bounds.
Use public-safe artifact captions rather than private paths or production notes.
Distinguish original evidence, source-backed crops, retrospective synthesis, and
generated decoration. A proposed after-state must not appear to be a shipped
result. Keep the qualification close enough to prevent that inference without
repeating a defensive disclaimer in every section.

## Bounded Final Verification

Record only what was checked, against final exported or served bytes:

| Check | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Claim consistency | Titles, dates, ownership, stage, outcomes, and contact details match across each role's site, resume, and deck. |
| Visual proof | Every final page/slide inspected; representative desktop/mobile/short-height anchors checked; native findings and authentic screenshots legible at intended scale. |
| Responsive media | Inspect actual `currentSrc`, rendered dimensions, viewport and DPR. `sizes` reflects layout; candidates stop at native resolution; above-fold proof loads promptly; originals remain available. |
| Contrast and zoom | Check rendered foreground/background pairs for body text, captions, dark sections, buttons, focus and hover states. Test 200% zoom and narrow reflow; report tested scope instead of claiming a full accessibility certification. |
| Progressive enhancement | Navigation, case links, source-image links, and downloads work without JavaScript. Enhanced viewers handle keyboard/focus, Escape, true backdrop clicks, and real image-load failure. |
| Downloads | Open the final PDF and editable files; verify type, page/slide count, selectable text, link destinations, file size, and privacy of metadata/notes. |
| Publication | Verify actual destination and served HTML/assets/download bytes after deployment. Confirm updated content, not merely HTTP 200 or a staged commit. |

For image performance detail, use `ux-design-toolkit:design-studio` and its
`libraries/visual-asset-integration/references/responsive-image-qa.md` only when the installed skill and reference are actually discovered. Otherwise inspect selected-image bytes, `currentSrc`, rendered dimensions, viewport, and DPR directly and record the observed scope. Compare unique selected-image bytes at a stated
viewport/DPR and loading scope. Label predictions separately from browser-observed
selection; neither is a measured internet load time or Core Web Vitals result.

## Authorization And Closeout

An explicitly authorized preview scope and destination do not need an extra skill
approval gate. Complete public-safety and artifact checks, publish the authorized
preview, and verify it. Ask only when an actual missing authorization or sharing
decision blocks that action; continue independent work. Distinguish preview,
approved final, and deployed states in the handoff. A live preview does not prove
historical product shipping, and historical hosting preferences alone do not
authorize a new publication.

Mark temporary contact sheets, audit captures, and working reports for cleanup
after the requested acceptance. Preserve original evidence and final deliverables.
Once the requested checks pass, close the lane instead of adding features.
