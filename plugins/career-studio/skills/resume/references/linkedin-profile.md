# LinkedIn profile: evidence, positioning and fix-first audit

Use for a profile audit, headline, About, Experience, Featured or professional-positioning rewrite. Career owns the claims and positioning; Social Content's professional-social-proof owns turning approved proof into campaigns. This is a local editorial rubric, not LinkedIn's score or a prediction of hiring success. Source and adaptations: [provenance](../../../libraries/linkedin-agent/PROVENANCE.md).

## Intake and evidence

Use the supplied profile text, export or screenshots, target audience and desired next action. Read the current private career voice/evidence and [workspace contract](../../../references/private-workspace.md); prefer current user corrections to old resumes. Do not require a new voice setup when a relevant profile exists. If only a headline is supplied, improve it without blocking on the rest. Request only essential missing evidence. A screenshot supports visible regions only; text cannot establish banner, photo, layout or mobile rendering.

Choose one primary reader for this version: recruiter/hiring manager, consulting buyer, teaching partner, or peer. Retain accurate role/search vocabulary alongside a clear contribution; do not force a sales funnel onto an employment profile. Map each claim to source, contribution, status, date, metric definition and public permission. Reconcile Experience dates/titles with the resume. Missing metrics stay omitted or explicitly marked as working placeholders; qualitative improvements need evidence too.

## Twelve-part audit, 100 available points

Read [rubric.json](rubric.json). For each item record observed evidence, score, missing input, and useful fix. Use 0 for a confirmed absent/ineffective item, half credit for partially meeting its stated criteria, and full credit for supported completion; explain intermediate scores. Unknown is N/A, not zero. Sum only observed items: report `earned / assessable points (coverage / 100)`. Do not normalize partial coverage to a purported full-profile score or assume everyone starts low. Visual items require visual inspection. Apply reasonable accommodations; do not judge age, appearance, attractiveness or inferred identity.

Prioritize by reader impact, evidence risk and effort, using points lost as a secondary signal. A false claim outranks a weak banner. A user-requested section takes precedence over a whole-profile overhaul.

## Rewrite and handoff

1. **Headline:** follow the requested quantity; otherwise offer up to three materially different options where evidence supports them: role-led, problem/outcome-led, and proof-led. All must fit supplied facts. Aim under the current composer limit (220 characters is a working check, verify before live edits); count characters including spaces. No padding to fill the limit. Never invent a result to fit a formula.
2. **About opening:** state the contribution and reader relevance in a strong first paragraph. Preview truncation depends on client and viewport; two lines is a craft aid, not a guaranteed visible boundary.
3. **About body:** connect the available relevant evidence to the kind of work sought. Two or three examples can help when supplied; one supported example is sufficient. Never pad thin evidence to meet a quantity target. Use first person if it matches the author; preserve an authentic nonnumeric story when no defensible metric exists. Explain what the author actually did and what was observed. End with one low-pressure next step and current contact choice. Do not publish a private address or assume present availability.
4. **Featured:** select a small set with distinct jobs, such as current portfolio, relevant case/deck, and teaching/resource proof. For each: proposed order, exact public destination, title, why it matters, rights and freshness. Check accessible downloads and stale duplicates. Removing a Featured tile is separate from deleting its underlying post; never confuse the two.
5. **Experience and projects:** show scope, decisions/actions, evidence and supported outcomes. Credit collaborators; distinguish proposed, prototype, shipped and measured states. Retain older relevant work rather than applying an arbitrary age cutoff. Describe role-specific public attachments and verify they actually belong to that role.
6. **Skills, recommendations and contact:** align selected skills to real experience and target work. Suggest recommendation topics and genuine recipients; do not invent endorsements, quotations or relationships. Check deliberate public contact paths and their destinations. Requests to another person remain separately authorized communication.
7. **Banner/photo:** propose positioning, crop/legibility and accessibility changes from inspected images. Use the existing design owner for requested assets; assess meaning and readability rather than cosmetic conformity.

Return current assessment, prioritized fixes and copy-ready blocks. An incomplete draft must carry its unresolved claims outside the copy block and be labeled working draft. Re-score proposed text separately from the current live profile; do not award unseen recommendations, new activity or a nonexistent banner. Report `proposed`, `saved`, and `live-verified` distinctly. If profile application is authorized, the existing publishing/provider workflow owns it under current tool/platform requirements; these methods create no browser bot or new account access. After an actual change, verify changed sections, links and attachments, including 3–8 named visual anchors where visual claims are made.
