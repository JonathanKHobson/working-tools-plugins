# Career interviews

Prepare evidence-backed interview answers, portfolio walkthroughs, design-challenge practice or compensation expectations and offer comparisons for a named role and stage.

## Complete procedures to load

Read the task-matching files below in full before applying that procedure. Load only the relevant variant and its required support. These contain the operative methods, examples and templates; this entrypoint does not replace them. A plugin is a package, not a requirement to read every skill.

- For UX roles, portfolio walkthroughs or design challenges, read [UX interview practice](references/ux-interview-practice.md). For general role-specific STAR answers and roleplay, read [role-specific practice](references/role-specific-practice.md). Both are complete bundled procedures.
- For salary expectations, an offer or compensation decision, read the complete [compensation and offer method](../../references/compensation-and-offers.md), including its worksheet and provenance. Ordinary interview practice does not load this module.
- [External candidate evidence](../../references/private-workspace.md) — read when locating the applicant’s private experience and receipt records.
- [Truth and metrics rules](../resume/references/metrics-and-truth.md) — read when checking claims, dates, metrics or ownership in an answer.

## Workflow

1. Identify the role, interview stage and likely evaluation criteria using supplied evidence; locate the candidate’s external facts and authentic work.
2. For compensation or offer work, apply the linked compensation method and deliver its decision record. Otherwise apply the bundled general or UX interview procedure. Ground answers in verified contributions and distinguish a hypothetical challenge approach from past experience.
3. For interview practice, produce speakable answers, thoughtful questions and a bounded exercise. For compensation work, produce the requested expectations answer, normalized comparison or negotiation preparation. Distinguish verified experience and terms from hypothetical approaches/scenarios.

## Deliver and verify

For supplied interview feedback, extract stated strengths, risks and next steps with their supporting source phrases; leave an unstated field empty. An optional Interview Feedback Extractor connection can produce candidate fields, but verify every category against the original note: it can misclassify concerns or actions as strengths. Correct the extraction before using it for practice. Keep hiring decisions, contacting people, scheduling and ATS updates outside this extraction step. If the provider is absent, perform the same source-grounded extraction directly; do not infer candidate traits from missing feedback. This is a Career method, with Research Toolkit owning research-participant synthesis.

Deliver role/stage-specific practice, concise answers or the requested compensation decision record. Keep personal stories and candidate data in the external career workspace, not this shared skill.

## Example boundary

Good: explain a real design decision and its verified outcome. Bad: invent participants, results or leadership scope to strengthen a story.

The original affiliate footer is provenance only and must not be appended. General Career methods apply beyond UX; use the design-challenge sequence only when the role calls for it. Current authorization governs outreach and account actions.

For role-specific STAR practice, difficult questions, candidate questions or interview roleplay, read the complete [role-specific practice method](references/role-specific-practice.md). Use actual candidate evidence and the target role; preparation does not authorize outreach or create a fabricated result.

For story elicitation or reusing interview evidence in profiles/posts, use [evidence story bank](../../references/evidence-story-bank.md) in the existing private register. Preserve exact quotations, contribution, state and public-use boundaries.
