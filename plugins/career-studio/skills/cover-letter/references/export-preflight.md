# Letter export preflight

Load when the requested letter becomes an attachment or form submission. Follow the employer's exact format and filename rules. Confirm the employer, role/requisition, recipient, candidate name, contact details, date, links, and resume-matched header. Keep a clear reading order, selectable text, readable type, and simple single-column layout for parser-sensitive uploads. Check letter or A4 page size for the actual destination; do not treat a country convention as a substitute for employer instructions.

Open the final exported file, extract its text, and render its pages. Inspect 3–8 named anchors for header, body flow, page break, signoff, and any link/attachment areas; recheck changes. Verify metadata and filename do not expose private notes or the wrong employer. A successful PDF write or selected upload file does not prove attachment, delivery, or submission; the `submissions` skill verifies those later states.
