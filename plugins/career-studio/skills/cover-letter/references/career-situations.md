# Candidate situations and proof selection

Load this reference for a career transition, employment gap, first role, executive role, internal move, relocation, remote work, or requested pay/availability language. Select only the situation in the user's application; do not append explanations for every possible concern.

| Situation | Useful move | Evidence and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Career change | State the move plainly, map two or three relevant capabilities to the new role, and show a real bridging project, course, volunteer assignment, or paid work. | Distinguish direct experience from transferable or adjacent experience. Do not claim a credential or outcome from merely attending a course. |
| Employment gap, layoff, caregiving, short roles | If the issue matters to this application, use one or two truthful lines, then return to current readiness and proof. | No apology, altered dates, invented activity, or compelled private medical/family reason. The candidate chooses what to disclose. |
| Entry level | Lead with the strongest project, internship, volunteer, tutoring, coursework, or part-time work that answers the role's requirement. | Name the work's setting and actual responsibility; never dress a class assignment as an employer project. |
| Executive or senior leadership | Show evidenced decision scope, team/budget/region if confirmed, tradeoffs, and outcomes. A proposed early approach can be labeled as a perspective, not a claim of company knowledge. | Do not infer executive title, budget control, people management, or a first-month plan from senior-sounding language. |
| Internal promotion | Use current-role results, knowledge of the organization, and a feasible handoff or transition contribution. | Keep relationships and organizational priorities factual; do not imply a promised promotion. |
| Relocation or remote | State a confirmed move window, interview availability, time-zone overlap, or remote delivery proof only when relevant. | Work authorization, notice period, location, private reasons, and start date require candidate confirmation. |
| Salary or availability | Answer the exact employer question with the user's approved range, unit, currency, notice, and start date. | Omit salary from the letter when unasked. Do not convert hourly to annual or invent flexibility. Verify current market data for a proposed range. |

For teaching roles, favor actual teaching, curriculum, learner work, and evaluation evidence; for sales, customer/revenue proof; for service, resolution and relationship proof; for software, shipped code and collaboration; for design, decisions and artifacts. These are selection cues, not keywords to add without evidence. End by checking whether the candidate can defend every claim in an interview.
