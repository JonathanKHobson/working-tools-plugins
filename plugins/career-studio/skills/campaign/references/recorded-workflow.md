# Reusable application lessons

Prior operations establish these checks, not reusable personal answers or current application status:

- Match live employer, requisition, packet and every attachment field before uploading. The last file-picker directory is not authority.
- Distinguish selected, processing, attached, submitted and employer-confirmed states. A disabled button or attached filename is not a receipt.
- Reconcile assistant totals and secondary-operator records against role-level evidence. Normalize aliases without merging distinct requisitions or discarding conflicting evidence.
- A setting change does not prove an upload works. Diagnose the supported tool flow and verify its actual result; do not replay historical permission toggles.
- Keep authored evidence, observations and timestamps in the private workspace. A historical confirmation neither proves a listing is still open nor authorizes a repeat application.

Personal provenance records live outside this plugin. Resolve them through the user's private workspace only when continuity or a disputed state requires it.
