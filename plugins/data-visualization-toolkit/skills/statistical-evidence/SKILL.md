# Statistical evidence

Read the complete [Statistical and Uncertainty Visualization procedure](../../libraries/build-web-data-visualization/skills/statistical-and-uncertainty-visualization/SKILL.md) before choosing or changing the display. Follow its task-specific links to distribution choices, uncertainty encodings, analytical pitfalls, and missingness/confidence; those full references are preserved in place. The statistical/data owner establishes the actual estimates, intervals, population and method. This head dispatches to the full procedure rather than replacing it.

Pair with the [visualization router](../data-visualization/SKILL.md) for a broader chart or story decision, and with [visualization verification](../visualization-verification/SKILL.md) when the rendered result or calculation needs testing. Do not fabricate bounds or infer statistical significance from a picture.
