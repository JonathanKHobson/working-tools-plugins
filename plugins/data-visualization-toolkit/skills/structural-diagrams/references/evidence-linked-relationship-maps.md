# Evidence-linked relationship maps

Use this for a small, bounded set of entities and directional relationships whose records a reader may need to inspect. It supplements the complete node-link and UML-like diagram methods in this owner. A map is a selected view over cited records, not a claim that an archive is complete.

1. **Bound the question and corpus.** Name the source collection, inspected revision or export date, inclusion rule and any known gaps. Record stable IDs for each entity and source record. A search hit is a lead until its content and relation have been inspected. Do not infer the absence of a relationship from an unsearched or inaccessible archive.
2. **Model every edge explicitly.** Store `from`, `to`, a relation verb, status (`fact`, `inference`, or `unknown`), and source record IDs. `from → to` must match the cited record's direction. A fact is directly supported by that record; an inference needs the stated reasoning; an unknown is a question to check, not a positive relationship. Keep claims about people, communication or authority at the granularity supported by the source.
3. **Choose the reading layout.** For a modest directed graph with a dominant flow, use stable layered placement and arrowheads. For an undirected network use the appropriate undirected method. Place labels outside crossings and offer a text relationship list so edge meaning is available without decoding geometry. If it grows dense, filter to a named neighborhood or use a table/matrix; do not shrink labels into illegibility.
4. **Keep exploration local and reversible.** Search by visible entity name/ID, select a node to focus its immediate neighborhood, show the related claims and record IDs, and provide a visible reset. Native controls support keyboard selection. A focus view must not imply that hidden items ceased to exist. Keep the unfiltered source ledger reachable.
5. **Verify source and render separately.** Check each consequential edge against its cited record and status, validate IDs and endpoints, inspect default/focused/empty-search views at desktop and narrow widths, and exercise keyboard search, focus and reset. Record unresolved questions beside the affected edge. A rendered diagram alone cannot establish historical truth.

The [standalone example](../examples/relationship-map.html) uses a fictional workshop logistics packet (`R-01`–`R-05`) to demonstrate this contract. Its record excerpts are invented and explicitly local; none are evidence about a real archive. It runs offline with no imported scripts or data calls. Replace the fixture, corpus statement and source ledger together when adapting it.
