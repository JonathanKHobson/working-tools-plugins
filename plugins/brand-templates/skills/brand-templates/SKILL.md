# Brand Templates

This plugin owns the complete original read-only brand-kit picker and source-closure runtime. The public edition includes the complete original 104 fictional reusable kits, reviewed author-owned artwork and licensed portable fonts. The additive Fresh Curiosity A2 kit is excluded. Follow the [external collection contract](../../docs/collection-contract.md) and [setup](../../README.md); retain data outside the installed plugin.

Start with `search_brand_library` for assistant retrieval. Use `browse_brand_library` when the human wants to choose or inspect options. Call `prepare_brand_handoff` for the selected exact ID and brief, then read that external kit's SKILL.md and DESIGN.md and only its relevant support files. The prepared text gives the real source path and closure hash. Preserve source facts, recognition rules and font notices; sample copy is not project evidence. A selection does not install, publish or trademark-clear an identity.

Graphic Design owns brand-guide production, composition and static execution. UX Design owns interface execution and design review. Marketing owns positioning and customer evidence. Design Templates owns originating artifact templates. Writing Templates owns explicitly selected generic writing structures; Writing Quality owns personal voice and final prose review. Use their installed capabilities when needed.

For a new kit, inspect a registered neighboring manifest and retain this runtime's source closure contract. Author and render a complete candidate with human guide, editable guide source, tokens, original assets, asset/rights register and applicable font notices. Review visuals and rights, compute actual hashes, register the catalog entry, and verify exact lookup, preview and prepared handoff. A created prompt is not a saved kit. Only use authorized material and do not expand publication authorization.

See the [chooser design contract](../../DESIGN.md) and [project map](../../docs/project-map.md). The synthetic fixture only exercises software behavior and must keep that label.
