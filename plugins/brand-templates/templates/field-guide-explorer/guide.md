# Open Field Atlas · brand guide

A digital field-guide identity that uses cool paper, strong wayfinding and small gold signals to help readers move between entries.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use slate-blue reading fields with restrained gold highlights.
- Keep the display face for entry titles and clear sans-serif text for detail.
- Give each entry a stable local label and route back to the index.
- Separate observations, practical guidance and source references.
