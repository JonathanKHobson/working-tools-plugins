# Orbit Story Studio logo provenance

Prepared 2026-10-07.

## Source

Derived from the fictional template identity `creative-business-model`. Source typography, palette roles and recognition rules inform the construction. This is an authored reusable identity, not an official identity of an external organization.

Authored logo direction: Use ORBIT STORY STUDIO in Futura with an open orbital arc and one offset node. Avoid a planetary globe; the arc represents connected working questions.

## Production

A raster concept was developed with the installed logo workflow (revision 2026-10-06.2) and the built-in image generation tool. Retained concepts and exact generation notes are in `concepts/`. The selected concept is `concepts/orbit-flat-concept.png`; its transparent export is `concepts/orbit-transparent-concept.png`. The four-hole reel, center opening, split orbit and satellite in the production SVG were drawn separately as precise vector paths. The raster concept is not represented as editable vector.

Type decision: Futura → Jost. licensed geometric sans adaptation, not exact Futura

- jost, Bold: Jost-Bold.ttf; license OFL.txt.
- jost, Regular: Jost-Regular.ttf; license OFL.txt.

The SVG masters are self-contained outlined paths. Text remains scalable artwork rather than live editable text. PNG files are transparent 2× exports of the corresponding SVG, rendered with MuPDF.
