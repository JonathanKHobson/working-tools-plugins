# Orbit Story Studio · brand guide

A creative-service identity organized around connected questions of offer, capacity, economics and delivery.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use the orbital-reel cover motif to introduce a connected model, not a funnel.
- Use the dark navy orbital-reel cover with light Futura titles; retain the separate warm-paper reading field in model layouts.
- Keep each model question linked back to the central What must be true prompt.
- Separate illustrative assumptions from commercial commitments.

## Use the system

Start with [the logo family](LOGO.md), [colors](COLORS.md), [type](TYPOGRAPHY.md) and [layout](LAYOUT.md). [Components](COMPONENTS.md) and [adaptation rules](ADAPTATIONS.md) retain the originating design's functional relationships. [Writing style](STYLE.md) gives the voice and its factual limits.
