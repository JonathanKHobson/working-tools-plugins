# Common Material · brand guide

A residency proposal identity that combines a generous literary title with a clear, workable account of making and participation.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Fraunces display typography for the residency proposition and Alegreya Sans for delivery detail.
- Keep the warm peach page ground, plum reading ink and a generous first-page title interval; the open painted vessel is supporting source artwork.
- Separate the creative premise, participant experience and production requirements.
- Treat the proposal as an invitation to review, not an announced residency.
