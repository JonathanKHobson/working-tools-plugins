# The Waterline · brand guide

A documentary-development identity that uses a horizon as an organizing thought while making the status of every image explicit.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Give the concept frame a generous image area with a distinct credit field.
- Use condensed Avenir for the title and Avenir for production explanation.
- Keep blue-gray, off-white and near-black tied to the shoreline atmosphere.
- Separate the film’s question from access, footage and production assumptions.
