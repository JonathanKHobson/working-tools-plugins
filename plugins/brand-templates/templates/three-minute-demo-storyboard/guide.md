# Three Minute Story · brand guide

A demonstration identity organized around six planned scenes, visible evidence and a disciplined running order.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Avenir headings on warm paper with a restrained amber timing cue.
- Make the planned total duration prominent without implying a measured rehearsal.
- Give each scene a clear purpose, visual and spoken cue.
- Keep local prototype captures labeled as demonstration material.
