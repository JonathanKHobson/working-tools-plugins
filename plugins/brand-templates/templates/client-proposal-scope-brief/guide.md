# Clear Terms Studio · brand guide

A proposal identity organized around the decision, deliverable and acceptance conditions a client must understand.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Place the proposal identifier, version and date above the title.
- Use numbered decision-and-terms sections with clear text tables.
- Keep scope, responsibility and acceptance criteria visually separate.
- Use black typography and generous table spacing to make commitments inspectable.
