# First Line Research · brand guide

A research identity that follows a person’s initial route while keeping the evidence ledger separate from the illustrative journey.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a continuous conceptual line as a reading path across observations and questions.
- Pair Gill Sans explanation with restrained Chalkboard annotations.
- Keep the evidence register on its own structured page.
- Use rust to mark moments worth investigating, not to label participant failure.
