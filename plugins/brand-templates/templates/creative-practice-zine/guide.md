# Work in View · brand guide

An editorial practice identity for explaining creative work in chapters. Constructed blue lettering opens the conversation; a generous serif voice makes room for method, credit and reflection.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Build the WORK masthead from the source’s rectangular letter segments; blue construction is the signature.
- Pair a large Baskerville proposition with small Avenir chapter indexing.
- Keep the portrait reading-page proportion and generous outer margins.
- Use red rules and chapter accents to connect evidence to commentary, not as full-page warning color.
- Separate own contribution, team decisions and evidence limits in the credits chapter.
