# The Material Register · design specification

A catalogue identity that gives samples a consistent record so texture, construction and provenance can be compared.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use Arial for specimen names and monospaced identifiers for records.
- Keep one specimen per repeated unit with stable label positions.
- Separate observed material properties from intended uses.
