# Show the Work · design specification

A presenter’s identity that turns a demonstration into a sequence of visible actions with room to recover when something changes.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Number each cue card and give it one imperative heading.
- Put planned time near the action, clearly distinguished from measured timing.
- Separate what to show from what to say.
