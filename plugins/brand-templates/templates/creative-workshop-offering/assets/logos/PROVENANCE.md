# Hands at Work logo provenance

Prepared 2026-10-07.

## Source

Derived from the fictional template identity `creative-workshop-offering`. Source typography, palette roles and recognition rules inform the construction. This is an authored reusable identity, not an official identity of an external organization.

Authored logo direction: Use HANDS AT WORK as a serif wordmark with a short underline ending before the final letter. No hand illustration or craft-tool cluster.

## Production

The wordmark is outlined from licensed type and the supporting geometry is drawn as native SVG paths according to this identity’s source direction. No image generation was used for this typographic or geometric construction.
