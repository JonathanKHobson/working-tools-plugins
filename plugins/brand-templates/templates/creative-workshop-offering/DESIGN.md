# Hands at Work · design specification

A workshop identity that joins an expressive serif invitation to a clear sequence of making and practical attendance details.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Pair the workshop serif display face with the source sans-serif body.
- Use forest ink on cream, with rust reserved for the primary action.
- Number the workshop stages and give each a title and short description.
