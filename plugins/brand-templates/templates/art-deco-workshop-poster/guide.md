# Cedar House Workshops · brand guide

A workshop identity with formal Deco lettering and practical invitations, designed to make a small craft session feel intentional.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Copperplate display lettering between restrained horizontal rules.
- Keep cream on deep teal as the principal poster relationship; brass accents frame rather than fill text.
- Separate the date, venue, access and booking lines so decoration never hides attendance details.
- Pair the poster face with a plain programme reverse.
