# The Comparison Lab · brand guide

A product-review identity that puts alternatives on one evidence desk with equal schematics, stable labels and visible revision status.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Keep a numbered vertical rail and revision line on every page.
- Use monospaced labels for concepts and measured specifications.
- Give each concept an equal-sized schematic panel.
- Use teal and rust for distinct comparison functions, not winner and loser decoration.
