# Studio Field Atlas · brand guide

A wall-scale research identity that makes a question, a small set of findings and their evidence readable at a distance.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use large Avenir headings with plain Arial supporting text.
- Organize the poster around the research question before individual findings.
- Keep source labels close to the corresponding evidence.
- Use the source cyan geometric strip with plum, near-black and mint circle/wedge/square forms above three reading columns; keep body copy black on white.
