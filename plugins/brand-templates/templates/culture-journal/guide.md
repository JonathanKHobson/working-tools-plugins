# Margin Culture · brand guide

An independent journal identity with condensed headlines, typewritten commentary and collage that feels assembled issue by issue.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Anton headlines against Courier Prime commentary.
- Keep cream paper, near-black ink and selective lime as the recurring relationship.
- Number stories and alternate their composition without changing the editorial hierarchy.
- Use collage as a subject-bearing image, not texture behind long copy.
