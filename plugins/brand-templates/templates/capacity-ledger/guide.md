# Room to Gather · brand guide

A capacity planning identity that keeps recorded enrollment, available places and hypothetical expansion visibly distinct.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use dark slate header bands, pale result cells and amber editable entry cells.
- Keep Overview, Sessions and Definitions as separate reading functions.
- Show counts and percentages with explicit denominators.
- Retain blank, missing-input and no-capacity states rather than converting unknowns to zero.
- Preserve the source conditional state colors and accompanying labels only when their recorded condition applies; do not turn exception, venue or validation colors into universal logo accents.
