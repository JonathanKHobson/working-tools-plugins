# The Night Window · brand guide

A performance identity that pairs a forceful title with a quiet programme readers can use before the lights go down.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Impact for the performance title and Georgia for programme reading.
- Keep cast, sequence and contextual notes in distinct text sections.
- Give credits a clear hierarchy rather than shrinking them into a decorative footer.
- Retain monochrome print legibility in folded formats.
- Use burgundy programme labels with the black condensed title; retain the warm-paper supporting panels.
