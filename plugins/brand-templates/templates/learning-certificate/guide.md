# A Record of Participation · brand guide

A certificate identity that gives an occasion dignity while stating exactly what the document records.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a formal Baskerville title with plain Arial participant and activity details.
- Keep the recipient name as the visual center.
- Separate participation wording from any assessed achievement.
- Give date, issuer and qualification enough room to be read.
