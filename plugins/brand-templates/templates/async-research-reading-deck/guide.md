# Atlas Reading Room · brand guide

An evidence publication identity for readers who need to follow an argument and then inspect the record at their own pace.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Didot for the main proposition and Optima for the explanatory route.
- Keep Reader map and Evidence links in consistent footer positions.
- Use plum and teal as restrained editorial accents on warm paper.
- Separate the argument route from the record route, with page numbers available in both.
