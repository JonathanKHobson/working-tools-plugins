# Work to Keep · brand guide

A portable portfolio identity that gives a reader a small, coherent sample they can revisit after a conversation.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Baskerville project headings with Helvetica Neue explanatory copy.
- Keep each case label close to its artifact.
- Separate the sample, contribution and qualification in a compact reading order.
- Use monochrome editorial structure so the work remains central.
- Use slate-blue case labels above serif project titles; retain the two-column artifact/contribution arrangement.

Start with [the logo family](LOGO.md), [colors](COLORS.md), [type](TYPOGRAPHY.md) and [layout](LAYOUT.md). [Components](COMPONENTS.md) and [adaptation rules](ADAPTATIONS.md) retain the originating design's functional relationships. [Writing style](STYLE.md) gives the voice and its factual limits.
