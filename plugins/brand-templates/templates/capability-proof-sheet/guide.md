# Useful Proof · brand guide

A compact capability identity that links an offer to a concrete sample and a bounded account of contribution.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Lead with the capability in the title, not a long personal introduction.
- Use a clear label-name-metadata hierarchy above the proof body.
- Keep a single reading column with brief evidence blocks and an explicit next conversation.
- Use red category labels, evidence numbers and invitation cues against black reading text on white; preserve the left number gutter.

## Use the system

Start with [the logo family](LOGO.md), [colors](COLORS.md), [type](TYPOGRAPHY.md) and [layout](LAYOUT.md). [Components](COMPONENTS.md) and [adaptation rules](ADAPTATIONS.md) retain the originating design's functional relationships. [Writing style](STYLE.md) gives the voice and its factual limits.
