# A Second Life · brand guide

An exhibition identity that asks what makes ordinary objects worth keeping, with an editorial balance of object and interpretation.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Baskerville titles with Gill Sans curatorial text.
- Keep warm paper, charcoal and rust as a quiet material palette.
- Give each concept object a clear image, caption and interpretive question.
- Keep collection access and opening status separate from the creative premise.
