# The Pop-Up Plan · brand guide

A cooperative game identity that makes a shared constraint visible while leaving room for different ideas and ways to contribute.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use comic-style illustration with clear, flat reading panels.
- Keep the token budget in a distinct high-contrast block.
- Use Trebuchet for rules and large numbered stages.
- Make the team goal and option costs explicit before play.
- Keep think, speak or write participation routes available.
