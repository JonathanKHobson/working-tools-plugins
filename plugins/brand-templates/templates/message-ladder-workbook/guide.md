# One Clear Message · brand guide

A writing identity that helps a person connect a central message to reasons, evidence and an appropriate invitation.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Pair Baskerville reflection with Gill Sans working labels.
- Make the ladder levels explicit through alignment and spacing.
- Keep the core message short before adding proof.
- Provide separate space for audience language and unsupported claims to remove.
