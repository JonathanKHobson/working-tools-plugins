# Time Together · brand guide

A team-planning identity that makes effort, timing and dependencies visible without treating every hour as interchangeable capacity.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use slate headers with pale lavender working areas.
- Keep amber editable planning cells distinct from calculated fields.
- Align people, weeks and tasks in a stable matrix.
- Show the planning period and assumptions above the table.
- Preserve the source conditional state colors and accompanying labels only when their recorded condition applies; do not turn exception, venue or validation colors into universal logo accents.
- Keep lavender person blocks and amber editable inputs; over-capacity and unassigned states use pink/red conditional warnings.
