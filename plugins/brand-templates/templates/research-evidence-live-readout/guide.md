# Atlas Live Evidence · brand guide

A presentation identity for explaining a bounded study aloud while keeping its scope and untested areas visible.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use plain Arial with strong declarative slide titles.
- Keep source and study scope in a stable lower text area.
- Pair What happened and What remains untested as equally legible columns.
- Reserve teal for the proposal or evidence route, not for overstating confidence.
