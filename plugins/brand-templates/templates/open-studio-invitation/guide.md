# Open Studio Hours · brand guide

A public invitation identity that makes the act of entering a creative space feel simple and concrete.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use oversized black OPEN and red STUDIO in Impact, with alternating black/red irregular open frames beneath the invitation.
- Keep category and practical details in compact Arial text.
- Give date, location and attendance action a distinct block.
- Leave enough negative space that the invitation works from a distance.
