# Open Studio Hours · adaptation rules

- A street poster emphasizes the invitation; a digital card adds access details.
- Keep the word OPEN legible at the smallest intended size.
- Keep the full name with any new symbol until the audience can recognize it independently.

## Keep

- Use oversized black OPEN and red STUDIO in Impact, with alternating black/red irregular open frames beneath the invitation.
- Keep category and practical details in compact Arial text.
- Give date, location and attendance action a distinct block.

## Verify by format

- Presentations: compare cover, dense evidence and closing slides at presentation and thumbnail sizes. Keep notes and navigation functional.
- Documents and PDFs: compare title, body and dense table pages; inspect pagination, orphaned labels, source notes and print contrast.
- Spreadsheets: preserve formulas, number formats, units, input states and calculation meaning. Validate print areas as well as the editing view.
- Websites: inspect narrow and wide layouts, keyboard focus, error/empty states, contrast and reduced motion where relevant.

Use the original template for a faithful native adaptation. A new format may change margins, scale and sequence while retaining the identity-defining relationships. Record deliberate changes and font substitutions. No text-only specification can guarantee identical results between models or rendering systems.
