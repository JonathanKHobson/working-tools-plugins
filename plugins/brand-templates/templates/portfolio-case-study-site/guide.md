# Case Notes · brand guide

A portfolio identity that treats a case as an inspectable piece of work, with clear authorship, artifacts and reflection.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use warm paper, serif case titles and a restrained sans-serif navigation frame.
- Keep project facts and disclosure near the opening.
- Attach captions to every artifact and distinguish evidence from reflection.
- Use rust for editorial emphasis and green for focus states, not interchangeable brand decoration.
