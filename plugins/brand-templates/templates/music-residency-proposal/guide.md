# Rhythm in Residence · brand guide

A music-learning identity that makes participation visible through count, rest, repetition and a modest performance goal.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a percussion-inspired cover with clear reading fields over the artwork.
- Pair Georgia statements with Arial practical detail.
- Use the deep plum cover with cream type, pink rhythm accents and the source swirling percussion artwork; lighter reading areas retain plum ink.
- Show an eight-count practice sequence with taps and rests equally legible.
