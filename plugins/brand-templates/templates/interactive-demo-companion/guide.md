# Demo Bench · brand guide

A demonstration identity that makes a local prototype inspectable through clear instructions, expected behavior and visible boundaries.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use navy reading text with rust emphasis and blue interactive states.
- Pair Arimo explanation with Courier Prime technical cues.
- Keep the demo controls separate from the interpretation.
- Show the starting state and reset path before asking the reader to experiment.
