# Cedar Learning Days · design specification

A public programme identity that makes dates, session choices and practical attendance information easy to carry and scan.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Keep the event name, dates and short programme promise at the top.
- Pair Georgia headings with Calibri programme detail.
- Align session time, title and practical notes in a consistent reading order.
