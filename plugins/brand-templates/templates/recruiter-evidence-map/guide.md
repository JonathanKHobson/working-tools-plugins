# The Relevant Record · brand guide

A career identity that helps a reviewer connect a role requirement to an inspectable example without inventing a fit score.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a simple Arial evidence map with clear requirement and proof fields.
- Keep forwarding scope visible above the record.
- Separate direct experience from transferable experience and unknowns.
- Use concise source pointers rather than unexplained ratings.
