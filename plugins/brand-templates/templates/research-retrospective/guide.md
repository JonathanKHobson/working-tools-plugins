# The Dated Record · brand guide

An archival research identity that shows how a conclusion changed as evidence arrived, with chronology as its organizing device.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a dated rail and file identifiers throughout the dossier.
- Pair Arial explanation with monospaced evidence codes.
- Keep the archival cover image distinct from the reading field.
- Separate duplicate exports from independent evidence.
- Give the current conclusion a route back through earlier records.
