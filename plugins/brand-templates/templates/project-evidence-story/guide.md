# Handoff Notes · brand guide

A project-story identity that makes ownership, unfinished work and the next action visible across a change of people or shifts.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a plain Arial headline with Georgia supporting narrative.
- Keep warm paper and brown editorial accents quiet around the artifact.
- Give the author’s role a distinct line near the opening.
- Show the shared sheet or prototype beside the principle it demonstrates.
