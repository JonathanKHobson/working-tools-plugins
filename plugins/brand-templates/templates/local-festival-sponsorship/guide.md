# The Festival Table · brand guide

A sponsorship identity that presents a cultural event as a clear set of proposed contributions, responsibilities and public benefits.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Bodoni display statements with Optima explanatory text.
- Keep gold and warm paper secondary to dark readable text.
- Separate sponsor contribution, organizer responsibility and unconfirmed assumptions.
- Make the proposal status visible beside financial figures.
