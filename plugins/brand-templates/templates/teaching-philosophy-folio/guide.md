# The Teaching Practice · brand guide

A reflective teaching identity that connects a philosophical statement to concrete choices in materials, facilitation and feedback.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Pair Cormorant Garamond display with Alegreya reading text.
- Use a warm cream folio field, dark serif reading text and a separate blue marginal commentary track linked by numbered references.
- Give examples of teaching behavior space beside the principle they demonstrate.
- Keep aspirations separate from observed learner outcomes.
