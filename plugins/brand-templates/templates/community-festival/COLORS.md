# Common Sun · color

The values below are observed in the originating Community Festival source CSS. Their names as application roles are provisional Common Sun interpretations. Use [tokens.json](tokens.json) for exact typed sRGB values; do not treat this as a complete or approved palette.

| Hex | Observed/proposed use | Application guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `#F6EFD9` | Paper / background (role proposed) | Warm page or panel field. Check contrast with all text and controls. |
| `#163C35` | Pine / text (role proposed) | Preferred text and line color on paper, subject to measured contrast. |
| `#BC8C38` | Brass / ornament (role proposed) | Decorative rules or large accents; do not rely on it for small text without a contrast check. |
| `#A4432E` | Rust / accent (role proposed) | Reserve for a restrained highlight; pair with a clearly readable text color. |

The source evidence also records `#225348`; it is not assigned a semantic role by this compact specification. Do not silently add it as a fifth role. Source observations and interpretation are detailed in [evidence.json](evidence.json).

Test text, links, focus indicators, charts, and controls in their actual combinations and states. Do not infer WCAG compliance from the palette, the visual guide, or a screenshot. If a pairing fails, choose a verified accessible pairing and record the departure. In mono/print use, preserve hierarchy with weight, labels, pattern, or rules rather than color alone. Raster-logo pixels can differ slightly from these exact source tokens.
