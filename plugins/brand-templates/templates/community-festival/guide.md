# Common Sun · brand guide

Version 0.1.0 · Draft, source-derived reusable style · Originating design: `community-festival`.

## Organizing idea

A civic festival identity that turns a public programme into a welcoming printed broadside. Tall display lettering, an open sun/fan motif, and stepped margins give practical event information a celebratory frame.

This is a proposed reusable style interpretation of a fictional template, not a claim about a real organization's identity. The source template's native design and implementation remain authoritative for observed source facts; this brand kit describes proposed portable use. Source fingerprint: `cb5010cfd624a765cd538f866fbefa13edf9910d82c15dbeaa9cb065fd3b0f35`.

## Recognition cues

1. Set a short, stacked display headline and keep clear space around its edges.
2. Use the half-sun/fan as a framing ornament, not behind schedule or access copy.
3. Make the date prominent; keep time, session title, venue, and access information separate and scannable.
4. Use warm paper and pine as the primary relationship; reserve brass and rust for accents whose contrast has been checked.
5. In print, remove interactive controls and retain the complete chronological programme.

These are proposed design rules. Preserve the source's function and task facts; adapt the treatment to the format. The [layout guide](LAYOUT.md) gives format-specific cautions.
