# Common Sun · logo and mark use

The Common Sun production masters are newly authored, all-vector geometry informed by the generated concept. They are proposed project assets, not recovered Community Festival logos and not evidence of trademark clearance. The production SVGs use exact source palette colors; see [COLORS.md](COLORS.md) and the asset register at [assets/manifest.json](assets/manifest.json) for recorded provenance and final hashes.

For one-color reproduction, use [mono lockup](assets/logos/logo-mono.svg), [mono stacked lockup](assets/logos/logo-stacked-mono.svg), [mono symbol](assets/logos/symbol-mono.svg), or [mono wordmark](assets/logos/wordmark-mono.svg), again with the same-named `.png` when needed. The PNGs are transparent exports of the SVG masters; they are raster files, not vector artwork.

The type in the production SVG lockups is outlined, so those lockups do not rely on live font rendering. Anton and Arimo remain the guide's typography choices for newly set text; their files and license notices are listed separately in the asset manifest. The older [generated concept PNG](assets/logos/common-sun-symbol.png) is retained as process evidence only and is not a production master.

## Provisional construction rules

Use the full name with the symbol when the audience may not recognize it. Provisional clear-space rule: reserve x equal to one quarter of the cap height around horizontal and stacked lockups; for an isolated symbol, reserve one quarter of the symbol height. Provisional digital minimums are 160 px for horizontal lockups, 120 px for stacked lockups, 120 px for wordmarks, and 24 px for the isolated symbol. These are design rules, not universal standards. Before use, render the exact variant at target size and confirm its details and name remain legible. Do not stretch, rotate, add effects, place over busy imagery, or recolor a supplied file. Choose mono when color printing is unavailable and reverse only on a sufficiently dark, quiet ground. If the symbol fails at the required size, use the wordmark or set the name as ordinary text.

These spacing and size recommendations are provisional design rules, not measured source invariants. The asset owner reports visual inspection of primary/reverse/mono variants, symbol sizes 24, 32, 48, and 64 px at 1×, and wordmark legibility at 120 px. Render and verify every selected variant at its target size; the horizontal 160 px and stacked 120 px thresholds still require target-render confirmation. Print minimum sizes have not been physically verified. Check contrast, crisp edges, transparent bounds, and orientation on the real background, then reopen exports in the target application. Record any redraw, substitution, or unresolved rendering issue. Source preview artwork is reference evidence only; its visible third-party content is not granted for reuse.
