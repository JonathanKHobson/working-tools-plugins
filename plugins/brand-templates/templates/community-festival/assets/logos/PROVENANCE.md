# Common Sun logo source

The logo direction began with the installed **Logo exploration** image workflow, catalog revision `2026-10-06.2`, and the built-in imagegen tool. The unchanged raster concept is [common-sun-symbol.png](common-sun-symbol.png). It is a source concept, not the production master.

The production SVGs are newly authored geometric artwork informed by that direction. They use seven open rays, a half-disc, a square-cut horizon and outlined Anton lettering. They do not embed or trace the raster concept. Their colors match the brand tokens exactly. PNG files with matching basenames are transparent 2× renders of these SVGs. Anton is supplied with its [SIL Open Font License](../fonts/anton/OFL.txt).

An imagegen reverse-color experiment showed edge artifacts and was excluded from the usable assets. The production reverse version is derived from the same authored vector geometry, so its shape is identical to the primary version.

The name and artwork were created for a fictional reusable template identity. No trademark clearance or exclusivity is claimed. The user's supplied brand-guide screenshots informed layout hierarchy only; no logos, photographs or other artwork from those references are included.

## Original generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: isolated transparent logo symbol for the original fictional brand Common Sun. Follow the Logo exploration template: a single original mark with a simple recognizable silhouette, carefully restrained geometry and strong small-size legibility, not a montage. Brand context: a welcoming community festival identity, warm civic Art Deco lettering and printed broadsides. Create a distinctive sunrise on an open horizon: a broad solid semicircular sun, a few substantial geometric radiating segments, and a long open horizontal horizon at its base. The silhouette should feel confidently designed, not a stock weather icon: architectural rhythm, calibrated spacing, optical balance, strong negative spaces. ONE solid pine-green color, exact target #163C35. No text or letters. No enclosing circle or badge, no leaves, mountains, gradients, shadows, textures or mockup. True transparent background, crisp flat edges. Mark alone generously centered in a square canvas. Must work in a single ink and remain recognizable at 24 pixels. This is the original raster master to pair with COMMON SUN in Anton type; do not render a guide page, wordmark, labels or any extra objects.
