# Common Sun · layout and hierarchy

The source is a responsive public-festival programme. Preserve the usefulness of that structure while adapting it to the destination, rather than copying a website grid into every format. The originating source page is linked from [guide.md](guide.md); detailed source limits are in [evidence.json](evidence.json).

## Information order

1. Event or programme name.
2. Dates and any essential orientation.
3. Chronological sessions, with time, title, venue, and access details visibly distinct.
4. Supporting description, filters, calls to action, and secondary information.

Use the stepped edge/rule and fan/sun motif to frame the content, never to reduce its reading area or sit behind text. Let the schedule determine page length. Keep venue and access details adjacent to the session they describe. The [logo](LOGO.md) and [color](COLORS.md) guides cover mark placement and contrast.

## Adapt by format

- **Site:** Retain semantic headings, responsive flow, working filters/actions, keyboard focus, and the source's distinction among dates, day groups, session times, titles, and venues. Test narrow and wide widths, not only a desktop first view.
- **Print/PDF:** Show the complete schedule without interactive filters; use clear page breaks and repeat day/date context where a page continues. Check grayscale and ensure nothing is clipped at trim or printer margins.
- **Slides/social:** Put one event/date message first and avoid compressing a multi-session schedule into an unreadable card. Link or direct the reader to complete programme details.
- **Spreadsheet:** Preserve sortable, labeled fields and visible date/time formats. Ornament must not impede scanning, selection, or screen-reader navigation.

These are portable recommendations, not all observed source behavior. Before delivery, check the actual format's completeness, reading order, responsive/reflow behavior, contrast, and output; document omissions required by the format.
