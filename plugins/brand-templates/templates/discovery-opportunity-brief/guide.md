# Before the Journey · brand guide

A discovery identity that makes a practical user problem concrete before proposing a solution.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Open with the outcome people need in a strong Rockwell-accented hierarchy.
- Use fieldnote labels and a clear opportunity number.
- Keep observation, interpretation and proposed response in separate blocks.
- Use amber fieldnote bands and brown table headers to organize evidence, retaining black reading text and explicit source labels.
