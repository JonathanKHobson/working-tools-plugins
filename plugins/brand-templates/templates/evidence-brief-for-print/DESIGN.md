# The Printed Record · design specification

A print-first evidence identity that makes one central finding readable while retaining its denominator and method.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Give the principal finding a strong Arial headline with its denominator intact; use Cambria for the reading paragraphs.
- Use Arial section headings and Cambria narrative text, retaining table headers and explicit count labels.
- Keep a visible How this brief was made section.
