# One Recent Experience · layout

## Composition rules

- Use a plain Calibri document with a clear facilitator-copy label.
- Put the time boundary and conversation scope near the title.
- Separate primary questions from optional probes.
- Leave note space between prompts and keep suggested interpretations out of participant-facing wording.
