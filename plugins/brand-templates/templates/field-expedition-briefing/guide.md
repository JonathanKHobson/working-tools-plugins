# Field Notes Expedition · brand guide

A briefing identity that combines a practical field plan with an observational spirit and clear limits on the proposed activity.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use dark green, cream and rust with a disciplined fieldnote hierarchy.
- Pair Arial instructions with monospaced timing and Georgia contextual reading.
- Keep duration, materials and return conditions visible before the activity.
- Separate observation prompts from safety and access facts.
