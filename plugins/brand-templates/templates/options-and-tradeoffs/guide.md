# The Choice Table · brand guide

A comparison identity that helps a group see what each option gives up as clearly as what it offers.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a slate header row and pale comparison fields.
- Keep criteria aligned across options rather than giving each a different visual treatment.
- Reserve amber for editable assumptions or unresolved entries.
- Separate a preference from the evidence supporting it.
