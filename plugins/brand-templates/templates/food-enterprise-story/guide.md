# Little Lunch · brand guide

A small food-enterprise identity that uses an approachable editorial story to connect the offer, the making and the practical plan.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use forest green and warm cream with rust food-story accents.
- Pair Georgia narrative headings with Arial practical detail.
- Keep the product idea separate from untested demand and operational assumptions.
- Use a few generous food or making images with useful captions.
