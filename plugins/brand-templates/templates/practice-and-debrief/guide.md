# Try and Reflect · brand guide

A learning identity that keeps a short practice cycle visible: attempt, model, revision and reflection.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Give each slide one large Arial action heading and a small timing label.
- Use white instructional type on the blue opening field, then blue emphasis within the quiet white reading layouts.
- Place the starting request and worked example in clearly separate areas.
- Keep write, speak or think options visible without making one the default.
