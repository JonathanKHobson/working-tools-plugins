# Open Room · design specification

A critique identity that combines editorial collage with a deliberately bounded conversation about one design question.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use a paper-collage opening and the short Futura refrain LOOK. LOOK AGAIN.
- Pair plum and pale mint on a quiet cream ground.
- Give the critique question a full focal area before showing feedback prompts.
