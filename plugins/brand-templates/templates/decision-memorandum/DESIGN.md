# The Decision Record · design specification

A memorandum identity that places the requested decision before its reasoning and keeps the evidence available for inspection.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use a compact title, recipient and date before the recommendation.
- Keep Calibri headings and black reading text, with navy table headers, white header labels and pale alternating rows.
- Separate evidence, limits and next action into clearly named sections.
