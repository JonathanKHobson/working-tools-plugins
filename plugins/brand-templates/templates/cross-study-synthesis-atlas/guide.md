# Woven Logic · brand guide

A synthesis identity that makes the relationship between studies visible without dissolving their different scopes into a single result.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Set the main title in Palatino and use Futura for navigation, study IDs and table labels.
- Use deep purple #563B75 table headers with white labels and pale lavender #F0EBF5 body-cell shading; retain black reading text on white pages.
- Repeat the source woven strip using lavender horizontal threads and purple, rust and charcoal vertical threads; preserve visible crossings and white breathing room.
- Carry study IDs through inventory, overlap and synthesis, showing convergence and disagreement with explicit labels and source links.
