# The Last Key Studio · brand guide

A story-development identity that pairs a literary title with a production-readable account of premise, scenes and decisions.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Source Serif 4 Display for the title and chapter openings.
- Use Source Sans 3 for production detail and longer explanation.
- Keep the story-in-one-breath summary near the opening.
- Separate creative intention from practical requirements and unconfirmed production facts.
