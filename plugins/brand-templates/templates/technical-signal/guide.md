# The Technical Record · brand guide

A career identity that presents technical work through concrete responsibilities, methods and inspectable results.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a clear Arial name and focus hierarchy.
- Reserve Courier New for technical identifiers, not whole paragraphs.
- Group relevant experience with readable dates and role labels.
- Use textual evidence instead of proficiency bars or keyword clouds.
