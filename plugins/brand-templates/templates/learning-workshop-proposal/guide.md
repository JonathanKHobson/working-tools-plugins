# The Learning Plan · brand guide

A workshop proposal identity that connects an observable learning aim to activities, materials and delivery conditions.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Georgia headings over a readable Arial document body.
- Reserve green for structural emphasis rather than success claims.
- Keep the learning aim, practice and assessment visibly linked.
- Make materials and facilitator responsibilities explicit.
