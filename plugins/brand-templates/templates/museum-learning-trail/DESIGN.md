# Three Small Investigations · design specification

A museum learning identity that gives visitors agency through short routes and specific things to notice.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use Trebuchet route headings with plain Arial instructions.
- Make the route choice visible before the activities.
- Keep each investigation to a looking prompt, an action and a response.
