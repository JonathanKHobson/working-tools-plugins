# Fieldnotes Workshop · brand guide

A workshop identity that acts as a live navigation system, returning participants to evidence and choice throughout the session.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a persistent spine, footer rule and return-to-menu tab.
- Pair Avenir instructions with selective Chalkboard annotations.
- Use green, cream and gold as the stable frame, with rust for focused cues.
- Keep menu bands labeled with activity and planned duration.
- Retain evidence IDs when moving from records to proposed tests.

## Use the system

Start with [the logo family](LOGO.md), [colors](COLORS.md), [type](TYPOGRAPHY.md) and [layout](LAYOUT.md). [Components](COMPONENTS.md) and [adaptation rules](ADAPTATIONS.md) retain the originating design's functional relationships. [Writing style](STYLE.md) gives the voice and its factual limits.
