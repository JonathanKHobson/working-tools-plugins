# Field Notes Practice · brand guide

A learning identity that turns an uncertain first attempt into something learners can inspect and improve.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Retain the notebook margin and top rule as a stable writing frame.
- Pair Optima instructions with Noteworthy annotations only where a handwritten cue adds meaning.
- Place a worked example before the guided attempt, with response lines close to each prompt.
- Use muted green and earth tones without tinting every writing area.
