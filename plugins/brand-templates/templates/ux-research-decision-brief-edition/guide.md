# The Invitation Brief · brand guide

A decision-brief identity that makes a specific product question, finding and proposed next test easy for a lead to review.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Calibri with a compact decision-oriented title.
- Keep audience, date and study status directly below the title.
- Separate finding evidence from interpretation.
- Give the requested decision a distinct closing block rather than burying it in the evidence table.
