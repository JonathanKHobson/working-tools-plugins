# Routes of Work · brand guide

A portfolio identity that lets different readers choose relevant evidence before committing to a full case study.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Keep the audience rail separate from the project list.
- Use electric blue for navigation and selected routes on a white ground.
- Build project rows from a strong name, concise metadata and a clear entry point.
- Carry case subtitles and sample evidence in quiet gray fields.
