# Case & Cost · components

## Recognition mechanisms

- Place assumptions on the left and selected-case calculations on the right, with a clear gutter between them.
- Reserve blue text on pale yellow exclusively for editable inputs, including the case selector.
- Use umber bands with white headings for the two table sections; pale rose rows identify active calculated assumptions.
- Underline subtotal and result rows with progressively stronger umber rules; do not add decorative charts without a decision need.
- Show currency and planning period beside the numbers and retain an explicit invalid-selection state.
- Preserve the source conditional state colors and accompanying labels only when their recorded condition applies; do not turn exception, venue or validation colors into universal logo accents.

## Native component record

- [Cost Scenario Planner: native components](references/cost-scenario-planner/COMPONENTS.md)

These source records provide the actual named shapes, paragraph/table styles, cells or interface selectors. Keep their semantic and functional purposes when applying the brand. A spreadsheet input must still differ from a formula cell; a linked slide or web action must retain a recognizable interaction; source notes must remain close to the claims they qualify.

Logo assets and supporting motifs are different roles. Use motifs where they reinforce section rhythm; do not substitute them for the brand name or imply a missing logo variant. New components must reuse the brand's actual color, type and alignment rules, then receive rendered review.
