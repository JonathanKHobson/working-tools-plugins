# Slotline · brand guide

A product identity that organizes a workshop request, room hold and review decision into one legible narrative.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Helvetica Neue and Avenir with a restrained blue product accent.
- Lead with the customer’s job before feature detail.
- Keep a visible rail beside the work artifact to explain the relevant decision.
- Show request, hold and review as distinct states with explicit labels.
