# Practice First · brand guide

A learning-offer identity that makes the participant’s activity and observable check more important than promotional promises.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Pair Palatino propositions with Avenir practical detail.
- Use forest green and cream with selective earth accents.
- Present the practice, feedback and outcome check in a clear sequence.
- Keep unpiloted status near the proposed offer.
