# First Shift · design specification

A pocket-guide identity that helps a new volunteer move from arrival to useful action with clear cues and a route to help.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use DIN Condensed for short stage headings and Noto Sans for instructions.
- Organize the guide around the actual sequence of arrival and work.
- Keep contact or help information separate and easy to find.
