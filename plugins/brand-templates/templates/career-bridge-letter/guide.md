# A Useful Transition · brand guide

A correspondence identity for explaining why prior work matters to a specific opportunity without overstating equivalence.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Keep the name and focus line compact above the letter.
- Use Carlito paragraphs with a clear opening reason, evidence passage and courteous close.
- Leave generous white space around the salutation and signature.
- Describe one transferable practice in concrete terms rather than repeating every resume item.
- Use green subject/focus labels and quieter green-gray contact information above dark reading paragraphs.
