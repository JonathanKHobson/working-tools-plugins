# Room Study · brand guide

An architectural concept identity that makes spatial alternatives comparable before material or construction commitments.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Lead with a room outline and a clearly reserved strip; retain the same object inventory across alternatives.
- Use monospaced dimensions and object labels beside plain sans-serif explanations.
- Give concept-only status a stable visible position near the title.
- Align plan drawings to a shared scale before comparing benefits.
