# The Research Route · brand guide

A stakeholder-report identity that gives busy readers a direct answer and a navigable path into the supporting record.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Keep the masthead and section navigation stable while the report content changes.
- Use teal for the reading route and rust for focused emphasis.
- Place the answer before metrics and detailed records.
- Keep metric labels, scope and source links adjacent to the numbers.
