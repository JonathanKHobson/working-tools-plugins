# The Common Paper · design specification

A local editorial identity whose authority comes from readable reporting, visible attribution and the familiar rhythm of a newspaper.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use the full serif masthead above a ruled issue line.
- Separate stories with columns, subheads and captions rather than rounded cards.
- Keep black-and-white reproduction as a first-class edition.
