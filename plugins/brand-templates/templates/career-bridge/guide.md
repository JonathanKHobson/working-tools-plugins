# Across Practice · brand guide

A career identity that translates experience into a new context through a clear record of transferable work.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a prominent name and short professional focus before experience.
- Keep Carlito for an open, readable resume rhythm.
- Group transferable evidence by relevance while preserving actual dates and role boundaries.
- Use restrained section rules and ordinary bullets rather than skill-rating graphics.
- Use dark green section headings and target-role labels, quieter green-gray contact metadata and a black name; keep the white page.
