# With Permission · brand guide

A correspondence identity that makes the intended recipient, purpose and limits of forwarding clear.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a modest title and a clearly visible forwarding-scope statement.
- Keep the permission request separate from the text that could be forwarded.
- Use ordinary Arial paragraphs with a calm, spacious signature area.
- Do not turn a planning example into evidence that permission was received.
