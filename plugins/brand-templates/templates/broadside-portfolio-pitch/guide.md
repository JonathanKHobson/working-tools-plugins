# Let Stories Travel · brand guide

A spoken portfolio identity with the energy of a printed broadside and the discipline of a three-part live argument.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use oversized condensed Impact statements with small Avenir authorship lines.
- Pair acid yellow and pink interventions with an otherwise dark-ink, pale-paper composition.
- Organize the proposition as words, paths and moments separated by act rules.
- Keep a clear project title and role credit below the opening statement.

Start with [the logo family](LOGO.md), [colors](COLORS.md), [type](TYPOGRAPHY.md) and [layout](LAYOUT.md). [Components](COMPONENTS.md) and [adaptation rules](ADAPTATIONS.md) retain the originating design's functional relationships. [Writing style](STYLE.md) gives the voice and its factual limits.
