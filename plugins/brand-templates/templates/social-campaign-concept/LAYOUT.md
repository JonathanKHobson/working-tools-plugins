# Rivet Swap Collective · layout

## Composition rules

- Use Impact statements over original punk-paper composition.
- Keep red and yellow as loud interventions against black ink and warm paper.
- Make the action and event details readable without relying on the decorative background.
- Keep the swap offer, participation conditions and unconfirmed details separate.

## Exact originating-format geometry

The following source extracts retain measured units and actual component positions. EMUs, points, cell units and CSS pixels are not interchangeable. They describe the originating template; adapt by relationships when changing format, and retain exact values when reproducing the original.
