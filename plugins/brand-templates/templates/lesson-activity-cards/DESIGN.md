# Pattern Practice · design specification

A card-based learning identity that invites short experiments with a rule, a response and a simple way to compare attempts.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use Chalkboard activity titles and large card numbers, with Trebuchet instructions and teal practice labels.
- Give each card one activity with materials and an action prompt.
- Use symbols only when accompanied by a readable label.
