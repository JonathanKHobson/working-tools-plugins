# Next Pilot · brand guide

A decision identity that makes a modest test proposal legible alongside what is known and what still needs evidence.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Lead with a direct recommendation heading on a clean white slide.
- Use paired Known and Still unknown columns.
- Keep the pilot size and duration concrete in the opening.
- Use slate text and restrained warm emphasis without adding a success dashboard.
