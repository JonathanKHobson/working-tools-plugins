# Equal Frame · design specification

A comparison identity that gives alternative concepts the same visual conditions before asking a team to choose a next test.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use Futura titles and plain Arial supporting text on warm paper.
- Give alternatives equal-sized fields and the same question order.
- Reserve red for a focused finding or decision marker.
