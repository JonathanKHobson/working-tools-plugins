# Studio Evidence Desk · design specification

An analytical identity that makes comparisons inspectable on a dark reading surface, with clear separation between evidence and gaps.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use the dark ground and slightly lighter panels as an instrument surface.
- Reserve mint for supported comparison emphasis and amber for gaps.
- Align chart scales and comparison rows to the same numerical frame.
