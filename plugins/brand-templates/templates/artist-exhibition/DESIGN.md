# Objects in Sequence · design specification

A quiet exhibition identity that gives artwork, captions and chronology room to be read on their own terms.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use a restrained sans-serif masthead and broad white margins around work.
- Keep image captions immediately attached to the corresponding object.
- Make chronology a vertical sequence of date and event pairs.
