# A Quieter Practice · brand guide

A reflective project identity that starts from one small material observation and follows it into a change worth trying.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Baskerville for reflective titles and reading paragraphs; reserve Noto Sans for record headings, metadata and links.
- Keep the observation concrete before introducing an explanation.
- Give the attempted change and its result separate passages.
- Leave room for an image or sketch with a factual caption.

## Use the system

Start with [the logo family](LOGO.md), [colors](COLORS.md), [type](TYPOGRAPHY.md) and [layout](LAYOUT.md). [Components](COMPONENTS.md) and [adaptation rules](ADAPTATIONS.md) retain the originating design's functional relationships. [Writing style](STYLE.md) gives the voice and its factual limits.
