# The Session Field Guide · brand guide

A facilitator identity that makes a live session easier to run through concise cues, visible timing and recovery options.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Lead with Run the session and a clear sequence.
- Use plain Arial headings with compact working notes.
- Keep spoken prompts distinct from facilitator instructions.
- Give participation choices and fallback actions their own labels.
