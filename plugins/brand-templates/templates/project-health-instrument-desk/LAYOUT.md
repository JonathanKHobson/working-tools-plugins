# Project Instrument Desk · layout

## Composition rules

- Use a dark ink framework with pale blue reading areas and warm exception fields.
- Pair Menlo instrument labels with Helvetica Neue explanations.
- Give key counts and decision fields enough space to read at a glance.
- Keep evidence, interpretation and next action distinct within the review.
- Do not merge with Issue Desk solely because both use dark instrument styling.
- Preserve the source conditional state colors and accompanying labels only when their recorded condition applies; do not turn exception, venue or validation colors into universal logo accents.
- Use pink/red validation cells for missing, invalid or over-planned actuals; retain the dark header and pale-blue summary field.

## Exact originating-format geometry

The following source extracts retain measured units and actual component positions. EMUs, points, cell units and CSS pixels are not interchangeable. They describe the originating template; adapt by relationships when changing format, and retain exact values when reproducing the original.

- [Project Health Instrument Desk: native layouts](references/project-health-instrument-desk/LAYOUTS.md)

For a new composition, choose the content order first, establish the dominant reading anchor, then place the recurring elements listed in [COMPONENTS.md](COMPONENTS.md). Keep margins and alignment consistent within the artifact. Preserve useful empty space and let long content flow rather than compressing every role. The guide's applications demonstrate authored extensions; they are not an assertion that all formats share identical geometry.
