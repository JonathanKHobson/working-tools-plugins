# Possible Commons · writing style

Traits: curious, inclusive, concrete.

- Make the invitation warm and the practical details exact.
- Use the identity’s sample as a rhythm and vocabulary reference, not as a factual project claim.

## Rhythm and vocabulary

Short opening, concrete follow-through; vary sentence length for explanation.

Original example: “Imagine one possible day. Name what you would keep, and what would need to change.”

Second example: “Come for the part that interests you; here is what to expect.”

Original fictional brand copy authored for this guide, not a quotation or factual claim.

## Boundaries

- Avoid speaking for an entire community or implying confirmed attendance.
- Avoid invented testimonials, performance claims and quotation attribution.

The style changes phrasing, emphasis and rhythm; it does not authorize new facts, invented quotations, altered measurements or stronger claims. For errors, constraints or sensitive subjects, keep the brand's clarity while lowering promotional emphasis.
