# Possible Commons · brand guide

A participatory futures identity that uses playful geometry to make room for serious differences in community priorities.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use rounded display lettering with a Memphis-style geometric plate.
- Pair plum and pink fields with selective lime accents.
- Give the central future question a large quiet area distinct from logistics.
- Keep quiet writing, speaking and passing visible as equal participation routes.
