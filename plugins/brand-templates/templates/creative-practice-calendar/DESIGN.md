# Make Room to Try · design specification

A practice identity that treats a calendar as an invitation to small experiments rather than a measure of constant productivity.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use a strong month heading and a rounded display invitation.
- Keep each day’s prompt brief enough to leave writing space.
- Separate planned practice from completed practice through labels, not celebratory scores.
