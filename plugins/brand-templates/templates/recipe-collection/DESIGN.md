# The Shared Table · design specification

A recipe identity that pairs welcoming food writing with a repeatable structure for ingredients, preparation and serving.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use Rockwell recipe headings with Noto Sans instructions.
- Keep ingredients separate from the ordered method.
- Make yield and practical preparation notes easy to find.
