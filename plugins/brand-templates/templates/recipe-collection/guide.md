# The Shared Table · brand guide

A recipe identity that pairs welcoming food writing with a repeatable structure for ingredients, preparation and serving.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Rockwell recipe headings with Noto Sans instructions.
- Keep ingredients separate from the ordered method.
- Make yield and practical preparation notes easy to find.
- Use generous white space so the page can be consulted while cooking.
- Use rust collection labels and green recipe links, with black recipe headings and roomy ingredient/method areas.
