# Open Press · brand guide

A public-graphics identity with the energy of assembled paper, forceful type and a clear invitation to make something.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Impact OPEN PRESS lettering with a vertical spine and a contrasting title strip.
- Keep the paper-and-red palette tactile without reducing text contrast.
- Use American Typewriter only for a secondary workshop-note voice.
- On the reverse, organize the activity into numbered paper-like steps.
