# The Common Thread · brand guide

A community review told as a newspaper: shared activity receives editorial weight without turning counts into impact claims.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Set the Georgia masthead above paired horizontal rules, with issue information aligned to its right.
- Use narrow narrative columns beside a workbench illustration rather than a grid of corporate statistic cards.
- Make large section numbers a reading route; burgundy identifies bylines and editorial emphasis.
- Keep participation counts separate from repair or learning outcomes.
