# The Programme Finder · design specification

A public-service identity that makes a schedule searchable without losing the time, place and access detail people need.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use dark teal headings on a pale, quiet ground.
- Keep filters separate from result cards and show the result count.
- Give time, title and venue a consistent order within each session.
