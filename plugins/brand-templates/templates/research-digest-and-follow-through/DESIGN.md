# The Research Digest · design specification

An editorial research identity that makes an update readable and its follow-through accountable.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Use Impact for the digest masthead and Gill Sans for reading text.
- Keep issue number, date and first-read context above the story.
- Use purple bands with white section labels and purple source links to distinguish New Evidence from Decision & Follow-through; keep Impact headlines black.
