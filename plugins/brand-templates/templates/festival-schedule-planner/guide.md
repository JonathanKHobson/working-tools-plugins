# Programme Desk · brand guide

A scheduling identity that makes event time, location and conflicts visible before a programme is shared.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use navy table headings and pale working rows.
- Keep amber cells for editable schedule inputs.
- Align time and venue fields consistently across the planner.
- Retain visible validity and conflict states, with text labels as well as color.
- Preserve the source conditional state colors and accompanying labels only when their recorded condition applies; do not turn exception, venue or validation colors into universal logo accents.
- Keep the Hall blue, Studio green and Courtyard tan booking cells distinct; mark conflicts in pink with red text, and outside-hours entries in yellow.
