# Trial Ledger · brand guide

A working evaluation identity with the feel of a field console: dated records, visible states and evidence beside interpretation.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use a dark green ground with pale green reading text and lime highlights.
- Keep a stable side rail for filters and notebook context.
- Use monospaced text for record IDs and evaluation states.
- Give evidence and uncertainty their own fields within each log entry.
