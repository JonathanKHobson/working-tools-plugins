# Value in View · brand guide

A consultative proposal identity that helps a reader compare service routes through concrete deliverables and retained responsibilities.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use restrained Helvetica Neue with a large numerical scope marker.
- Set the required result beside the client’s retained responsibility.
- Use red sparingly to identify the choice under discussion.
- Keep pricing assumptions and non-commitment language near the comparison.
