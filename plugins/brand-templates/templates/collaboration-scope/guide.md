# Shared Scope · brand guide

An agreement identity that makes collaborative work legible through ownership, versioning and observable acceptance checks.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Place Working agreement and version beneath the title.
- Use clear Calibri headings and compact acceptance tables.
- Separate what each person provides from what the joint result must contain.
- Keep changes recorded as dated decisions rather than invisible wording revisions.
