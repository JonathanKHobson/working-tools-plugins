# First Arrival · design specification

An editorial portfolio identity that walks a reader through the first moments of an experience and the decisions behind its design.

## Identity-defining relationships

- Pair Baskerville or Georgia reading titles with Avenir labels.
- Use a warm ivory field, muted blue accents and a stable footer rule.
- Keep chapter numbers and margin evidence notes visible.
