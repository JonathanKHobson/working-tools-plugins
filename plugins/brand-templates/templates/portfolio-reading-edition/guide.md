# First Arrival · brand guide

An editorial portfolio identity that walks a reader through the first moments of an experience and the decisions behind its design.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Pair Baskerville or Georgia reading titles with Avenir labels.
- Use a warm ivory field, muted blue accents and a stable footer rule.
- Keep chapter numbers and margin evidence notes visible.
- Separate illustrative imagery from the actual evidence of a design decision.

## Use the system

Start with [the logo family](LOGO.md), [colors](COLORS.md), [type](TYPOGRAPHY.md) and [layout](LAYOUT.md). [Components](COMPONENTS.md) and [adaptation rules](ADAPTATIONS.md) retain the originating design's functional relationships. [Writing style](STYLE.md) gives the voice and its factual limits.
