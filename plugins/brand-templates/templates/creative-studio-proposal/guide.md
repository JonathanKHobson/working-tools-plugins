# Bright Assembly · brand guide

A studio identity that pairs electric-blue confidence with a visible account of how creative work will be organized and delivered.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Archivo headlines with large open spacing around the proposition.
- Let blue, yellow and mint form distinct fields rather than blended gradients.
- Keep the work grid separate from engagement and service panels.
- Make the enquiry area a clear next step after the scope is understood.
