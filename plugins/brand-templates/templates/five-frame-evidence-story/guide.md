# Five Frames · brand guide

A portfolio identity that makes a compact argument through five deliberate frames, each carrying one job.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Baskerville for the central proposition and Avenir for evidence and navigation.
- Keep the five-frame sequence explicit in page labels.
- Use dark teal, warm paper and a restrained gold marker.
- Give each frame one focal statement, one supporting element and a visible qualification.
