# Try a Mark · brand guide

A small experimental identity that encourages material play through forceful titles, plain instructions and a record of attempts.

[View the two-page visual guide](guide.pdf). Version 0.2.1.

## Recognize the identity

- Use Impact for the short invitation and Courier New for working notes.
- Keep each experiment compact enough to fit a zine panel.
- Leave space for a printed mark and a short observation.
- Show variation across attempts without ranking them as good or bad.
- Use rust PRINT LAB labels and black condensed invitations above textured collage examples, with monospaced working notes.
