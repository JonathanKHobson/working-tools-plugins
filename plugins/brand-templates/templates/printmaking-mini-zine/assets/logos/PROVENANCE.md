Prepared 2026-10-07.

## Source

Derived from the fictional template identity `printmaking-mini-zine`. Source typography, palette roles and recognition rules inform the construction. This is an authored reusable identity, not an official identity of an external organization.

Authored logo direction: Use TRY A MARK in Impact with one irregular solid rectangle as a new supporting mark. Keep the rectangle simple enough for one-color printing.

## Production

The wordmark is outlined from licensed type and the supporting geometry is drawn as native SVG paths according to this identity’s source direction. No image generation was used for this typographic or geometric construction.

Type decision: Impact → Anton. Portable variant selected. Impact is a separately licensed Microsoft/system font; not bundled. Exact OFL family already shipped by the source template; the family is intentionally supplied as a single display weight.

- anton, Regular: Anton-Regular.ttf; license OFL.txt.

The SVG masters are self-contained outlined paths. Text remains scalable artwork rather than live editable text. PNG files are transparent 2× exports of the corresponding SVG, rendered with MuPDF.
