# Threat Model

- Manifestation: What can be directly observed?
- Appetite or pressure: What does it seek, repeat, transform, expose, or enforce?
- Trigger: What invites, activates, or strengthens it?
- Rule: What consistent relationship governs its effects?
- Limit: What can it not do, reach, know, or survive?
- Feedback: How can characters or players infer its state?
- Learning cost: What must be risked to understand it?
- Resistance cost: What must be spent, sacrificed, changed, or accepted?
- Adaptation: How does it respond to repeated tactics?
- Human meaning: Which fear, value, history, or conflict does it pressure?
- Residue: What remains after apparent victory?
